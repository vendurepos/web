VendurePOS is an open-source point of sale for [Vendure](https://vendure.io). The till runs in the browser and signs in to your own Vendure store with a staff account or a device key; [Connect a till](/docs/connect-a-till) lists the role each one needs. A server plugin, [@vendurepos/plugin](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@vendurepos/plugin), records each sale as a Vendure order. It is built on [TallyUI](https://github.com/TallyUI/tallyui). To look around first, open the [live demo](https://demo.vendurepos.com/demo).

## How a sale reaches Vendure

The till syncs your catalogue and store settings to a local database. A completed sale is queued on the till and sent to the plugin's `/tally/v1/commands` route. The plugin creates the order at the till's price with the `tally-pos` payment settled and the `tally-in-store` fulfilment delivered, and stock comes down through Vendure's own allocation. If the till is offline, sales wait and are sent when it reconnects; a resend never makes a second order.

## Requirements

{/* Node range: plugin-v0.3.0 packages/vendure-plugin/package.json:27-28 (engines). */}

{/* plugin-v0.3.0 packages/vendure-plugin/package.json:41-42 (peerDependencies @vendure/core ^3.6.0) */}
- Vendure 3.6 or a later 3.x release on Postgres (tested on 3.7.3). MySQL, MariaDB and SQLite are not supported.
- Node `^20.19 || >=22.12` on the server that runs Vendure, as [Plugin setup](/docs/plugin-setup#check-your-store) says.
- The [@vendurepos/plugin](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@vendurepos/plugin) package on npm, added to your Vendure config.
- A web till for now. The app is pre-release: expect changes before 1.0.
