Some steps depend on the store's VendurePOS plugin. With no plugin, or one too old to sell,
the till shows "This store's VendurePOS plugin is missing or out of date.
Install or update it, then this till continues."

## Open the register

After the catalogue and settings load, enter the opening float you counted and open the register.
You can fill the cart first. Until the register is open, the open-register card takes the place of **Checkout**.

## Find and add products

Tap a product, or type in the "Search or scan barcode / SKU" box.
You can also scan with a barcode scanner or the camera.
A code with no product shows "No products match" with the code.
On a narrow screen, switch between **Products** and the cart with the tabs.

## Discount or change a price

Tap a line's quantity to open its keypad. It holds **Discount**, **Price** and **Remove**.
Choose **Order discount** under the lines to discount the whole sale.
If this till allows price changes, tap a line's price for a keypad with quick discounts.

## Add a charge

Choose **Add charge**, then **Custom item**, **Fee** or **Shipping**.
Enter a **Name** and an **Amount**, pick a **Tax class** if you need one, and choose **Apply**.
Choose **Remove** beside a fee or a shipping charge to take it off.
A sale takes one shipping charge: "This store takes one shipping charge per order."
**Checkout** needs at least one product; charges alone can't be sold.

Charges need plugin 0.3.0. On an older plugin the cart shows no **Add charge**.

## Open sales, park and the customer

Each open sale is a tab under the cart. Choose **New** to start another sale, and tap a tab to go back to one.

Choose **Park** to set a sale aside. The button then reads **Parked (n)**.
Open it to resume or discard a parked sale. Parked sales are still there after a reload.

A sale starts with **Guest** as the customer. Tap **Guest** to search the store's customers and pick one.
Tap the cross beside the name to remove them. The customer prints on the receipt.

## Order details

The ⋮ beside **Checkout** opens **Order details**.
**Void** drops the sale from the till. Nothing has been sent to the store.
A sale that is partly paid can't be voided.

On plugin 0.3.0, **Order details** has no customer note.
On the live demo, type a **Customer note** to send with the order. The store keeps it as a public note in the order's history, so the customer can see it too.

## Take payment

Choose **Checkout**, which shows the total.
On a wide screen the payment pane takes the place of the products, and the cart shows **Paid** and **Remaining**.
On a narrow screen the payment pane fills the cart tab.

Pick cash or card. Type the amount on the keypad, or tap a quick amount, then take the payment.
The keypad has no decimal key: 5, 0, 0 is 5.00.
For card, take the payment on your own card terminal, then record it on the till.
When the total is paid, complete the sale. For cash, the till shows the change.
A scan while you take payment shows "Finish this sale before scanning the next item."

To split between cash and card, take part in cash, then pick card.
The pane types in what is left. The order keeps both payments.

To split evenly, choose **Split**, then **2 ways** up to **6 ways**.
The pane shows which part you are on, such as "1 of 2", and its amount. **Don't split** clears the split.

## Receipt and next sale

The receipt lists each payment and the change.
**Print receipt** opens the browser's print dialog and prints the receipt alone.
It prints on any printer that device's browser can use; the till does not open the cash drawer, so open it by hand ([Limitations](/docs/limitations)).
**New sale** starts the next one.
If your till is set not to show the receipt after each sale, it shows "Sale complete" and an empty cart.

## Refund a sale

{/* Pay out for a Dashboard refund: plugin-v0.3.0 (2d3699e, npm latest) packages/vendure-plugin/src/service/register.service.ts:267-279 (the register counts each order's tallyPayments, written once when the till creates the order: order-create.service.ts:910, config/custom-fields.ts:35 readonly), :251 ("refunds stay 0"); src/vendored/register-figures.ts:22-27 (expected cash = counted float + those payments + paid_in - paid_out, non-voided); src/vendored/register-payload-shape.ts:60 (paid_in, paid_out, no_sale). A Dashboard refund changes none of these. */}

1. On plugin 0.3.0 the till cannot refund. Ask whoever manages your Vendure store to refund the order in the Vendure
   Dashboard. The refund then shows as Pending until they settle it;
   [Plugin setup](/docs/plugin-setup#settle-a-dashboard-refund) has the steps for them.
2. If you hand back cash, record it on the till with **Pay out** (tap your name in the bar), the amount,
   and the order code as the reason. The register's expected cash counts the till's own payments,
   pay ins and pay outs, not Dashboard refunds, so without it the drawer count comes up short.

### Refund at the till (live demo)

Open **Orders**, choose **This till** and open the sale. Only sales made on a till can be refunded.
With the register closed, the order shows "Open the register to refund this order."

Choose **Refund items**. Each line shows how many are left to refund. Enter how many to refund.
**Put the items back in stock** is on unless you switch it off.
Enter a **Reason**, check the amount, and choose **Refund**.
The till shows "Refunded … to the original payment." A cash refund lowers the cash expected in the drawer.

If the store doesn't answer, don't hand back cash. Choose **Send again**; a refund is never made twice.

## Check the register during the day

**Register ›** in the bar opens the **Register** panel.
It shows the cash in the drawer, this session's sales and other tenders, and prints an X-report.

**Reports** opens on the drawer card: opening float plus cash sales is what is expected in the drawer.
**Print X-report** prints the figures so far. The register stays open.

Tap your name in the bar for your cashier sheet. It shows "Your sales today" and "In the drawer".

Choose **Pay in** or **Pay out** to record cash in or out, with an amount and a reason.
**Hand over** signs you out and leaves the drawer open for [the next account](/docs/connect-a-till#give-each-cashier-their-own-account).

## If the internet drops

{/* Sources: content/docs/troubleshooting.mdx:9-11 (sells offline; sent once, never twice), :15 (header line), :73-74 (customer search and adding a customer need a connection); content/docs/limitations.mdx:44-45 (unsent sales are kept only in this browser and lost if site data is cleared or the device is lost). */}

Once the catalogue has synced, the till keeps selling offline, with the network off,
except customer search and adding a customer, which need a connection to the store.
Each sale waits on the till until the store is reachable, then lands in Vendure once, never twice.
The header shows `N orders waiting to send`, with ` · sending…` while sending.
Until they send, those sales are kept only in this browser. If this site's data is cleared, or the device is lost,
while the header still shows **N orders waiting to send**, they are lost ([Limitations](/docs/limitations)).
[Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting#check-whether-a-sale-has-reached-the-store) covers the other sync notices.

## Count the drawer and close the day

Choose **Close register** in your cashier sheet, or close from the **Register** panel.
Finish the sale in progress first. Otherwise the till says "Finish or cancel the sale in progress first."
Count the cash, enter it, and close.

If the difference is over this till's limit, **Manager approval** asks for the manager's name.
The name is recorded as typed, not verified. **Cancel** leaves the count unchanged.

The closure shows the expected and counted cash. Print the Z report there, then finish.
While orders are still sending, the till shows "Closing — waiting for" and the number of orders.
**Closures** in your cashier sheet lists past closures and reprints a Z report as a copy.

The Z report lists:

- The closure number, the store, and the opened and closed times.
- The number of sales and the sales total.
- The opening float and the sales per tender, cash and card.
- Tax per rate: net, tax and gross.
- Paid in, paid out and other drawer entries, with their reasons.
- Expected, counted and the variance per tender.
- The approver's name, when there is one.
- How many orders were still syncing at close.
