## Open Notifications

**Notifications** sits at the foot of the till's nav, with **Store health**, **Settings** and **Support**.
While there are notices, it carries a badge with their number.
Choose it to open the **Notifications** sheet on the right.
Choose **Close** to close it.

## Read the sheet

Each notice shows a title and a body.
With no notices, the sheet shows "No notifications" and
"Plugin updates and notices about VendurePOS arrive here."

Until the till has read the store's capabilities, the sheet shows no notices.

## Update the VendurePOS plugin

Today the till has one notice: **Update the VendurePOS plugin**.
It shows while the store's VendurePOS plugin is missing or older than this app needs.
Its body names what is held back, from these:

- sales
- fee and shipping charges
- the register

The live demo's store offers everything the till needs, so the demo never shows this notice.

Update the plugin on the store as in [Plugin setup](/docs/plugin-setup).
[Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting#act-on-a-header-notice) covers the till's other plugin messages.

## Short messages at the till

Two short messages show on the till itself, not in the sheet:

- "… added to cart", with the product's name, at the top of the till each time a product goes into the sale.
  It goes after a few seconds and needs no tap.
- "Sale complete", after a sale, when the till is set not to show the receipt after each sale.

## Turn notifications on or off

- The **Notifications** sheet and the "added to cart" message have no setting. They are always on.
- "Sale complete" shows only while **Settings › Printing › Show receipt after each sale** is off. It is on by default,
  and each device keeps its own choice.
- The till sends no phone, browser or email notifications and asks for no notification permission.

## Find problems with sending

Problems sending orders or register changes are not in Notifications.
They show as a line in the till itself, with **Send again** when the store refused the orders.
See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting#act-on-a-header-notice) for each one.
