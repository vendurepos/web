## Check whether a sale has reached the store

The till keeps the catalogue in a local database in your browser and sells with the network off.
Each sale waits on the till until the store is reachable, then lands in Vendure once,
never twice, at the POS price, paid and fulfilled.

Read the sync status in the header:

- `N orders waiting to send`, with ` · sending…` while sending.
- `Closing — waiting for N orders` while a close waits for them.
- `N orders need attention` opens the orders list.

Open **Orders** to see the till's recent sales with a status: **Waiting to sync**,
**Synced** or **Not accepted**. Sales the store refused appear under **Needs attention**
with a **Retry**. Choose **Back to sale** to return without losing your cart.

## Act on a header notice

- "The store refused the orders (reason). They are kept; send them again once the store is fixed."
  Fix the store, then choose **Send again**.
- "The store's VendurePOS plugin isn't answering. Orders are kept and retried."
  Check that `/tally/v1/info` and `/tally/v1/commands` reach the plugin through the store's proxy.
- "N orders keep failing at the store. They are kept and retried."
- "The store refused this sign-in. Sign in again below; orders are kept."
  Sign in again in the bar that appears.
- "This store's VendurePOS plugin is missing or out of date. Install or update it, then this till continues."
  Run plugin 0.1.0 or later, apply its schema changes and restart Vendure.
- "Can't reach the store's settings yet. Retrying…"
  The till takes no sale until it has read the store's settings.

## Refresh prices and stock

An open till picks up a price change made in Vendure within about a minute and a
stock change within about five minutes. Each product shows its stock "as of" the last check.

To pull the whole catalogue fresh, sign out and sign in again. Signing out removes
the local catalogue but never the orders. Unsent orders and the register wait on
that device for the next sign-in to the same store and channel.
Sign out waits while a sale is saving ("Sign out waits until the sale is saved.")
or the register is closing.

## Sell while offline

Once the catalogue has synced, everything at the till works offline except
customer search and adding a customer, which need a connection to the store.

## Resolve a sale the store kept part-way

A sale marked `needs_admin` answers 409 until an admin resolves it through the
Admin API with SuperAdmin access. List these sales with `tallyNeedsAdminCommands`.
Resolve one with `tallyResolveNeedsAdmin`, using `applied` or `rejected` as the
resolution and a note:

```graphql
mutation {
  tallyResolveNeedsAdmin(commandId: "command-id", resolution: applied, note: "checked the till") {
    id
    status
  }
}
```

Send a rejection with the sale's channel token (`vendure-token`).
An order flagged `tallyRejected` counts as never placed: leave it out of register
and sales figures, and use the till's **Retry** for the sale that counts.

## Check browser storage

If the local database cannot start, the till shows the error with a **Reload** button.
Private windows that give a site no storage, such as Safari's, cannot run the till.

## Fix CORS, 401 or plain-http store problems

Follow [Troubleshoot in the quick start](/docs/quick-start#9-troubleshoot).
A till served over https talks only to https stores; `http://localhost` and
`http://127.0.0.1` are allowed for development.
