## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): Above the fold is clear (H1 'Point of sale for Vendure', a one-line explanation of Vendure, Live demo first), but below it the home page hands a newcomer server mechanics: the comparison table's refund row reads 'The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls', and 'Before you start' lists 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12' and 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. A visitor who only half knows Vendure stops reading there. _[home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start'); home.phone.04-05]_
- J2 (8): The hero subtitle opens with developer jargon, "an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework", before it says what my staff can do at the counter. On a phone, the "For cashiers: At the till" link wraps onto its own orphan line under the two buttons. _[home.desktop.01; home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[Home desktop and phone screenshots; text/home.txt]_
- J4 (9): On the phone the hero CTA row wraps, leaving the 'For cashiers: At the till' text link alone on a second row, indented about 7 px from the text column's left edge. The hero till screenshot's category chips are also cut at 'Gif'. _[home.phone.01]_
- J5 (9): The hero says what it is and who it is for, but nothing above the fold says who should NOT pick it over a hosted till such as Shopify POS or Square. A buyer has to scroll to 'Before you start' or open Limitations to learn it is desktop-Chrome only, has no cash-drawer or terminal integration, and refunds go through the Dashboard. _[home/desktop/01 hero]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The home page never says who makes VendurePOS. The only clue is the demo row 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie', which names another product's domain without explaining it, so a newcomer wonders whose analytics these are. The footer only says 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; footer]_
- J2 (9): The demo-vs-store table is honest that refunds are "No, not on plugin 0.3.0 … stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". It doesn't tell a shop owner who that administrator is (my freelance developer?) or how quickly a customer's refund actually settles. _[home.desktop.02 (demo vs your store table)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt; text/at-the-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): In the comparison table the 'Refund a sale at the till' row has a 4-line 'Your Vendure store' cell, which makes that row much taller than its neighbours and breaks the table's rhythm. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): The site leads with 'open-source' and 'The till's source is MIT licensed', then says building it yourself needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. It never says this is a paid, third-party commercial licence or what it costs. A sceptic reads that as hiding that the till cannot be self-hosted from the MIT source alone. The demo row 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' also names an unexplained third-party domain on the home page. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet and comparison table last row; limitations 'Setting up the store']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Step 3 says 'Review the generated migration and include it in config.dbConnectionOptions.migrations (or your existing migration glob)' but gives no snippet for that edit. ## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor cannot tell who is behind VendurePOS. No maker or team is named anywhere on the home page, and the only hint is an unexplained third-party-looking domain ('anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com') in the demo row. That makes trying the demo feel like being tracked by an unknown party. _[home.desktop.02 comparison table, last row; footer]_ Fix: Name the maker in the footer, for example 'Made by the team behind WCPOS', and in that table cell say what ph.wcpos.com is: 'our own usage analytics (PostHog), run by WCPOS'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till is the one page my staff would actually use, and it is a long, dense read with no short 'day at the till' checklist (open with float → scan and sell → card on our own terminal, then record it → keep the tab open if the internet drops → close, count, print Z). Its refund steps hand the cashier off to the developer page with "Plugin setup has the steps for them". _[at-the-till.phone.01-09; at-the-till.desktop.03]_ Fix: Put a 6-8 line printable checklist at the top of At the till covering opening, selling, card, offline and closing. Replace the refund pointer with one cashier instruction ("Write down the order code and tell the store manager; the refund is settled from the admin side") and keep the Admin API steps on Plugin setup only.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both inclusive and exclusive pricing must guess how to reconcile the two recommended tax strategies with one store-wide setting. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Add a mixed-channel case stating the supported configuration and expected rounding behavior, or explicitly identify it as unsupported. Include a concrete verification example covering both channel pricing modes.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The At the till docs screenshots are drawn at three different widths and in three different frame styles (full width, ~380 px card, ~510 px card), with about 70 px of extra top whitespace on the last two, so the page has no consistent figure rhythm. _[at-the-till.desktop.02, at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.desktop.04]_ Fix: Give every docs screenshot one figure component: the same max-width (or full content width with phone-shaped shots centred inside a fixed frame), the same border, radius and caption alignment, and the standard paragraph-to-figure top margin.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): 'Open-source, MIT licensed' is the headline claim, but self-building the till needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token' with no word that this is a paid commercial licence from a third party. A sceptic advising a client would flag it as an undisclosed cost and lock-in to the hosted app.vendurepos.com. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' → 'Setting up the store', last bullet; limitations 'Setting up the store' first bullet]_ Fix: Say plainly in that bullet and in Limitations that RxDB Premium is a paid licence sold by RxDB (link its pricing). Add that the hosted app.vendurepos.com needs no licence from you, and that the plugin is fully MIT with no paid dependency.
