# vendurepos.com climb: round 26, 2026-11-18

Judged: the live site https://vendurepos.com after #151 and #152 deployed.
The method, judges and rubric are the same as in [round 6](2026-11-17-round-6.md). J3 scored on its first run.

**The capture changed this round.** Round 25's J5 C5 ("the docs sidebar's grey panel stops near 900 px") was not
on the site. A full-page clip paints a sticky element only where it sits at scroll 0, so the Fumadocs sidebar
(sticky, as high as the viewport) ended at y=900 in every desktop docs shot. A visitor who scrolls always sees it.
`harness/capture.mjs` now shoots each desktop `/docs` segment as a scrolled viewport the height of the segment.
Home and the phone shots keep the full-page clip, because their sticky top bars would cover scrolled content.
So round 26's desktop docs shots differ from round 25's in that one way, and round 26 pick 1 was answered by this
harness fix, not by a site change.

## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): Above the fold is clear (headline, one-line Vendure explainer, Live demo / Quick start), but the first pill a newcomer reads is 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0', and lower down 'Before you start' asks them to make sense of 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', 'one tab per till and not a private window' and 'Z report' before anything tells them what they would pay or who the hosted app is for. _[home/desktop/01 (badge above h1); home/desktop/02 'Before you start' bullets 4-5]_
- J2 (8): The hero explains the product to developers, not to a shop owner: the line reads "(Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework)". Nothing above the fold tells the owner that a developer must first install a plugin and run migrations before staff can sell. That fact only becomes clear in the quick start. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero lede is a single 4-line paragraph (7 lines at 390 px) that includes a parenthetical definition '(Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework)', so the one-sentence promise gets lost in a block of grey text before the CTAs. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and offers two clear buttons, but nothing on the home page says who should not pick it or how it compares with what a Vendure shop would otherwise use (a hosted POS such as Shopify POS or Square, or no POS). A buyer has to assemble 'not for you if…' from the scattered 'Before you start' bullets: Postgres only, desktop Chrome tested, no drawer, no coupons. _[home.desktop.01 hero and home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (7): The site says 'open-source' in the hero and 'MIT licensed' in the footer, then says self-building needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token', but no page says whether the hosted app at app.vendurepos.com is free, paid or limited, or what RxDB Premium is. The words free, price and cost appear nowhere in home, docs, quick-start, connect-a-till or limitations, so a newcomer cannot tell what the catch is. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' bullet 5; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Building it yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token'; text/limitations.txt bullet 3]_
- J2 (9): The payment screenshot on the docs page shows a test-data product, "Tally Fixture Mug". It looks like a test rig rather than a shop. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 payment image]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): The comparison table's demo cell says it sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a different brand's domain, and the home page never says what wcpos is or how it relates to VendurePOS, which reads as an unexplained third party. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J5 (7): The headline and footer say 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', yet 'To build and host it yourself instead of using app.vendurepos.com, you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. A commercial dependency gates self-hosting. The site never says what that licence costs, never names who runs app.vendurepos.com, and gives no support contact or maintainer. The only hint at an owner is an unexplained ph.wcpos.com in the comparison table. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5 and the 'Sales reach Vendure' table row; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The quick start opens with 'Start with a working Vendure 3.6+ project backed by Postgres' but gives no link to how to get one, so a visitor without a Vendure store hits a dead end on line one. After that the nine numbered steps, the commands and 'Check it worked' are complete. _[quick-start/desktop/01, paragraph under 'Try it first']_
- J2 (7): Refunds are missing from the real-store till, which is stated honestly as "On plugin 0.3.0 the till cannot refund... If you hand back cash, record it on the till with Pay out". The owner learns this only after a developer has finished steps 1-7 of the quick start (npm, TallyPosPlugin, tsx migrations, bearer tokenMethod, CORS, tax strategy). No single page answers "can my staff sell tomorrow" with a yes or no and a checklist for the owner. _[quick-start.desktop.01-04; at-the-till refunds section]_
- J3 (8): The tax instructions prescribe different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but only say a store mixing pricing modes 'must account for the store-wide strategy', leaving that configuration decision unresolved. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start step 6, 'Why these pairings'; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' is nine lettered sub-steps (a–i) of prose with no screenshot of the 'Sign in to Vendure' form or a synced order, so the first moment a developer touches the till has no visual check of what they should see. _[quick-start.desktop.03, step 8]_
- J5 (8): Troubleshooting says the plugin-missing notice clears when 'The store must advertise order.create v4 and register v1, as every plugin release from 0.1.0 does' (the quick start's step 9 says the same). But At the till says 'Charges need plugin 0.3.0' and Notifications says an old plugin holds back 'fee and shipping charges'. A store on 0.1.0 or 0.2.0 passes the stated check and silently loses Add charge, with no troubleshooting line that says why. _[troubleshooting.txt 'Store settings'; quick-start.desktop.04 step 9; at-the-till.desktop.01 'Add a charge']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor never learns what VendurePOS costs. The hero says open-source and the footer says MIT, but the hosted app's terms are never stated, and the only cost signal is an unexplained 'RxDB Premium licence token' needed to self-host, which reads like a hidden catch. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' bullet 5; no free, price or cost wording on any page]_ Fix: Add one plain line near the hero or in 'Before you start', e.g. 'app.vendurepos.com is free to use with your own Vendure store; self-hosting the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence', or whatever the true terms are, and link RxDB Premium to its page.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): When sales or register updates get stuck, the troubleshooting entry "N till updates need attention" gives the owner and counter staff no plain action. It sends them to dig a DEBUG "status=rejected errorCode=..." line out of Settings > Sync log, then says "the till has no retry or skip, and fixing the store does not make it send the update again". On a phone between customers this reads as "your sales may be lost and you can't do anything". _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 / troubleshooting.phone.01, "N till updates need attention"]_ Fix: Open the entry with two lines for the cashier: "Keep selling. Don't reload or sign out. Close the day as normal and tell whoever runs your store." Then add one sentence on whether the stuck sales are safe. Move the sync-log, errorCode and tallyResolveNeedsAdmin detail into a sub-section headed "For whoever runs your Vendure server".
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to reconcile the two prescribed strategies with the single store-wide setting. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start step 6, 'Why these pairings'; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg]_ Fix: Add a short mixed-pricing recommendation stating which strategy to use and how to verify totals in each channel. If that configuration is outside the supported setup, state that explicitly.
- **J4 Designer** (C4): The Troubleshooting 'Till updates line' section packs the cashier's action, the store owner's log-copy procedure, a DEBUG log pattern, three errorCode outcomes and a warning into one bullet with nested bullets and an orphaned trailing paragraph. On a phone this is a 15+ line wall that is very hard to follow when a till is actually failing. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 / troubleshooting.phone.02-03]_ Fix: Split it into two short labelled blocks ('At the counter' and 'For the store owner': numbered steps 1–4), and put the errorCode outcomes in a two-column list or table, with the 'Do not clear the site's data' warning as a callout.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells VendurePOS as 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', but self-hosting the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence at a cost never stated. Otherwise a shop depends on app.vendurepos.com, whose operator, support channel and continuity are never named. A sceptic would advise a client that the open-source claim does not buy independence. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5; limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3; connect-a-till.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a short 'Who runs this and what self-hosting costs' block to the home page and Limitations. It should name the maintainer and a support contact, give the RxDB Premium price or a link to it, and say what happens to tills if app.vendurepos.com goes away.
