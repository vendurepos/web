## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the rest of the home page turns into a setup checklist that a newcomer can't parse. 'Before you start' gives 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'tested on 3.7.3' and 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', and the badge above the H1 says 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' before the page has said what the plugin is. _[home.desktop.01 (badge) and home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04-05 ('Before you start')]_
- J2 (9): On a phone the header shows only "Docs" and "Live demo"; "Quick start" and "At the till" (the pages an owner needs: can staff sell, how do I close the day) are hidden behind a chevron, and the hero never says in plain words that a shop owner needs their developer for setup. _[home.phone.01 header and hero]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero puts a 58-word, four-line centred paragraph ('VendurePOS is an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework. It is made for shops ... keeps selling when the connection drops.') between the H1 and the CTAs, and its last line 'connection drops.' sits alone. That is a lot to read in five seconds. _[home/desktop/01 hero; home/phone/01]_
- J5 (8): Nothing on the home page says who makes or maintains VendurePOS: the only hint is the footer line 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.', so a buyer comparing it with Square or Shopify POS cannot tell a company product from a side project. _[home.desktop.02 footer; home.phone.05]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The live-demo row of the comparison table says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'. A newcomer sees a different brand's domain receiving data with no word on who that is. The explanation (a self-hosted PostHog server that also counts this site's page views) appears only on Connect a till. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (9): The phone hero shows the till running in a phone layout (Products / Cart tabs), but "Before you start" says "tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet". That is a mixed signal for an owner choosing a counter device. _[home.phone.01 vs home "Before you start"]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The home comparison table says the demo 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' and never says on that page why a VendurePOS demo reports to a wcpos.com host. An unexplained third-party domain is the one line that reads as something left unsaid. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J5 (7): The hero says 'open-source till' and the footer 'MIT licensed', but self-hosting needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. The site never says whether that licence costs money or who sells it, and never says who runs app.vendurepos.com or on what terms. So the practical route is a third-party hosted till with no named operator, while the demo reports to an unexplained ph.wcpos.com. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store'; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The quick start has no picture of the sign-in screen, the open-register card or a synced order. 'Check it worked' starts 'Once the till shows the order as synced' but never says what synced looks like on the till. That is the 'N orders waiting to send' header text, which only Troubleshooting explains. _[quick-start.desktop.03-04 (step 8 and 'Check it worked'); text/quick-start.txt]_
- J2 (8): For a developer the path is complete, but the device-key role a till uses "has the step 4 permissions except ReadOrder, so add ReadOrder to that role yourself". A freelancer can miss this, and the till's order history is then refused on day one (the changelog repeats it as "Not in the release notes"). _[quick-start "Optional: a device key"; changelog.desktop.01 0.2.0 last bullet]_
- J3 (8): The tax instructions prescribe different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but tell a store mixing these modes only to 'account for the store-wide strategy', leaving its configuration unresolved. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:846–875, Quick start step 6]_
- J4 (8): Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' lists nine lettered sub-steps (a. store URL, b. channel token, c. barcode field ... i. offline) describing a sign-in form and register screen with no picture of either, so the reader cannot see what success looks like until 'Check it worked'. The only till screenshots are on At the till. _[quick-start/desktop/03-04; quick-start/phone/07-08]_
- J5 (8): Quick start step 8h and 'Check it worked' say 'Once the till shows the order as synced' but never say where the till shows that: the Orders list status 'Synced', or the header 'N orders waiting to send' clearing. That is only on Troubleshooting, so the success signal on the till is a guess. _[quick-start.desktop.04 / quick-start.phone.08]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The home page's demo-versus-own-store table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. For a visitor who has never heard of VendurePOS, an unexplained third-party domain receiving data from the demo is the line most likely to make them hesitate before clicking 'Live demo'. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline', Live demo column]_ Fix: Say who it is in place, e.g. 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to our own self-hosted analytics (ph.wcpos.com, the same server that counts this site's page views), with no cookie', and keep the link to the details.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): On a real store a till refund can't be finished by the shop: "No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". For every refund the owner has to call their freelance developer, and the docs give no non-developer way to settle it. _[home.desktop.02 "Live demo or your own store" refund row; limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Give owners a no-code way to settle POS refunds (a Dashboard action, or a one-page "refund a till sale" checklist an owner can follow), and say in the home table what staff do at the counter meanwhile (Pay out cash / refund on the card terminal), linking to that checklist.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the supported store-wide tax strategy from the setup instructions. _[Quick start step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:873–875]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and how to verify its totals, or explicitly state that this configuration is outside the documented setup.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a phone, the Quick start's TypeScript blocks soft-wrap with a hanging indent. generateMigration(config, { name: 'AddVendurePos', outputDir: './src/migrations' }) and the runMigrations, cors and taxOptions snippets break into fragments that no longer match the code's real structure, on the one page a developer follows line by line. _[quick-start/phone/03, 05, 06]_ Fix: Below 640 px, make code blocks scroll horizontally (white-space: pre; overflow-x: auto). Alternatively, re-format the snippets so each argument and property sits on its own line under about 40 characters.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): For a sceptic advising a client, the site does not say who stands behind the product or what self-hosting really needs. The 'open-source, MIT' till needs an 'RxDB Premium licence token' to build, with no word on whether that is paid or where to get it. The alternative is app.vendurepos.com, with no named operator, terms or continuity. No maintainer is named, and the demo's analytics go to an unexplained ph.wcpos.com. Together with a plugin changelog that stops at 2026-10-06, a client cannot judge lock-in or longevity. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store' + footer; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go'; changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a short 'Who runs this' line to the home 'Before you start' block: who maintains VendurePOS and hosts app.vendurepos.com (and that ph.wcpos.com belongs to them), whether the hosted till is free and for how long, and whether the RxDB Premium licence costs money, with a link to where to get it.
