## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the sentence under the buttons ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first: the quick start sets it up, then signs the till in and makes a sale') is a second, denser pitch, and the term 'till' is never glossed, so a visitor who searched 'point of sale' has to work out that till = the POS app. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01, hero]_
- J2 (8): Hero's second sentence leads with developer jargon, "the open-source headless commerce framework", before telling an owner what the till does at the counter; phone header shows only "Docs" and "Live demo" _[home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home.desktop.01.jpg; home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): At 390 px the hero proof image is a mobile till crop showing only two product cards, with the category chip row cut off at "Gif" and no cart visible, so the one picture meant to sell the product says very little on a phone. _[home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The first screen says what it is, who it is for and what to click, but never what it costs: nothing near the hero says the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free to use, which is the first question a buyer comparing against Shopify POS or Square asks. _[home/desktop/01 (hero and 'Your own store needs the plugin…' line)]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (7): The home page never says whether the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com costs anything, yet 'Before you start' says building it yourself 'you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', and the demo row names an unexplained third-party domain 'ph.wcpos.com'. Together they make a first-timer suspect a paid catch or tracking behind 'open-source'. _[home/desktop/02, 'Setting up the store' last bullet and the 'Sales reach Vendure' table row]_
- J2 (9): Refund row is honest but ends in jargon an owner cannot act on: "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls" _[home.desktop.02 (Live demo or your own store table)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): A real limit is buried in a dense table cell: the "Refund a sale at the till" row runs four lines in the right column and says refunds need "two Admin API calls", while the demo column on "Sales reach Vendure" runs three lines. The table rhythm breaks exactly where the caveat sits. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): The default path runs your till from app.vendurepos.com, yet the site never says who operates it, whether it stays free or what happens if it goes away; the only exit, self-hosting, needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' with no price or link, so 'MIT licensed' quietly depends on a paid third-party licence, and the analytics host ph.wcpos.com hints at a parent (WCPOS) the site never names. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start › Setting up the store'; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The quick start is complete and ends with a 'Check it worked' list, but it carries leftover side paths a newcomer must skip past: a collapsed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' note in step 5 and an 'Other ways to run the migration' box offering two alternatives with a warning not to combine them. _[quick-start/desktop/03 top ('If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app') and quick-start/desktop/02 ('Other ways to run the migration')]_

- J3 (8): The tax setup prescribes different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channel pricing but leaves mixed-mode stores with only 'must account for the store-wide strategy', without a supported choice or verification procedure. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:840-869]_
- J4 (7): The quick start is 10,580 px tall on a phone and has no image at all. Step 8 (signing in at the till) and "Check it worked" (the order in the Vendure Dashboard) describe success in prose only, so the reader cannot match their screen to a picture. _[quick-start.desktop.03, quick-start.desktop.04, quick-start.phone (all slices)]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor cannot tell what it costs. The home page says 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed' but never says whether app.vendurepos.com is free to use, while 'Before you start' brings up an 'RxDB Premium licence token' and the demo row names 'ph.wcpos.com'. That reads like a hidden paid dependency or tracker from an unrelated brand. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start > Setting up the store']_ Fix: Add one plain line near the hero or in 'Before you start', such as 'The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free to use; you only need an RxDB Premium licence if you build the till yourself'. Name ph.wcpos.com as the project's own analytics server, or link it to the explanation on Connect a till.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): Troubleshooting tells the "store owner" to copy the refused line from the sync log and match its errorCode, and mixes GraphQL needs_admin steps with counter actions, so a non-developer owner cannot tell which steps are theirs and which belong to their developer _[troubleshooting.desktop.01–02]_ Fix: Split each notice into an "At the counter" box with plain actions (what staff do now, whether the sale is safe) and a "For your developer" box holding errorCodes, sync-log lines and GraphQL; replace "store owner" with "whoever runs your Vendure server" wherever the step is technical
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to reconcile the two prescribed strategies with the single store-wide setting. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:840-869]_ Fix: State the supported configuration or explicit limitation for mixed-mode channels. Provide a short verification procedure covering a sale in each pricing mode and its expected tax and rounding results.
- **J4 Designer** (C3): The quick start, the page that decides whether a developer adopts the tool, is 10,580 px of text and code on a phone with no screenshot anywhere. Neither the till's "Sign in to Vendure" screen in step 8 nor the synced POS order in the Vendure Dashboard under "Check it worked" is shown, so success is something the reader has to imagine. _[quick-start.desktop.03-04, quick-start.phone (all slices)]_ Fix: Add two captioned screenshots in the same framed style as the at-the-till images: the "Sign in to Vendure" till screen in step 8, and the synced POS order (with its tally-pos payment and tally-in-store shipping) in the Vendure Dashboard under "Check it worked". On a phone, crop them to the key panel so each stays under one screen tall.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A consultant cannot tell a client who stands behind VendurePOS: the till they would run every sale through is hosted at app.vendurepos.com by an unnamed operator with no terms, the self-host fallback needs a paid RxDB Premium licence with no price, and the only clue to the maintainer is the unexplained ph.wcpos.com analytics domain. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start'; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go']_ Fix: Add two sentences to 'Before you start': who maintains VendurePOS and runs app.vendurepos.com (and its relation to WCPOS/TallyUI), that the hosted till is free with no uptime promise, and a link to RxDB Premium's price for self-hosting.
