## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.2)
- J1 (7): The home page never says what Vendure is: the hero ('Point of sale for Vendure ... signs in to your own Vendure store') assumes you already know, and the only way to find out is the 'Vendure' link in the first line of /docs, not anything on the home page; the four equal-weight buttons 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' also read as the same thing until you parse the small print below them. _[home/desktop/01 hero and CTA row; home/phone/01]_
- J2 (8): The home page's first feature cards talk to developers, not shop owners: "records it in one transaction with an idempotency ledger" and "Keyboard-wedge scanners". And the three outline buttons "Quick start", "Try with your store" and "GitHub" give no hint which one is for the owner and which is for the developer. _[home.desktop.01 (hero buttons, 'Every sale lands once' and 'Barcode scanners' cards); home.phone.01-02]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero has four CTAs ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). The three secondary buttons look identical, so 'Try with your store' and 'GitHub' carry the same weight as 'Quick start', and the hero needs an extra caption line ('Your own store needs the plugin… the quick start sets it up') to say which one to pick. _[home.desktop.01 hero button row; home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The home page says what it is and where to click, but nothing tells a merchant who should not use it or how it differs from a hosted till. A merchant with integrated card terminals, coupon-driven promotions or MySQL only finds out from scattered bullets under 'Before you start' or a link to Limitations; there is no 'not for you if…' line. _[home.desktop.01-02 / text/home.txt ('What it does today', 'Before you start')]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the demo 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' with no word on what wcpos.com is or why a VendurePOS demo reports to it, so a newcomer meets an unexplained third-party domain right next to the privacy claim (and connect-a-till later adds that building the 'open-source' till needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token', which the home page never mentions). _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where your data goes']_
- J2 (8): The demo row says "All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie", but a VendurePOS site never explains why a different brand's domain (wcpos.com) gets data. To a shop owner that reads like an undisclosed third party. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home.phone.04; connect-a-till 'Where your data goes']_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/plugin-setup.txt]_
- J4 (8): The comparison table says the demo 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That is a different brand's domain, never explained anywhere on the site, and it reads as an unexplained third party in an otherwise very candid table. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; connect-a-till 'Where your data goes']_
- J5 (6): The home page and JSON-LD sell it as 'MIT licensed' and 'open-source' (isAccessibleForFree: true). Only a paragraph deep in Connect a till admits that 'Building it yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token', a paid proprietary dependency. Neither the home page nor Limitations mentions it. Separately, refunds and customer notes are shown in the demo but need 'a plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', and the changelog's last release is 2026-10-06 with no date for the next one. _[connect-a-till.desktop.01 ('Where your data goes', 2nd paragraph) vs home.desktop.02 footer and tech.json home JSON-LD; at-the-till.txt 'Refund a sale'; changelog.desktop.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who only half knows Vendure gets no explanation of it on the home page, so 'Point of sale for Vendure' plus 'signs in to your own Vendure store' doesn't tell them whether this is for them; they have to open the docs to learn what Vendure is. _[home/desktop/01 hero subline; home/phone/01]_ Fix: Add a short clause to the hero subline, for example 'for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce platform', with a link to vendure.io. Then make the 'Try with your store' button read 'Open the till' or similar, so it is clearly different from 'Quick start'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): The site never tells an owner how to set up a till login for each staff member. The only sign-in it documents is "a Vendure administrator" or a per-device key, while At the till assumes each cashier has their own name and account ("Tap your name in the bar", "Hand over … for the next account"). Without that, I can't tell whether my two staff can sell tomorrow without being given admin access to my store. _[connect-a-till 'Sign in with a staff account' (connect-a-till.desktop.01); quick-start step 8a (quick-start.desktop.03); at-the-till 'Check the register during the day']_ Fix: Add a short 'Set up your staff' section to Connect a till. It should say to create one Vendure administrator per cashier with only the vendurepos-pos-till role (TallyPosSell plus reads), not full admin, and how Hand over switches between them. Link it from step 8 of the quick start and from At the till.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The recommended staff-account setup does not provide a complete, explicit till-role permission set; developers must reconstruct it from prose and the changelog. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 4; text/connect-a-till.txt; text/changelog.txt, 0.2.0]_ Fix: List TallyPosSell, ReadCatalog, ReadSettings, ReadCustomer and CreateCustomer together in the staff-account instructions, with assignment to the intended channel. Link that list from quick-start step 4.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a 390 px phone the Quick start's config code blocks soft-wrap with no indentation, so the code a developer must copy loses its structure. The 'taxOptions' block and both migration scripts break mid-expression, and './vendure-config' is split across lines. _[quick-start.phone.02, quick-start.phone.03, quick-start.phone.05]_ Fix: Turn soft-wrap off in code blocks ('white-space: pre' with 'overflow-x: auto') so long lines scroll sideways instead of wrapping. If wrapping must stay, give wrapped lines a hanging indent.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The home page sells it as 'MIT licensed' and open-source, but self-hosting the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence token. That fact appears only mid-paragraph on Connect a till and is missing from the home page and from Limitations. I would flag that to a client as an undisclosed open-core catch. _[connect-a-till.desktop.01 ('Building it yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token') vs home.desktop.02 footer 'MIT licensed' and limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a Limitations bullet and one line in the home page's 'Before you start': 'The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free; building the till yourself needs a paid RxDB Premium licence.' Link both to the Connect a till paragraph.
