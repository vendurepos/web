## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero's last sentence, "Every sale lands in your Vendure store once, as a normal order at the till's price", and the first two feature cards ("Every sale lands once", "The same sale sent 200 times makes one order") answer a duplicate-order worry a newcomer doesn't have yet, so the first screen spends its space on plumbing instead of saying who it's for (a shop selling at a counter that already runs Vendure). _[home/desktop/01 hero paragraph and first feature row; home/phone/01-02]_
- J2 (8): The hero's till screenshot is cropped on phone so the cart column header reads only "Q...". Beyond one feature card, the home page never says in counter terms what a cashier's day looks like (open register, sell, close). _[home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero has a third line of small print under the two buttons ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first…'), which pulls attention from the CTA pair. On phone the first hero screenshot shows only the cart column, not the product grid that sells the product. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): Hero says "Point of sale for Vendure" with "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0" but never says who it is for or not for, or how it compares with Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed or a Vendure admin-only workflow; a sceptic has to infer fit from the "What it does today" cards. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A newcomer never learns who makes VendurePOS: the home page and footer name no maintainer, and the only outside party mentioned is an unexplained ph.wcpos.com that receives the demo's usage events. _[home/desktop/02 comparison table last row and footer]_ Fix: Add one line to the footer (or under the hero) naming who builds and maintains VendurePOS, and in the table say ph.wcpos.com is that maintainer's own self-hosted analytics.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): The one thing a shop owner most needs to brief staff on, what to do when the internet drops, is a single dense paragraph. The "don't reload / don't clear site data" warning that protects unsent sales is buried mid-paragraph. Troubleshooting also mixes counter actions with errorCode/GraphQL content meant for whoever runs the server. _[at-the-till.phone.07 (If the internet drops); troubleshooting.desktop.01]_ Fix: Split "If the internet drops" into three short bullet groups: what still works; DO NOT reload, close the tab or clear site data (bold); how to tell queued sales have sent. In Troubleshooting, put the errorCode and GraphQL admin material under a separate "For whoever runs your Vendure server" heading, below the cashier notices.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The refund settlement recipe leaves developers to infer how an external card payment is actually returned before the refund is marked settled. _[shots/plugin-setup.desktop.03.jpg; text/plugin-setup.txt, Settle a Dashboard refund]_ Fix: State that the operator must return cash or complete the refund on the external terminal/provider before recording settlement, and explain what transactionId should reference. Link cash refunds to the existing Pay out instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The hero till screenshot, the first thing a visitor sees, ends mid-row with slivers of a fourth row of product tiles and a clipped 'Guest · 3 items' strip, so it reads as an accidental crop rather than a composed product shot. _[home.desktop.01 (and the same crop in og-image.png)]_ Fix: Crop or mask the hero screenshot on a row boundary: end cleanly after the third tile row with a soft fade or a frame edge, or recapture at a viewport height that ends between rows. Then regenerate the OG image from the same clean crop.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): No stated operator or support path: the site never says who builds VendurePOS or runs the hosted till app.vendurepos.com (footer: "MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS."), the hosted till is unversioned while "Pre-release: expect changes before 1.0", and the only exit from it needs a paid "RxDB Premium licence token". I would advise a client not to depend on an anonymous hosted till. _[home.desktop footer slice; limitations.phone.01; changelog.phone.01]_ Fix: Add a short 'Who runs this' block on home and Overview naming the maintainer/company, an issue-tracker and support link, and the hosted till's current version with its own changelog entry.
