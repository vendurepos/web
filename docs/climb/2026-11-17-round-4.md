## Capture fix: the first round-4 scoring was thrown out

The first capture of round 4 (mean 7.43, delta -0.34) showed the `generateMigration` code block on
`quick-start.phone.02` as an empty grey box, and every judge marked C6 down for it. The live page renders the
block. The blank came from the capture itself: a second full-page clip on the same Chromium page left that
horizontally scrolling block unpainted. Reproduced on the live site with two clips in a row (blank), and fixed by
re-applying the viewport between clips (painted). `capture.mjs` now does that, the round was captured again,
and all five judges scored the new capture. The scores below are from that second capture; #60 does not touch
code blocks (`.prose :not(pre) > code` only).

## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.0)
- J1 (7): The home page never shows the till: the hero is a headline, a paragraph and four similar-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), then ten identical grey text cards, with no screenshot of the POS anywhere, and nothing says in plain words what Vendure is (only the docs Overview links it). A visitor from search cannot picture the product without leaving for the demo. _[home.desktop.01 hero and 'What it does today' grid; home.phone.01]_
- J2 (8): The hero gives four equal-sized buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). A non-developer can't tell 'Quick start' from 'Try with your store', and only the small line underneath ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first') says the second one needs the developer first. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap 2+2)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (7): The hero offers four near-equal buttons in one row ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), and the line under them has to explain the difference between 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store'. There is no image of the till anywhere above the fold, so a visitor reads about a POS without ever seeing one. _[home.desktop.01 (y 372-460), home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The home page has no picture of the till: the hero, all ten 'What it does today' cards and the demo comparison table are text, so a buyer comparing it with Shopify POS or Square cannot see what the till looks like without leaving for the demo. _[home/desktop/01, home/phone/01-03]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): Naming is inconsistent: the product is VendurePOS, but the code and docs use 'TallyPosPlugin', 'tally-pos', '/tally/v1/commands' and 'TallyPosSell', and the footer says 'Built with Tally UI' while the Overview says 'built on TallyUI'. Nothing explains the relationship, so a newcomer wonders whose product this is. _[home.desktop.02 footer; docs.desktop.01 intro; quick-start.desktop.01 step 2 heading]_
- J2 (8): The site never says what counter hardware works beyond keyboard-wedge scanners. Receipts only 'open the browser's print dialog', with no word on thermal receipt printers or whether the cash drawer opens, and Limitations is silent on both, so the limits a shop owner needs most are missing from the honest list. _[limitations.desktop.01; at-the-till.txt 'Print the receipt and start again'; home 'Cash, card and receipts' card]_
- J3 (9): The quantified acceptance-run claim links to test source, without a dated run result identifying the tested plugin version. _[text/home.txt, 'Keeps selling offline'; tech.json, home.desktop.links]_
- J4 (8): No page on the site shows the product: there is not a single screenshot of the till, the receipt or the Z report. The only proof that the UI exists is the 'Live demo' link and text claims. Only 2 of the 10 feature cards ('Read the acceptance test', 'Read the 200-send test') link to any evidence. _[home.desktop.01 feature grid; connect-a-till.desktop.01 ('On Sign in to Vendure, choose Email and password or Device key' with no image)]_
- J5 (6): The site asks you to type a Vendure administrator email and password into a hosted third-party origin ('Open https://app.vendurepos.com ... your administrator email and password') and to allow that origin in CORS with credentials: true, yet no page says who runs app.vendurepos.com (or the second origin vendurepos.vercel.app), whether credentials or orders pass through it, or how to host the till yourself. Nor does the site say who maintains the project: the only provenance is 'Built with Tally UI'. _[quick-start/desktop/01 (hosted POS paragraph) and 03 (step 8); quick-start/desktop/02 step 5; docs/desktop/01; home/desktop/02 footer]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): No picture of the till anywhere on the home page (or in the docs): a first-time visitor reads ten text cards about registers, Z reports and parked carts but never sees what the product looks like, so deciding whether it is worth a click means leaving the site for the demo. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, between the hero buttons and 'What it does today']_ Fix: Add one screenshot of the till mid-sale (cart, products, tender) under the hero buttons, linked to the live demo. Also add a half-line to the hero: 'Vendure is an open-source TypeScript commerce platform'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): A shop owner can't learn from the site whether their counter hardware works. Only keyboard-wedge scanners are covered. Receipts go through 'the browser's print dialog', and nothing is said about thermal receipt printers, a cash drawer opening on a cash sale, or whether a print dialog appears on every sale. Limitations, the page meant to answer 'what does it not do', doesn't mention any of it. _[limitations.desktop.01 / limitations.phone.01; at-the-till 'Print the receipt and start again']_ Fix: Add a short 'At the counter' block to Limitations (and link it from At the till) saying plainly what works: scanners (keyboard-wedge), receipt printers (any printer the browser can print to, with the paper width tested), cash drawer (opens or not), card terminal (separate, not connected).
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): Recovering a needs_admin sale requires choosing applied or rejected without documented decision criteria, leaving a developer to guess during a potentially money-sensitive recovery. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way']_ Fix: Add a short checklist explaining which persisted order, payment, fulfilment and stock states justify each resolution. State what each resolution changes and when the till should retry.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a 390 px phone, the one table a developer must copy from in the quick start (step 6, 'Channel pricing' -> 'taxOptions.orderTaxCalculationStrategy') splits the class names it is there to deliver into two-line fragments ('new OrderLevelTax' / 'CalculationStrategy()') with orphaned parentheses, so the critical config value is hard to read and to copy. _[quick-start.phone.05 (bottom) and quick-start.phone.06 (top)]_ Fix: Below about 640 px, render this table as two small labelled code blocks (tax-exclusive / tax-inclusive), or set white-space: nowrap on inline code inside tables and let the table scroll horizontally.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Setting up a real store means typing a Vendure admin password into app.vendurepos.com and whitelisting that origin (plus vendurepos.vercel.app) with credentials in CORS. No page says who operates the hosted till, what data reaches it, or whether you can serve the till from your own domain, and the site names no maintainer. A sceptical adviser would stop a client here. _[quick-start/desktop/01 (hosted POS paragraph), quick-start/desktop/02 (step 5 CORS), quick-start/desktop/03 (step 8 sign-in)]_ Fix: Add a short 'Where your data goes' block to the Overview and the top of the quick start: the hosted till is a static build that talks only to your store, nothing is sent to vendurepos servers, it is run by <named maintainer>, and you can self-host it with <link>.
