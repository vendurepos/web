## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The first feature card, 'Keeps selling offline', warns that 'sales not yet sent are lost if this site's data is cleared'. On vendurepos.com, 'this site' reads as the marketing site, not the till app. It also puts a data-loss caveat in the first thing a newcomer reads below the hero. _[home/desktop/01 (card 'Keeps selling offline'); home/phone/02]_
- J2 (8): On a phone the header shows only "Docs" and "Live demo" plus a chevron, so "At the till", the one page my staff need, is hidden behind a menu; the hero also leads with developer words ("headless commerce framework", "plugin 0.3.0") rather than what a counter gets. _[home.phone.01 (header); home.desktop.01 hero]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Hero lead paragraph runs four lines before the CTAs, and a second sub-note sits under the buttons, so the fold reads text-heavy next to the till screenshot _[home.desktop.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero never says who VendurePOS is not for or how it differs from Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed. Real limits a client would weigh (refund is developer-only on a live store, needs_admin chores) sit far below the fold. _[home.desktop.01 hero]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor weighing a pre-release till for their shop cannot tell who is behind it. No maker or team is named anywhere on the home page, and the only clue is an unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' analytics domain in the comparison table, which looks like data going to an unknown third party. _[home/desktop/02 (comparison table, last row, and footer)]_ Fix: Add one footer line naming the maker (for example 'Made by the team behind WCPOS, wcpos.com'). Where ph.wcpos.com appears, say it is that team's own self-hosted analytics server.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): For a shop owner, refunding a real sale on their own store is not a counter task the site can walk staff through. The home table says "No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". At the till then tells staff to also record a Pay out "or the drawer count comes up short". The owner, who is not a developer, is left with a three-party, API-call job for a normal customer return. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, row 'Refund a sale at the till'; at-the-till 'Refund a sale'; plugin-setup 'Settle a Dashboard refund']_ Fix: Add a boxed 'Refunds on your own store (plugin 0.3.0)' checklist on At the till that names who does each step: the cashier records the Pay out, the owner refunds in the Dashboard, and the freelancer runs the two settle calls once (paste-ready). Link that box from the home table row.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the supported store-wide tax strategy from the installation instructions. _[Quick start, step 6; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; llms-full.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Add an explicit mixed-channel case stating the supported configuration and how to verify totals in both pricing modes. If no configuration is supported, state that limitation directly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): At the till screenshots use three different widths and frames: the payment screen fills the column while the cashier sheet (~420px) and closure (~545px) float centered inside oversized padded outer frames (box in a box) _[at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.desktop.04]_ Fix: Render all till screenshots at the full content-column width with one shared frame style, and drop the extra padded outer frame around the cashier sheet and closure crops
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site says the till's source is MIT licensed, but self-hosting needs an RxDB Premium licence token that the site never says is paid. That undercuts the open-source claim for anyone weighing it against Shopify POS or Square. It is compounded by no plugin release since 2026-10-06, with a known ReadOrder bug left as a documented workaround. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store'; Limitations 'Setting up the store']_ Fix: State that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence, link its pricing, and say plainly that the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com avoids it. Say who runs app.vendurepos.com, that it is free, and how long that is promised for. Ship a plugin patch that adds ReadOrder to the till-key role, or give a dated roadmap line for it.
