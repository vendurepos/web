## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero itself is clear, but the home page below it turns into docs a first-timer cannot parse: the comparison table's refund cell reads "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", and "Before you start" lists "Node ^20.19 || >=22.12" and "RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)" on the marketing page. _[home/desktop/02 ('Live demo or your own store' refund row, 'Before you start'); home/phone/04]_
- J2 (8): On the phone the hero till screenshot is cropped to two product tiles (Canvas Apron, Chocolate Biscuits) with the category chips cut at "Gif" and no cart, total or Pay button visible, so a shop owner reading on a phone never sees what a sale looks like above the fold. _[home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): On phone the hero product shot is cropped to a two-card slice of the product grid, so the cart and the €20.09 total that the caption and alt describe ('a cart of three items totalling €20.09') appear only as the 'Cart (3) · €20.09' tab label. The hero intro is also a four-line paragraph where a one-line deck would do. _[home.phone.01 (screenshot below the CTAs); home.desktop.01 hero paragraph]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and offers 'Live demo' / 'Quick start', but the facts that rule a shop out for someone comparing it with Square or Shopify POS (no integrated card terminal, no cash drawer, iPad and phones 'not tested yet', Postgres only) sit two screens down under 'Before you start'. Nothing near the top says who should not use it. _[home/desktop/01 hero vs home/desktop/02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The home page's demo row says the demo "sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com". A second brand's domain shows up with no word on who wcpos is or how it relates to VendurePOS, so a newcomer wonders whose product this really is. _[home/desktop/02 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (8): Home promises every sale "lands in Vendure once" and keeps selling offline, but the cost of that (a reload or reopened browser needs the connection; unsent sales are lost if site data is cleared; refused register open/close updates never reach Vendure, "keep the printed Z reports") is only stated on At the till and Limitations, not next to the offline claim on Home. _[home.desktop.01 hero vs at-the-till.desktop.03 "If the internet drops" and limitations.desktop.01]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The live-demo row of the comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That is a third domain the home page never explains (the explanation sits on Connect a till), so a privacy-minded reader meets an unknown host in the one place meant to reassure them. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; home.phone.04]_
- J5 (7): The demo is ahead of anything you can install, and the newest plugin is six weeks old. The changelog's last plugin release is '0.3.0 (2026-10-06)', 'Released the same day as 0.2.0, a few hours later', with nothing since. Meanwhile the demo refunds at the till and takes a customer note ('On plugin 0.3.0, Order details has no customer note. On the live demo, type a Customer note'), and the site gives no next version or date for either. The home 'Live demo or your own store' table lists the refund gap but leaves out the customer-note gap. _[changelog/desktop/01; at-the-till.txt 'Order details'; home/desktop/02 comparison table]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): Step 6 makes you change the store-wide orderTaxCalculationStrategy and warns "These settings are store-wide and also affect the web shop". It never says what changes for the existing web shop's totals, so a developer with a live store has to guess whether turning on the POS will move their online prices. _[quick-start/desktop/03 '6. Choose tax rounding for your pricing mode']_
- J2 (7): No page says what to put on the counter: At the till is written for touch ("Tap a product", "tap a tab") while Home and Limitations say only desktop Chrome is tested and "tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet", and the scanner, receipt printer, card terminal and cash drawer are only covered as things the till does not do. _[at-the-till.desktop.01 vs limitations.desktop.01 and home "Before you start"]_
- J3 (8): The tax guidance says a store mixing pricing modes must account for the store-wide strategy, but provides no configuration or verification procedure for that case. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start step 6, 'Why these pairings'; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Step 4 lists six permissions ('Sign in with a Vendure administrator whose role in the till's channel has these permissions') but never says where to create that role or administrator (Dashboard > Roles). It also comes after three file-creation and migration sub-steps, so the one manual Dashboard task in the path is the only step without an instruction. _[quick-start.desktop.02, '4. Enable bearer authentication']_
- J5 (8): A known bug in the shipped plugin is left for the reader to work around by hand. The device-key role 'has the step 4 permissions except ReadOrder, so add ReadOrder to that role yourself'. The changelog even calls this 'Not in the release notes', and no 0.3.1 fixes it. A developer following the device-key path has to catch this caveat or the till's order history is refused. _[quick-start 'Optional: a device key for each till'; changelog/desktop/01 0.2.0 last bullet]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the home page's lower half reads as setup docs: the refund cell explains Pending refunds and "two Admin API calls", and 'Before you start' lists Node semver ranges, Postgres versions and an RXDB_PREMIUM licence token. A first-time visitor deciding in under a minute meets this jargon wall and gets no plain 'what you need' summary. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04 ('Live demo or your own store' refund row, 'Before you start')]_ Fix: Shorten the refund cell to "No, refund in the Vendure Dashboard for now (see Limitations)". Cut 'Before you start' down to three plain lines (a Vendure 3.6+ store on Postgres, the plugin, a desktop browser) linking to the Overview's requirements, and move the Node, RxDB and Admin API details there.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): A shop owner asking "can my staff sell with this tomorrow" gets no counter setup: At the till says "Tap a product" as if on a tablet, while Home and Limitations say only desktop Chrome is tested and iPads, tablets and phones are not, and the scanner, printer, card terminal and drawer appear only as limitations. _[at-the-till.desktop.01 (top) vs limitations.desktop.01 and home "Before you start"]_ Fix: Add a short "What you need at the counter" box at the top of At the till: a desktop or laptop running Chrome, a USB barcode scanner that types like a keyboard, any printer the browser can print to, the card terminal run separately with the amount keyed in, and the drawer opened by hand. Change "tap" to "click".
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels is told to account for a store-wide tax strategy without being told how to configure or verify it. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start step 6, 'Why these pairings'; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg]_ Fix: Add an explicit recommendation for mixed-pricing stores, or state the supported configuration constraint. Include a small sale example showing the expected tax and rounding result in each channel.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): Docs prose on phone is busy because every identifier is a padded, bordered chip. On a 390 px screen the chips split across lines ('packages/vendure–' / 'plugin/CHANGELOG.md'), stretch the line height of any line that holds one, and bloat headings ('2. Add TallyPosPlugin…' wraps to three lines). The quick start and changelog, the pages a developer reads on a phone, look ragged even though nothing overflows. _[changelog.phone.01; quick-start.phone.02 and .05; plugin-setup.phone.03]_ Fix: In prose and headings, give inline code no vertical padding and a hairline or no border, with box-decoration-break: clone, so it never changes line height. Drop the code formatting from the step-2 heading, and let long paths break only at '/'.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): As a sceptic advising a client: the proof and the demo describe code that has not shipped, while the installable plugin has not moved since 2026-10-06. The 'Read the acceptance test' and 'Read the 200-send test' buttons link to github.com/vendurepos/app/blob/main/…, not the plugin-v0.3.0 tag. The demo refunds at the till and takes customer notes that no released plugin supports. A known ReadOrder bug from 0.2.0 is documented as a workaround on three pages instead of fixed. That reads like a project whose demo outruns its releases, and the site gives no next-release date or maintenance signal to answer it. _[home/desktop/01 'Read the acceptance test' / 'Read the 200-send test' (tech.json links); changelog/desktop/01; at-the-till.txt 'Order details']_ Fix: Point the two test links at the plugin-v0.3.0 tag. Add a short 'Next plugin release' note at the top of the changelog that names what is on main but unreleased (till refunds, customer note, ReadOrder in the role) and when it is expected.
