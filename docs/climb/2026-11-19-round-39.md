## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The top of the page is clear ("Point of sale for Vendure", a real till screenshot, Live demo / Quick start), but the lower half of the home page drops into developer-only requirements a newcomer can't parse: "Node ^20.19 || >=22.12", "RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)", "refund stays Pending until it is settled", and "Built with TallyUI" is never explained. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' list and footer; home.phone.04-05]_
- J2 (8): The hero says 'Point of sale for Vendure' clearly, but the line under it leans on developer jargon ('the open-source headless commerce framework'), which means nothing to a shop owner whose store a freelancer built. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, hero subtitle]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero stacks three text blocks of different widths: the centred lede is about 640 px wide, but the caption under the buttons ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first: the quick start sets it up…') runs about 830 px, wider than the lede it sits under, so the centred column loses its shape just above the till screenshot. _[home/desktop/01]_
- J5 (8): The home page never says who should NOT use this or how it compares with a hosted POS: no line set against Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed, and no 'pick this if / skip this if'. The footer's only comparison is 'For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' A client weighing it against Square can't tell from the page when this is the right choice. _[home.desktop.01-03 / text/home.txt]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (7): Nothing on the home page says whether the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free or paid, and the only cost signal is that self-hosting needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token". On top of that, the demo row says usage events go to "ph.wcpos.com", a different brand the page never explains, so a newcomer is left wondering what it costs and who runs it. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 6 and 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J2 (9): The home badge reads 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' while the changelog says 'The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is version 0.1.0'. Two version numbers for one product, with nothing on the home page saying which one I'm running. _[home.desktop.01 badge vs changelog.desktop.01 / changelog.phone.01 intro]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The changelog says 'The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is version 0.1.0' directly above '0.3.0 (2026-10-06)', and the home badge says 'plugin 0.3.0'. App and plugin versions are never told apart in a sentence, so the two version lines look contradictory at a glance. _[changelog/desktop/01]_
- J5 (7): A known defect is shipped and documented instead of fixed. The changelog says 'Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder, so the till's order history is refused until you add it to the role.' The last release is 2026-10-06, six weeks back, and the hosted till is 'version 0.1.0' against plugin 0.3.0. The demo also shows 'Refund at the till (live demo)' while 'On plugin 0.3.0 the till cannot refund.' To a sceptic, the demo is selling features the installable plugin lacks. _[changelog.desktop.01 / text/changelog.txt; text/at-the-till.txt 'Refund a sale']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Quick start assumes you already run Vendure 3.6+ on Postgres. Step 2 then has to stop and explain "The plugin's code names say Tally, not VendurePOS: TallyPosPlugin, the TallyPosSell permission and the /tally/v1 routes", which adds a second name to keep in your head in the middle of setup. The steps themselves are ordered, have commands and end in a clear 'Check it worked' list. _[quick-start.desktop.01 step 2; quick-start.phone.02]_
- J2 (8): The staff-till setup hands a permission chore to the owner: 'On plugin 0.3.0 that role has no ReadOrder: add it yourself, or the till sells but its order history is refused'. Without that step, staff can't look up past orders on day one. _[quick-start step 4 (quick-start.desktop.02 / phone); repeated in changelog.desktop.01 0.2.0 notes and connect-a-till]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 prescribes different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but says a store mixing pricing modes 'must account for' this without explaining the supported configuration or verification. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start]_
- J4 (8): 'Check it worked' is a text-only checklist ('the payment is tally-pos, settled; the fulfilment is delivered…'). The quick start shows no picture of a synced order in the till or in the Vendure Dashboard, so success has to be pictured from prose at the end of a 6,700 px page. _[quick-start/desktop/04]_
- J5 (8): Step 4 hides a trap in a caveat: 'Also add ReadOrder, or the till sells but its order history is refused'. The optional device-key section then repeats that the plugin's own vendurepos-pos-till role lacks ReadOrder, so you must patch the role by hand. Step 5 also carries an unexplained collapsed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' expander that a new store owner can't act on. _[quick-start.desktop.02-03, quick-start.phone.05]_

### C4 Docs quality (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The versions contradict each other at first read: the Changelog says "The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is version 0.1.0" while the home badge says "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0". The changelog entries are internal shorthand ("advertises order.create 1–5 (ADR 0005)") with no plain summary above them. Connect a till also says data is "kept in SQLite databases inside this browser" when every other page says "SQLite are not supported". _[changelog.desktop.01 intro and 0.3.0 list; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Stored in this browser' bullet 4]_

Troubleshooting also sends staff round in a loop: 'Ask the store owner to look at the sync log…', then sends the owner on to whoever runs the server. _[at-the-till.desktop.02–03 (Refund a sale / Refund at the till (live demo)); troubleshooting.desktop.01]_
- J3 (8): The search-linked 'Sell while offline' section says everything except customer operations works offline, but omits the keep-tab-open restriction and a direct link to that essential caveat. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, Sell while offline; text/endpoint_api_search_query_offline.txt]_
- J4 (8): Changelog typography is inconsistent: 'order.create v5 custom lines…' and 'order.create 1–5' are plain text while every other identifier on the same lines (tallyCustomName, GET /tally/v1/info, TALLY-FEE) is set as code. The search API also gives every hit the breadcrumb 'VendurePOS' only, with no page name. _[changelog/desktop/01; text/endpoint_api_search_query_offline.txt]_
- J5 (8): The same ReadOrder caveat appears in quick-start (twice), connect-a-till (twice) and the changelog instead of once, as a fixed step. The changelog reads as internal protocol notes ('order.create v5 … (ADR 0005)'). Troubleshooting says 'The store must advertise order.create v4 and register v1' while the changelog lists versions up to v5, so the reader can't tell which applies. _[text/changelog.txt, text/troubleshooting.txt, text/connect-a-till.txt]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor can't find out from the home page what VendurePOS costs or who runs the hosted till. The page says "MIT licensed", then says self-hosting needs an "RxDB Premium licence token", and sends demo analytics to an unexplained "ph.wcpos.com", but it never states whether app.vendurepos.com is free to use. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' and 'Live demo or your own store' table; footer]_ Fix: Add one plain line near the hero or in 'Before you start', for example "The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free to use; the till and plugin are MIT licensed", plus a short note that ph.wcpos.com is the maker's own analytics server (WCPOS, the same team).
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till, the page my staff will actually use, mixes steps that only work on the live demo ('Refund at the till (live demo)', 'On the live demo, type a Customer note') into the instructions for a real store. A cashier on plugin 0.3.0 reads a refund procedure their till cannot do. _[at-the-till.desktop.02–03, sections 'Refund a sale' and 'Refund at the till (live demo)']_ Fix: Put everything that only works on the demo into one clearly labelled 'Only on the live demo' box, or take it out of the cashier guide. Keep 'Refund a sale' (Vendure Dashboard plus Pay out) as the only refund procedure for a real shop.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining inclusive and exclusive channels must guess how to reconcile the two prescribed tax strategies with the single store-wide setting. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Document the supported approach for mixed-pricing channels and how to verify totals in both modes. If that configuration is unsupported, state the limitation explicitly before the configuration snippet.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The home page's main block is ten equal-weight grey cards. The two tested, linked claims that set VendurePOS apart (offline selling, every sale lands once) get no more emphasis than 'Barcode scanners'. On a 390 px phone the grid becomes about 2,200 px of identical boxes, which buries the demo-vs-your-store comparison and the prerequisites below it. _[home/desktop/01-02; home/phone/02-03]_ Fix: Make the two tested claims a full-width, highlighted pair with their test links. Set the other eight as a compact two-column list (one column on phone) without card chrome, so the section is about half its current height.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic (knows Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed and the open-source WooCommerce/Medusa/Odoo tills)** (C2): The plugin's own till role (vendurepos-pos-till) has shipped without ReadOrder in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0, so out of the box a device-key till's order history is refused. The site documents this as a standing workaround in four places ('Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder'), and no fix has been released in the six weeks since 2026-10-06. To someone comparing it with Square or Shopify POS, that reads as an unmaintained pre-release. _[changelog.desktop.01 / text/changelog.txt; quick-start step 4 and the device-key section]_ Fix: Release 0.3.1 with ReadOrder added to the till role's permissions, and note it in the changelog. Until then, make 'add ReadOrder to the role' a numbered quick-start step and delete the repeated caveats.
