## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero, CTAs and screenshot make it clear at once, but the home page's closing 'Before you start' list falls into developer jargon a newcomer can't follow: 'you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', 'Vendure 3.6 or later on Postgres ... MySQL, MariaDB and SQLite are not supported'. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' (also home/phone/05)]_
- J2 (9): The hero is clear ('Point of sale for Vendure', Live demo / Quick start, 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0'), but the small note 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first…' is the only hint that a shop owner cannot just sign up; it's easy to miss under the two big buttons. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Below a strong hero, 'What it does today' is ten grey cards of exactly the same weight in a 5x2 grid. The three proven claims ('Keeps selling offline', 'Every sale lands once', with test links) sit at the same size as 'Barcode scanners' and 'Park a sale', so nothing in the section tells the reader what matters most. _[home/desktop/01-02 (grid at y~1300 on 01 to y~255 on 02); home/phone/02-03]_
- J5 (8): The fold says what it is and offers two clear next steps, but never says who should not pick it. The deal-breakers a buyer comparing it with Square or Shopify POS needs (no integrated card terminal, no cash drawer, no receipt printer, Postgres only, Chrome only) sit in the sixth section, 'Before you start', below ten feature cards. _[home.desktop.01 hero vs home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; home.phone.04-05]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): In the home comparison table the live demo 'sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That is a domain from a different brand that the home page never explains, so a visitor who has never heard of VendurePOS asks who wcpos is and why the demo reports to it. Only Connect a till explains it is a self-hosted PostHog server. _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' (home/phone/04)]_
- J2 (9): The at-the-till payment screenshot shows a cart line named 'Tally Fixture Mug', a test-fixture name that looks out of place next to the real-looking catalogue ('Cold Brew', 'Canvas Apron') used everywhere else. _[at-the-till/desktop/02]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): The cashier guide's screenshots come from different sessions and don't agree. The payment shot contains a product named 'Tally Fixture Mug' (a test fixture name). The cashier sheet shows 'Your sales today €21.29 / In the drawer €121.29', but the closure shot right after it shows 'Counted €120.09 / Expected €120.09'. _[at-the-till/desktop/02 (payment screen), 03 (cashier sheet and closure)]_
- J5 (7): The site leads with 'MIT licensed' and 'open-source till', but the only way to run the till without paying anyone is the hosted app.vendurepos.com. The site never names who runs that app or states its cost, terms or uptime. Self-hosting 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and never says this is a paid commercial licence or what it costs. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5; limitations 'Setting up the store'; connect-a-till 'Where your data goes']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The quick start is complete and gives a clear 'Check it worked', but step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' opens with a 'Before a sale on a live store' warning that sends the reader off to two other pages ('Set up email', 'Check your own code') before the first sale. That breaks the straight line, and the reader can't tell whether those steps are required. _[quick-start/desktop/03 step 8 warning box (quick-start/phone/07)]_
- J2 (8): The docs overview says the till 'signs in to your own Vendure store with an administrator account', while connect-a-till tells you to give each cashier a limited role and to add 'ReadOrder' by hand because the auto-created 'vendurepos-pos-till' role lacks it on 0.3.0. The overview reads as if staff need a full admin login. _[docs/desktop/01 (text/docs.txt line 22) vs connect-a-till/desktop/01]_
- J3 (9): The tax guidance says a store mixing pricing modes 'must account for the store-wide strategy' without specifying a supported configuration or verification procedure for that case. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, 'Why these pairings']_
- J4 (8): The quick start is ~6,700 px on desktop and ~10,350 px on phone with no image at all (its text file has no [image:] marker). Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' and 'Check it worked' describe the sign-in form and the synced order in prose only, so the reader never sees what success looks like. _[quick-start/desktop/03-04; quick-start/phone/07-08]_
- J5 (8): Step 8c asks for 'an existing single text ProductVariant custom field, such as barcode'. Adding one means a config change and another migration (connect-a-till: 'Then run a database migration'). Step 3 runs the migration earlier, so a developer who wants scanning has to go back and generate a second migration after the server is already set up. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8c 'how to add one' vs step 3; connect-a-till 'Enter your store details']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The first table on the home page tells a stranger that the demo sends usage events to 'ph.wcpos.com', a domain from a brand they have never heard of, and gives no explanation of who that is. For someone deciding in under a minute whether to trust and click 'Live demo', that unexplained third-party domain is the line most likely to make them hesitate. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row (home/phone/04)]_ Fix: Say who runs it in place, e.g. 'anonymous usage events to our own self-hosted analytics (ph.wcpos.com, run by the VendurePOS maintainers), with no cookie'. Or drop the hostname from the home page and keep it on the linked privacy section.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): When a till shows 'N till updates need attention', the shop owner lands on a troubleshooting block that mixes counter instructions, owner instructions and a developer errorCode/GraphQL list in one paragraph run. The how-to ('To find the line in the sync log') is collapsed, so the owner can't quickly see what to do at the counter versus what to send to their developer. _[troubleshooting/desktop/01 and troubleshooting/phone/01]_ Fix: Split the entry into three short labelled boxes: 'At the counter' (keep the tab open, keep selling), 'Store owner' (numbered steps to copy the sync-log line, expanded by default), and 'For your developer' (the errorCode list and GraphQL calls).
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The real-store refund procedure does not explicitly require returning card funds through the external terminal before recording the refund as settled. _[shots/plugin-setup.desktop.03.jpg; text/at-the-till.txt, 'Refund a sale']_ Fix: Add a card-refund step directing the operator to return funds through the external terminal or provider, then record settlement in Vendure using that refund reference. State explicitly that settleRefund records settlement and does not itself reverse the terminal payment.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The three till screenshots on At the till don't look like a set. They are three widths, all left-aligned (payment ~836 px full column, cashier sheet ~384 px, closure ~512 px). The closure shot has red and dark slivers bleeding under its border. Their figures disagree too (sheet €21.29 / €121.29 in the drawer, closure €120.09 counted), and the payment shot shows 'Tally Fixture Mug'. This is the only page that shows the till in use, and it looks assembled from leftover crops. _[at-the-till/desktop/02-04]_ Fix: Capture all three in one demo session with a real-looking product so the numbers follow from screen to screen. Crop them cleanly with no bleed, and render them at one consistent width (full column, or one shared max-width, centred), with phone-sized shots paired side by side if kept narrow.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site calls the till 'MIT licensed' and 'open-source', yet a client can only run it free through app.vendurepos.com, whose operator, cost, terms and availability are never stated. The self-hosting route needs an 'RxDB Premium licence token' that the site never identifies as a paid commercial licence. A sceptic advising a client cannot say what the till really depends on or what it will cost. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5; limitations.desktop.01 'Setting up the store' bullet 1]_ Fix: Add one 'Who runs app.vendurepos.com' line on the home page and in Connect a till: name the operator, say it is free and what happens if it goes away. Next to RXDB_PREMIUM, state that RxDB Premium is a paid third-party licence and link its pricing.
