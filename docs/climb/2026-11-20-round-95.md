## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero passes the five-second test, but the lower home page puts developer jargon in front of a newcomer: the comparison table's refund cell says 'The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls', and 'Before you start' lists 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12' and 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' with no plain-language summary of what a shop owner actually needs. _[home/desktop/02 ('Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start'); home/phone/04-05]_
- J2 (8): The hero says what it is and the 'Live demo / Quick start / For cashiers: At the till' row is clear, but nowhere on the home page (or the docs) does it say whether using the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com costs anything or who runs it; 'MIT licensed' in the footer covers the source, not the hosted service my staff would sign in to. _[home.desktop.01 hero and home.desktop.02 'Before you start' / footer; quick-start.txt 'The hosted POS is live at https://app.vendurepos.com']_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home desktop and phone slices]_
- J4 (9): The hero subcopy is a four-line centred paragraph ('VendurePOS is an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework. It is made for shops ... keeps selling when the connection drops.'). Under it come three CTAs plus a two-line note, so the eye has to get through about seven lines of grey text before reaching the screenshot. _[home.desktop.01 (hero, y≈260-500); home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero ('Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0', 'Point of sale for Vendure') never says who runs the project or whether the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free to use. A sceptic comparing it with paid POS options has to guess both. _[home.desktop.01 hero]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (7): The home page calls the till 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', then says building and hosting it yourself 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. It never says this is a paid third-party licence, or whether the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free, who runs it, or on what terms, so a newcomer can't tell what using it will really cost. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet; home/phone/04-05; connect-a-till text 'Where your data goes']_
- J2 (8): Limits are stated plainly everywhere (tab must stay open offline, unsent sales lost if browser data is cleared, no cash drawer, iPad untested), but the only operator named on the home page is an unexplained third-party domain: 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. Only Connect a till explains what ph.wcpos.com is, and no page says who stands behind app.vendurepos.com. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; connect-a-till.txt privacy lines]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The comparison table's 'Live demo' cell says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That is a domain from a different product, and the cell never says why. It is honest, but it reads as an unexplained third party inside a trust table. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J5 (7): No page names a maintainer, a support channel or an issue tracker. The footer has only 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' plus GitHub, npm, Limitations and Changelog, and the last plugin release (0.3.0) was 2026-10-06. The site also states 'MIT licensed' but leaves the cost of the required RxDB Premium licence (RXDB_PREMIUM) for self-hosting unstated. _[home.desktop.02 footer and 'Before you start'; changelog.phone.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A newcomer is told VendurePOS is 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', but the only self-host route 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token', and the site never says that licence is paid or whether the hosted app.vendurepos.com till is free, who runs it, and on what terms. The visitor can't tell what using it will cost before investing in setup. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start › Setting up the store'; home/phone/05]_ Fix: Add one plain sentence beside 'Before you start' (and in Overview) saying whether app.vendurepos.com is free to use, who operates it, and that RxDB Premium is a separate paid licence needed only to build the till yourself.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): A shop owner deciding whether staff can sell on it tomorrow cannot find out whether the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free, paid or best-effort, or who runs it. The site explains the plugin, the data path and every limit, but never the terms of the service the till runs on. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' (and quick-start.txt intro: 'The hosted POS is live at https://app.vendurepos.com')]_ Fix: Add one line under 'Before you start › At the counter' (and the quick start intro) saying who runs app.vendurepos.com, that it costs nothing (or what it costs), and that the till only talks to your own store.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): Developers arriving directly at the refund-settlement recipe are not pointed to the separate steps for returning card payments or recording cash payouts. _[shots/plugin-setup.desktop.03.jpg; text/plugin-setup.txt, 'Settle a Dashboard refund']_ Fix: Add a sentence explaining that API settlement records the refund, with a link to 'At the till → Refund a sale' for returning money and recording cash payouts.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): On At the till, the only docs page with screenshots, the three figures are set at three unrelated widths (full column, ~380 px centred, ~510 px centred). The narrow ones also carry extra empty space above them and left-hung captions, so the cashier guide's visual rhythm breaks at every figure. _[at-the-till.desktop.02-04]_ Fix: Put every till screenshot in the same figure frame: full content width with the card centred on a neutral backdrop, or one fixed max-width. Use the same top and bottom margins for all of them and centre each caption under its image.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site never says who maintains VendurePOS or where to get help or report a bug. There is no maintainer name, support channel or issue-tracker link in the footer, home or docs, and the plugin was last released 2026-10-06. A sceptic weighing this against a supported POS cannot tell whether anyone will answer. _[home.desktop.02 footer; all pages]_ Fix: Add one line to the home footer and the docs Overview. It should name the maintainer, link GitHub issues and a support channel, and say plainly whether app.vendurepos.com is free to use.
