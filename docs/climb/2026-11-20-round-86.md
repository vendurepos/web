## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but below it the home page switches to developer wording a newcomer cannot parse: the refund row says 'The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls', and 'Before you start' lists 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12' and 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. _[home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' table, 'Refund a sale at the till' row; 'Setting up the store' list); home.phone.04]_
- J2 (9): The hero and intro lean on developer framing ('headless commerce framework', plugin/npm talk) before saying in shop terms that two staff can sell at a counter tomorrow with a scanner and their own card terminal. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Hero stacks a 4-line grey centred intro and then a second 2-line grey note under the CTAs ('Your own store needs the plugin…') before the till image appears, so the eye meets two grey paragraphs of equal weight around the buttons _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01]_
- J5 (9): The hero badge reads "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0", which versions the server plugin, not the till a shop actually uses; the till's own version (0.1.0) appears only on the Changelog page, so the first screen understates how early the till is. _[home/desktop/01 hero badge; changelog/desktop/01 "till app version 0.1.0"]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The demo row says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie', but the site never says what wcpos.com is or how it relates to VendurePOS, so a stranger sees a different brand's domain getting their data. _[home.desktop.02, 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' row, Live demo column; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (9): The refund limit is stated honestly but in server terms: 'The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls; see Plugin setup.' A shop owner cannot tell whether that is a chore for them or for the freelancer. _[limitations.desktop.01 / text/limitations (Refunds)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): In the Yes/No comparison table the Refund row's 'Your Vendure store' cell is a 4-line paragraph ('No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls; see Plugin setup.'), which breaks the table's scan rhythm _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): The hero says "open-source till" and the footer says "MIT licensed", but self-hosting the till needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)". The site never says that licence is a paid commercial product or what it costs. It also never says who runs app.vendurepos.com or who is behind the project; the only hint is analytics going to an unexplained ph.wcpos.com. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet; limitations 'Setting up the store'; connect-a-till 'Where your data goes']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): For someone who arrived from a search and half knows Vendure, the home page drifts into server and API detail ('settles it with two Admin API calls', 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RXDB_PREMIUM', 'ph.wcpos.com'). That makes it look like a tool only for Vendure developers just when the visitor is deciding whether to read on or try the demo. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04 ('Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start')]_ Fix: On the home page, say each row in plain words (e.g. 'No, not yet: refund in the Vendure Dashboard'). Move the Admin API, Node range and RxDB token details into a single 'Requirements' link to the docs.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): Troubleshooting 'When till updates need attention' tells 'the store owner' to 'match its errorCode in the list below' and then lists raw codes (register_session_already_open, register_closure_number_invalid) next to needs_admin GraphQL operations. A non-developer owner gets a developer runbook in the middle of counter guidance. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 (and following slices)]_ Fix: Keep the counter steps ('leave the app open, don't reload, call the owner') on the page. Move the errorCode list and the GraphQL into a clearly labelled 'For whoever runs your Vendure server' block or page. Give the owner one plain sentence: copy the line from the sync log and send it to your developer.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Quick start's 'Vendure 3.6+' prerequisite is broader than Plugin setup's explicit support for Vendure 3.6 and later 3.x releases. _[text/quick-start.txt, prerequisites; text/plugin-setup.txt, Check your store; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start]_ Fix: Replace 'Vendure 3.6+' with 'Vendure 3.6 or a later 3.x release' in quick start and its machine-readable rendition, retaining the tested version.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The payment screenshot on At the till, the largest image in the docs, is mostly dead space: about 420 px of empty white cart pane between the Espresso Cup row and Subtotal _[at-the-till.desktop.02]_ Fix: Recapture the payment screen in a shorter browser window (about 760 px tall), or crop it so the cart pane ends just below the lines, and match its width to the other till screenshots
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells an "open-source", "MIT licensed" till, but building it yourself depends on an RxDB Premium licence. The site does not say this licence is paid, what it costs, or that the free path means relying on a hosted app.vendurepos.com whose operator, terms and longevity are never stated. That is the first thing I would raise with a client before recommending it. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' bullet 4; connect-a-till 'Where your data goes'; footer]_ Fix: In that bullet, add one sentence saying RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence, with a link to its pricing. Add a short line naming who runs app.vendurepos.com and on what terms (free, no account, data goes only to your store).
