## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the home page's 'Before you start' list hits a newcomer with unexplained jargon: 'you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', 'one tab per till and not a private window', 'Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome', all before they know whether they want to try it. _[home desktop slice 02 / phone slice 05, 'Before you start' bullets 4-5]_
- J2 (8): The hero speaks to the developer (Vendure plugin, browser till) and does not say in plain words what my two staff need at the counter (a browser, scanner, card terminal kept separate) until 'Before you start' further down. _[home.desktop.01 (hero)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero lead runs to four centred lines (seven on phone) because it stops to define Vendure in a parenthetical, '(Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework)', so the one-sentence promise is buried in a grey paragraph between the H1 and the buttons. _[home.desktop.01 (hero paragraph under 'Point of sale for Vendure'); home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The home page says what VendurePOS is and offers 'Live demo' / 'Quick start', but never says who it is not for or how it differs from Shopify POS, Square or a hosted till. A buyer has to read the 'Before you start' bullets and Limitations to work out that a shop needing card-terminal integration, a cash drawer or refunds at the till is not the target. _[home/desktop/01-02 (hero and 'Before you start')]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor never learns who is behind VendurePOS. The only hint is an unexplained third-party domain, 'ph.wcpos.com', in the home comparison table, next to footer mentions of TallyUI and MedusaPOS. That makes 'should I trust this with my store?' harder to answer than it needs to be. _[home desktop slice 02 / phone slice 04, 'Live demo or your own store' table last row; footer]_ Fix: Name the maker once in the footer or hero (e.g. 'Made by the WCPOS team'), and in the table say 'to ph.wcpos.com, our self-hosted analytics server'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): When the till says 'N till updates need attention', the troubleshooting page hands the shop owner a developer procedure: open Settings → Sync log → All, copy a DEBUG 'Register command result … status=rejected errorCode=…' line before reloading, and accept that for 'Any other code' the day's closure and cash records never reach Vendure. Nothing says plainly what staff should do right now. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 ('Till updates' / 'N till updates need attention' section)]_ Fix: Open the section with a three-line 'What to do at the counter' box: keep selling, print and keep the Z report or count sheet, message your developer. Move the sync-log, errorCode and reload detail into a collapsed 'For your developer' block underneath.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with channels using both inclusive and exclusive pricing must guess how to satisfy the conflicting recommendations for the store-wide order tax strategy. _[Quick start, step 6 and 'Why these pairings', also reproduced in text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed-pricing channels, or explicitly identify that configuration as unsupported or unverified. Include a concrete check of receipt and Vendure tax totals for each pricing mode.
- **J4 Designer** (C3): On a very long quick start (9 steps, 6342 px desktop / 9946 px phone), the 'what success looks like' check is a bold run-in paragraph under a lettered a–i list in step 8. It has no heading or anchor, so a developer scanning the TOC or the phone section dropdown cannot jump to the one place that says the till worked. _[quick-start.desktop.03 / quick-start.phone.08 ('Check it worked.')]_ Fix: Make 'Check it worked' its own H3 (or an H2 between steps 8 and 9) so it appears in the TOC and phone dropdown. Present its five checks as a visually distinct checklist callout.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A client being advised whether to run a till that handles their takings cannot find out who maintains VendurePOS, where to get support, or whether it is actively developed. No maintainer or support channel is named, 'ph.wcpos.com' appears in the home table unexplained, and the last plugin release (0.3.0, 2026-10-06) ships a known permission gap that the docs tell users to patch by hand. _[home/desktop/02 (Live demo table and footer); changelog/desktop/01; quick-start.txt 'Optional: a device key for each till']_ Fix: Add a short 'Who builds this' line to the home footer and the Overview. It should name the maintainer (and its relation to wcpos.com), link a support or issues channel, and state the release cadence or the date of the next plugin release that fixes the ReadOrder role gap.
