## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero's one long paragraph closes on 'every sale lands in Vendure once, as a normal order at the till's price', which a visitor who barely knows Vendure cannot decode in five seconds. The first thing above the headline is 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0', a version number for a plugin that hasn't been introduced yet. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01, hero badge and paragraph]_
- J2 (8): The hero paragraph spends its middle explaining what Vendure is ('Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework') rather than telling a shop owner that staff sell at the counter with a scanner and card terminal; on phone the paragraph runs seven lines before the buttons, and the Docs link is hidden behind the header chevron. _[home/phone/01 (hero paragraph, header chevron)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; text/home.txt]_
- J4 (9): The hero lede runs four centred lines including a parenthetical definition of Vendure ("(Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework)"), and below the buttons sits a second, two-link sentence ("the quick start sets it up. Then try the till with your store"). At 390 px that makes seven lines of grey centred text before the screenshot. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, hero]_
- J5 (8): Five seconds tell you what it is and where to click, but the home page never says who builds or maintains VendurePOS and has no 'who this is not for' or comparison line (e.g. versus a hosted Shopify/Square till). A buyer comparing tills has to infer the fit from the Limitations list. _[home/desktop/01-02 (hero and 'Before you start')]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', but the home page never says what WCPOS is or how it relates to VendurePOS, so a newcomer sees an unexplained third-party domain receiving data. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (8): Limitations says 'Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome; other browsers are not tested yet', yet At the till describes narrow-screen tabs and camera scanning as if tablets and phones are fine, and the home page never says which device to put on the counter. Separately, the payment screenshot shows a test-fixture product called 'Tally Fixture Mug'. _[limitations/desktop/01 vs at-the-till/desktop/01 'On a narrow screen...' and at-the-till/desktop/02 screenshot]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): On a vendurepos.com page, the comparison table's demo cell says "All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com". The wcpos.com domain is never explained on the home page, so a careful reader is left wondering who that third party is. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J5 (7): The home page sells an 'open-source till' and its footer says 'MIT licensed', but building the till yourself 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM'. That paid dependency appears only on Limitations and Connect a till, so in practice you depend on the hosted app.vendurepos.com, which the home page never says. _[home/desktop/02 footer vs limitations/desktop/01 bullet 3 and connect-a-till text]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The 'Quick start' runs to nine steps over about 6,100 px. A long 'How POS sales take stock' paragraph (stockLocationStrategy, PaymentSettled, per-sale locationId) and a pre-sale warning that sends you to two other pages ('Set up email', 'Check your own code') sit between you and the first sale, so the fastest route to a working till is hard to pick out. _[quick-start/desktop/03, steps 7-8; quick-start/phone/06-07]_
- J2 (8): Quick start step 8a signs the till in with 'your administrator email and password'. Giving each cashier their own limited account (the vendurepos-pos-till role, Hand over) is only on Connect a till, and step 8 does not link it. A developer following the quick start would hand staff the admin login. _[quick-start/desktop/03 step 8a; connect-a-till 'Give each cashier their own account']_
- J3 (9): The quick start omits the Node requirement (^20.19 || >=22.12) stated in Plugin setup, so its opening prerequisites are incomplete. _[text/quick-start.txt, opening prerequisites; text/plugin-setup.txt, Check your store]_
- J4 (8): Step 8 says the same thing twice. Sub-step h ("check Vendure for the POS prices, settled tally-pos payment, delivered fulfilment, stock reduction and tallySaleAt") is followed straight away by a 'Check it worked' list of the same five checks. Meanwhile sub-step i, the offline test, is buried among the a–i sale steps. _[quick-start.desktop.03, step 8]_
- J5 (8): Connect a till sends you to 'System → API keys → New POS till key', then a few paragraphs later says to check that 'Settings → API keys shows New POS till key'. Two menu paths for one button make the reader guess, on top of having to add ReadOrder by hand because plugin 0.3.0's own role leaves it out. _[connect-a-till text, 'Create and use a device key' and 'Make the key button available in a pnpm project']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor reading the demo comparison is told their activity goes to 'ph.wcpos.com', a domain belonging to an unnamed third party (WCPOS) never mentioned anywhere else on the home page. For someone deciding whether to trust an unknown pre-release POS, that is the one line that looks like it's hiding something. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04, 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' row, Live demo column]_ Fix: Name the owner in that cell, for example 'to our own self-hosted PostHog (ph.wcpos.com, run by the WCPOS team who make VendurePOS)', or add one footer line saying who makes VendurePOS.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): A customer return is an everyday counter job, and the cashier page does not say what staff do on a real store running plugin 0.3.0. 'Refund a sale' gives till steps that only work on the demo. The real path is a Dashboard refund that stays Pending until someone runs a GraphQL settleRefund mutation. Nothing tells the cashier how to hand back cash and keep the drawer and Z report right. _[at-the-till/desktop/02-03 ('Refund a sale', 'Settle a Dashboard refund')]_ Fix: Open 'Refund a sale' with a short 'On your store (plugin 0.3.0)' box for the cashier: refund in the Dashboard, then record the cash handed back with Pay out and the order code as the reason. Move the demo-only till steps and the settleRefund GraphQL to a collapsed section or to Troubleshooting for the developer.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The device-key guide gives two different Dashboard navigation paths for the same API keys page. _[shots/connect-a-till.desktop.02.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:341 and :376]_ Fix: Use the correct navigation path for the documented Vendure version in both steps, and keep the machine-readable export consistent.
- **J4 Designer** (C4): The last step of the most important page is the hardest to scan. Quick start '9. Troubleshoot' packs each failure (CORS, 401, missing plugin) into one 4–6 line bullet that mixes quoted till messages, env vars and CSP terms. At 390 px it fills about two screens of unbroken grey text, so a developer whose first sale failed can't spot their error message. _[quick-start.desktop.03-04 / quick-start.phone.08-09, section 9]_ Fix: Restyle section 9 as a symptom → fix list: each item starts with the exact till message (or HTTP status) as a bold or callout title, followed by one or two short fix lines. Move the LAN-http/CSP aside to Troubleshooting.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The 'open-source, MIT licensed' pitch on the home page hides the fact that building and self-hosting the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence. A client is therefore tied to the hosted app.vendurepos.com, and the home page says nothing about who runs that host or what it commits to. A sceptic who finds this on Limitations stops trusting the rest of the pitch. _[home/desktop/02 footer and 'Before you start' vs limitations/desktop/01 ('Building the till yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token')]_ Fix: Add a 'Before you start' bullet on the home page saying the plugin and till source are MIT, that self-building the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence, and that most stores use the hosted app.vendurepos.com. Link it to a line on who runs that host.
