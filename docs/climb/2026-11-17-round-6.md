## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.0)
- J1 (7): The home page never says what Vendure is: the hero says the till "signs in to your own Vendure store", and nothing on the page explains Vendure or links to it. The only link to Vendure is on the docs Overview. On top of that, four near-equal buttons (Live demo / Quick start / Try with your store / GitHub) leave a newcomer unsure whether "Try with your store" or "Quick start" comes first. _[home/desktop/01 hero and button row; home/phone/01]_
- J2 (8): The hero offers four equal-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), and 'Try with your store' opens the app even though the line under the buttons says your store needs the plugin first, so a shop owner can't tell which one is theirs. On the phone, the till screenshot below is shrunk so far that product names and prices can't be read. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons; home.phone.01 hero screenshot]_
- J3 (9): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero offers four calls to action: 'Live demo' is solid, but 'Quick start', 'Try with your store' and 'GitHub' are three ghost buttons of equal weight. 'Try with your store' then needs the caption below to explain that it won't work until the plugin is installed, so the next click is less obvious than it should be. _[home/desktop/01 hero button row; home/phone/01]_
- J5 (8): Home never says who should NOT use it or how it compares to Shopify POS / Square / Lightspeed; a client weighing tills gets no 'not for you if' section, and the hero spreads weight across four equal CTAs _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor arriving from a 'Vendure point of sale' search who only half knows Vendure gets no one-line explanation or link on the home page. The whole pitch ("signs in to your own Vendure store", "lands in Vendure once, as a normal order") assumes the visitor already runs Vendure. With four equal-weight buttons and no explanation, a newcomer can't tell whether this is for them. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 hero paragraph]_ Fix: In the hero sentence, call Vendure "the open-source TypeScript commerce framework" and link it to vendure.io. Make 'Live demo' the single primary button, with 'Quick start' as the only secondary one.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The home page's 'Live demo or your own store' table is where a shop owner checks what their own store gets, and it leaves out refunds. The demo refunds at the till; a real store on the released plugin 0.3.0 can't, and Dashboard refunds stay 'Pending until someone settles it by hand'. A shop owner who tries the demo will think staff can take returns tomorrow. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.03-04 comparison table; text/at-the-till.txt 'Refund a sale']_ Fix: Add a table row 'Refund a sale at the till | Yes | Not yet: needs the plugin release after 0.3.0; refund in the Vendure Dashboard and settle it there'. Link it to a short how-to for settling that Pending refund.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The needs_admin recovery instructions leave the developer to decide whether a partially recorded sale should be marked applied or rejected without a reconciliation procedure. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, Resolve a sale the store kept part-way; shots/troubleshooting.desktop.01.jpg and shots/troubleshooting.desktop.02.jpg]_ Fix: Add a short decision procedure identifying the order, payment and stock state to inspect before choosing either resolution. Include the listing query and the expected state after resolution and any retry.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a phone, the home page's only product visual is the desktop till scaled down to 390 px. Every label, price and total is unreadably small, so the first thing a phone visitor sees as proof is a grey-and-orange blur, and below it the comparison table and code blocks are squeezed in the same way. _[home/phone/01 ('A sale in progress on the live demo till.')]_ Fix: Use a phone-specific image (<picture> with a media query) that crops the screenshot to the cart and checkout panel, or uses the till's narrow layout, at about 2x so text stays at least 11 px on screen.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Privacy claim contradicted: home table says of the demo "nothing leaves the page" while Connect a till says it "posts anonymous demo events to ph.wcpos.com"; together with "MIT licensed" hiding the RxDB Premium licence needed to self-build, a sceptic stops trusting the honesty claims _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04 vs connect-a-till.desktop.01]_ Fix: Change the home cell to "No: the demo store lives in your browser; only anonymous usage events go to ph.wcpos.com", and add a Limitations bullet plus a footer note that building the till yourself needs a paid RxDB Premium licence
