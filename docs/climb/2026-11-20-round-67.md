## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero passes the five-second test, but its first explanation leans on jargon a half-aware visitor cannot decode: "Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework", topped by a "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0" badge that names a plugin before the page has said what one is for. _[home/desktop/01 hero (same on home/phone/01)]_
- J2 (8): The lead under the hero speaks to developers before shop owners: "VendurePOS is an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework"; a shop owner has to read on to "made for shops that sell online with Vendure and also sell in person" to see it is for them. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 (text/home.txt line 12)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero carries a four-line centred paragraph plus a second small-print line under the buttons ("Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first..."). That makes two text blocks around the CTAs, and the top-nav label "At the till" means nothing to a first-time visitor. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 (hero)]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and who it is for, but nothing on the home page tells a buyer who should NOT pick it or how it differs from Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed. The disqualifiers (desktop Chrome only, no cash drawer, no card-terminal link, refunds outside the till, Postgres only) are scattered across 'Before you start' bullets and the demo table instead of one plain 'Not for you if…' line. _[home.desktop.01 hero / home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The home page calls the till "open-source" and the footer says "MIT licensed", yet 'Setting up the store' says self-hosting needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)" without saying what RxDB Premium is or whether it costs money, so the catch is stated but not explained. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' > 'Setting up the store', last bullet; home/phone/05]_
- J2 (9): None that hurts trust. The limits are stated plainly (tab must stay open offline, clearing browser data loses unsent sales, refunds stay Pending on plugin 0.3.0, only desktop Chrome tested). The one loose end is the changelog line "Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder", which is honest but sits only on the changelog page, not in Limitations. _[changelog/desktop+phone]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The comparison table says the demo's only traffic is "anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com". That is a domain from another brand, never explained anywhere on a VendurePOS site, so a reader is left asking who runs this. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor is told the till is open source and MIT licensed. The same page then says self-hosting needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)", with no word on what that is or whether it is paid. The visitor either skims past a cost they don't know about or stops trusting the 'open-source' claim. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store', last bullet (also connect-a-till and limitations 'Setting up the store')]_ Fix: In that bullet, say in plain words that RxDB Premium is a commercial database licence from a third party and that you need it only if you build the till yourself, not if you use app.vendurepos.com, with a link to its pricing.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): The refund flow is the one everyday counter task that the site leaves the shop without a procedure for. At the till it only says "Ask whoever manages your Vendure store to refund the order in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund then shows as Pending until they settle it", so a two-person shop does not know what to do while the customer is waiting. _[at-the-till/desktop/02, at-the-till/phone/05]_ Fix: Open the "Refund a sale" section with a short "Customer wants money back now" box: what staff do at the counter (refund on the card terminal or hand back cash, write the order number down), and say that the Dashboard refund and settle are a back-office follow-up for whoever runs the store.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the required store-wide tax strategy from the quick start. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Document the supported strategy and verification steps for mixed pricing modes, or explicitly state that this configuration is unsupported and identify the prerequisite for proceeding.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): In the home page's 'What it does today' grid, the nine plain features sit in two columns, so 'Custom items, fees and shipping' hangs alone on the last row with an empty right-hand cell. On the page every visitor sees first, this is the one visible layout fault. It repeats in dark mode. _[home/desktop/02 (top); home.desktop-dark.jpg]_ Fix: Lay the nine unboxed features out in a 3-column grid at desktop widths so they fill a 3x3 block, or drop or merge one item so the 2-column grid ends on an even count.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Nothing on the site shows the project is maintained or supported. There is no maintainer, no issue tracker or support link, and no roadmap to 1.0. The only plugin dates (three releases between 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-06, nothing since) sit beside known defects that the docs work around instead of fixing or scheduling: the auto-created till role missing ReadOrder, and Dashboard refunds stuck Pending until someone hand-writes two GraphQL calls. The changelog even says that role 'does everything a till does' in the sentence before admitting it does not. I would not advise a client to rely on that without asking who answers when it breaks. _[changelog.desktop.01 (0.2.0 last bullet); home.desktop.02 refund row; footer of home.desktop.02]_ Fix: Add a short 'Status and support' block to the home page and Overview: who maintains it, where to file issues, and what the next plugin release fixes (ReadOrder in the till role, refunds at the till). Delete 'does everything a till does' from the changelog bullet.
