## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The hero's main button 'Live demo' does not say the demo needs no sign-up or install; the reassuring line ('a simulated store in your browser, with nothing to install') only appears in the third section, 'Live demo or your own store'. Meanwhile the badge 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' names a plugin the visitor hasn't heard of yet. _[home/desktop/01 hero and badge; home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store']_
- J2 (9): The hero promises it "keeps selling when the connection drops" without the keep-the-tab-open condition, which only appears in the first feature card below; and on phone the header shows only "Docs" and "Live demo", so the cashier page "At the till" is hidden behind the menu. _[home.desktop.01 hero / home.phone.01 header]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): On phone the H1 wraps as "Point of sale for / Vendure", leaving "Vendure" alone on the second line, and on desktop the centred four-line hero paragraph ends on a short orphan line "connection drops." _[home.phone.01; home.desktop.01]_
- J5 (9): On the phone the hero image crops the till to two product cards (Canvas Apron, Chocolate Biscuits), so the cart and the EUR 20.09 checkout that the caption 'A sale in progress' promises are not visible. _[home/phone/01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The home page never says who makes or maintains VendurePOS (the footer has only 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.'), yet the demo row says it 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a third domain that home never explains. A newcomer can't tell whose analytics that is. _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' and footer]_
- J2 (9): For an own store, refunds are "Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", stated as a bare fact with no plain line that a non-developer owner will need their developer for every refund. _[home (live demo vs own store table, Refund row) / limitations.desktop.01]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The comparison table's "Refund a sale at the till" own-store cell is a four-line caveat ("No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls…"). It is the densest cell on the page and buries the limitation in small table text. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): The home page and footer say 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', but self-hosting the till needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. No page says this is a paid commercial licence, what it costs, or who runs app.vendurepos.com. So in practice a shop depends on an unnamed hosted app. Also, the 'Live demo or your own store' table says 'This is what it shows today' but leaves out that the Customer note works only on the demo (At the till: 'On plugin 0.3.0, Order details has no customer note'). _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' bullet and the demo-vs-store table; connect-a-till/desktop/01; at-the-till text 'Order details']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Before step 1, the box 'Before a sale on a live store' tells you to 'swap its order-confirmation handler first: see Set up email' and to read 'Check your own code', both on another page, without saying what goes wrong if you skip them. The first-run path forks before it starts. _[quick-start/desktop/01 warning callout]_
- J2 (8): No recommended counter device: "tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet" and tests "run in desktop Chrome" leave the owner guessing what hardware to put on the counter tomorrow. _[home 'Before you start' / limitations.desktop.01]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 prescribes different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but tells mixed-mode stores only to 'account for the store-wide strategy' without explaining what to configure. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:846-875; Quick start, step 6 and 'Why these pairings']_
- J4 (8): Step 2's H2 embeds a code chip, "2. Add `TallyPosPlugin` to your existing Vendure config", which wraps to three lines on phone, and the plugin-name mismatch then has to be explained in prose. _[quick-start.phone.02]_
- J5 (8): The plugin's own 'New POS till key' role (vendurepos-pos-till) ships without ReadOrder on 0.3.0. The one-click device-key path therefore leaves the till's order history refused unless the reader finds the manual fix. That fix is repeated as a caveat in four places instead of being fixed in the plugin. _[quick-start 'Optional: a device key for each till'; connect-a-till 'Create and use a device key'; changelog/desktop/01 0.2.0 'Not in the release notes']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor can't find out who is behind VendurePOS. The home page names no maker or maintainer, and the only hint is an unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' analytics domain in the demo comparison table, which looks odd rather than reassuring on a site the visitor is deciding whether to trust. _[home/desktop/02 demo table row and footer]_ Fix: Add one footer line naming who builds and maintains VendurePOS, with a link. In the demo row, say that ph.wcpos.com is that maintainer's self-hosted analytics, as the Connect a till page already does.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): The docs Overview 'Next steps' offers only developer setup (Quick start, Plugin setup, GitHub) and no At the till or Limitations card, so a shop owner opening Docs to learn how staff sell, ride out an outage and close the day is not shown where that lives. _[docs.desktop.01 / text/docs.txt 'Next steps']_ Fix: Add an 'At the till' card (open the register, take payment, close the day) and a 'Limitations' card to the Overview Next steps.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess which store-wide order tax strategy to configure. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:846-875]_ Fix: Add explicit guidance for mixed-mode stores, including the supported strategy choice and how to verify totals in each pricing mode. If that configuration is unsupported, state the restriction directly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): Home "What it does today" mixes two bordered cards with eight unboxed text items in one 2-column grid, and the shorter "Every sale lands once" card leaves an empty band above its button, so the section reads as two unrelated components. _[home.desktop.01 (and home.phone.02)]_ Fix: Give all ten items one treatment: either equal-height cards throughout, or the two proof items as one full-width callout band above a plain list. Place each card's button directly under its text instead of bottom-aligning it.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site leads with 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', yet building and hosting the till yourself needs an RxDB Premium licence. The site never says this is a paid third-party licence or what it costs, and never says who operates app.vendurepos.com. So a client cannot judge lock-in or what self-hosting would cost, and I would advise caution until that is clear. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet; limitations/desktop/01 'Setting up the store'; connect-a-till/desktop/01]_ Fix: Say plainly in that bullet and in Limitations that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence (with a link to its price), and add one line naming who runs app.vendurepos.com and how it is kept up.
