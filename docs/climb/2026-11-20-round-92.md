## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C3): A first-time visitor who follows the quick start's own advice ('npx @vendure/create my-shop') ends up with a store on http://localhost. Step 8 never says what store URL to type for it, and step 9 only says 'Use HTTPS for a remote store'. They have to guess whether the hosted https till can reach their local store, and the answer is only on Troubleshooting. _[quick-start desktop slices 03-04, step 8a and step 9 / phone slices 07, 09]_ Fix: In step 8a, add: 'Trying it on a local store? Enter http://localhost:3000; the hosted till allows http://localhost and http://127.0.0.1 for development.' In step 5, either drop the localhost:8081 example or say that it is only for a self-built till.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): The troubleshooting page puts counter-facing notices on the same page as developer material (errorCode strings like "register_session_already_open", tallyResolveNeedsAdmin GraphQL blocks, VENDUREPOS_WEB_ALLOW_LAN_HTTP). A shop owner reading it on a phone hits "Those closures and cash records do not reach your Vendure store from this till" and gets no plain step for what staff should do next. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01-03 / troubleshooting.phone, 'When till updates need attention']_ Fix: Split the page in two. 'At the counter' keeps each notice with one plain action (e.g. 'keep the printed Z report and tell whoever runs your store'). 'For whoever runs your Vendure server' holds the errorCodes, the GraphQL and the LAN http note.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to reconcile the recommended tax strategies with the single store-wide setting. _[Quick start, step 6, shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:226-228]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and how to verify its totals, or explicitly identify this configuration as unsupported.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The only screenshot of the payment flow, the core moment of a POS, is a wide capture whose cart half is about 420 px of empty white between three line items and the totals. The figure looks unfinished and the keypad, which is the subject, gets only half the width. _[at-the-till.desktop.02]_ Fix: Recapture the payment screen at a narrower till viewport (about 1000 px) or with five or six cart lines, so the cart pane fills. Alternatively crop the figure to the payment pane plus totals.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A client cannot tell whether this project is alive or supported: three plugin releases all between 1 and 6 Oct 2026 (0.3.0 "Released the same day as 0.2.0, a few hours later"), nothing since, no named maintainer or support channel, and the 'MIT licensed' self-host path depends on a paid RxDB Premium token whose cost is not stated. _[changelog.desktop.01; home.desktop.02]_ Fix: Add a short 'Project status' block on home: who maintains it, latest release and latest commit dates, where to ask for help or file issues, and what the RxDB Premium licence costs; plus one 'Not for you if…' line (e.g. you need refunds at the till, a cash drawer, iPad tills or a non-Postgres store — use Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed instead).
