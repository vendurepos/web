## Worst defect per judge and criterion

Further down the home page, the 'Before you start' section and the demo table switch to developer jargon a half-informed visitor can't parse: "Node ^20.19 || >=22.12", "you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)", and "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". _[home desktop 02; home phone 04-05]_
- J2 (8): The first sentence a shop owner reads explains the product with developer jargon: "an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework"; and on the phone the top bar shows only "Docs" and "Live demo", so the owner's own page "At the till" is hidden behind the chevron. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 (text/home.txt line 12)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): On phone the top bar shows only 'VendurePOS', 'Docs' and 'Live demo' (plus search and a chevron), so Quick start, the second hero action, is not in the nav. The phone hero crop of the till shows only two product cards (Canvas Apron, Chocolate Biscuits) and cuts the category chips at 'Gif'. _[home.phone.01]_
- J5 (9): Hero says "Point of sale for Vendure" with a pre-release badge and clear CTAs, but nowhere says who should not use it or how it differs from Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed or a Vendure-native alternative; a buyer comparing tills gets no positioning. _[home.desktop.01 (hero) and home.desktop.02-03]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The home page never says whether VendurePOS, or the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com, costs anything, or who runs it. It does mention needing "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)" to self-host, and that demo usage goes to an unexplained "ph.wcpos.com". A first-time visitor can't tell whether this is free, paid, or tied to some other company. _[home desktop 02 / phone 04-05 ('Live demo or your own store', 'Before you start')]_ Fix: Add one plain sentence by the hero CTAs or at the top of 'Before you start', such as: "The plugin is MIT-licensed and the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free; self-hosting the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence." Add a short note that VendurePOS is made by the WCPOS team, which explains ph.wcpos.com.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): On plugin 0.3.0 a customer refund cannot be done at the till: the owner must refund in the Vendure Dashboard, where "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", then record a Pay out and refund the card on the terminal; the site never says in plain words who in a small shop does the API step, while the demo shows refunds at the till as "Yes". _[home.phone.04 / home.desktop.02 (table row "Refund a sale at the till"); at-the-till (Refund a sale)]_ Fix: In the refund row and the At the till refund section, add one sentence saying the settle step is a one-time job for your developer (or link a ready script), and link straight to Plugin setup's "Settle a Dashboard refund" anchor instead of the page.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess which store-wide order tax strategy to configure. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Add explicit guidance for mixed-pricing channels: document a supported configuration and verification example, or state that this combination is not covered by the setup instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The product screenshots, the site's main visual proof, have an empty white band in the cart pane between the line items or 'Add charge' and the Subtotal (about 420 px on the payment shot), so the till looks half-empty and unpolished in the hero and on At the till. _[home.desktop.01 (hero screenshot); at-the-till.desktop.02]_ Fix: Recapture the till screenshots at a shorter viewport height, or with more cart lines, so the cart pane fills; or crop the empty band out of the cart column.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): "MIT licensed" till whose self-host path requires "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)" — a paid proprietary dependency with no price or terms given, which a sceptic would flag to a client as a hidden cost and lock-in to app.vendurepos.com. _[home.desktop.02 (Setting up the store); footer "MIT licensed"]_ Fix: Next to "MIT licensed" and on the self-host instructions, state plainly that self-hosting needs a paid RxDB Premium licence, what it costs or link its pricing, and that app.vendurepos.com is the free alternative; add a short who-maintains-it / support line.
