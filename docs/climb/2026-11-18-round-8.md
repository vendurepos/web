## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero says 'Point of sale for Vendure' and 'signs in to your own Vendure store' but never says what Vendure is or links to it (only the docs Overview links vendure.io), and the first feature cards then lean on jargon a newcomer can't parse: 'idempotency ledger', 'Keyboard-wedge scanners', 'counted float', 'Z report'. _[home.desktop.01 hero paragraph and 'What it does today' cards; home.phone.01-02]_
- J2 (8): The hero has four buttons side by side ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), and 'Try with your store' opens app.vendurepos.com, which the line under it says will not work until the plugin is installed. A shop owner can't tell which button is theirs. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons; home.phone.01 (two rows of buttons)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero has four buttons where three ('Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub') share the same outlined style and weight, so the second step reads as a three-way tie; at 390 px they wrap into a ragged, centred 2+2 block of mismatched widths ('Live demo | Quick start' over 'Try with your store | GitHub'). _[home.desktop.01 hero button row; home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub). 'Try with your store' sends a visitor to app.vendurepos.com, which cannot work until the plugin is installed, so a small caption under the buttons has to warn them off. Nothing on the page says who should not use it, for example shops on MySQL, shops that need integrated card terminals or native apps, or anyone needing a stable 1.0. _[home/desktop/01 hero buttons and caption 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a different brand's domain with no explanation on the home page (only Connect a till explains it is a self-hosted PostHog), which makes a newcomer wonder who else is involved; refunds and customer notes are also promised for 'the plugin release after 0.3.0' with no date while the changelog's latest release is 2026-10-06. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; changelog.desktop.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who only half knows Vendure is never told what it is on the home page: the hero assumes it, nothing links to it, and the first feature cards switch to developer jargon ('idempotency ledger', 'Keyboard-wedge'), so within a minute the reader can't tell if this is for them. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero paragraph and first two feature cards]_ Fix: Make the hero sentence say 'for Vendure, the open-source TypeScript commerce platform' with Vendure linked to vendure.io, and rewrite 'idempotency ledger' and 'Keyboard-wedge scanners' in plain words (e.g. 'a resent sale is recognised and never duplicated', 'USB barcode scanners').
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): 'At the till', the page an owner would hand to staff, has no screenshots of the till at all. It is 5119 px on desktop and 6984 px on phone of text that points at controls by description ('The ⋮ beside Checkout', 'Register › in the bar', 'Tap your name in the bar for your cashier sheet', 'Close register in your cashier sheet'). A cashier can't match those words to the screen, and the owner can't check from the page how closing the day and the Z report look before trusting their counter to it. _[at-the-till.desktop.01-03; at-the-till.phone.01-07]_ Fix: Add four or five annotated screenshots from the live demo to At the till: opening the register, the payment pane with a split payment, the receipt, the Register panel, and the close-register count with its Z report.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): A developer handling a needs_admin sale must guess which existing order state warrants applied versus rejected; the example supplies the mutation but not the recovery decision. _[shots/troubleshooting.desktop.02.jpg, 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way']_ Fix: Add the checks required before choosing each resolution and explain the resulting order and retry behavior. Include a complete tallyNeedsAdminCommands query showing how to obtain the command ID and relevant state.
- **J4 Designer** (C4): The docs never show the product they describe. At the till, Connect a till and Troubleshooting are long walls of prose that name UI ('Order details', 'Hand over', 'Parked (n)', header notices) without one screenshot, and 'Where your data goes' is a single 14-line paragraph. The site looks careful but reads as a spec, especially at 390 px. _[at-the-till.desktop.01 / phone.01-07; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where your data goes']_ Fix: Add one annotated till screenshot each to At the till (cart and keypad, payment pane) and Troubleshooting (header sync notice). Split 'Where your data goes' into a short list: what is stored, what is sent where, and what sign-out deletes.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): 'Open-source' and 'MIT licensed' are the home page's trust claims, but self-hosting the till requires a commercial RxDB Premium licence, and that is disclosed only deep in Limitations and Connect a till. The real choice is to pay a third party or to enter a Vendure administrator password on app.vendurepos.com. A sceptic who finds that later concludes the home page was hiding it. _[home/desktop/02 footer 'MIT licensed' and 'Before you start' vs limitations/desktop/01 bullet 3 and connect-a-till/desktop/01 'The till's source is MIT-licensed…']_ Fix: Add one bullet to the home page's 'Before you start': 'Use the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com (sign-in goes only to your store; see Where your data goes), or build it yourself with a commercial RxDB Premium licence.' Link it to Connect a till.
