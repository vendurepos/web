## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The second path has three names that go to two places: the nav says 'Connect your store' (goes to /docs/connect-a-till, the till sign-in page), the hero button says 'Quick start' (plugin install), and the line under it says 'try the till with your store' (goes straight to app.vendurepos.com). A newcomer can't tell which one to click first, and the nav item skips the plugin step. _[home.desktop.01 header nav and hero CTA row; tech.json home.desktop links]_
- J2 (8): The hero and both buttons speak to the developer ("Live demo", "Quick start", "signs in to your own Vendure store"); the cashier guide a shop owner wants, At the till, is linked from the home page only in the last paragraph above the footer ("For cashiers, At the till covers selling...") and is not in the top nav. _[home/desktop/02 (bottom paragraph), home/phone/05; top nav home/desktop/01]_
- J3 (9): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero till screenshot is cut off at its bottom edge through a fourth row of product tiles (half-icons in mustard/red visible under Filter Coffee…Leather Keyring, and the 'Guest · 3 items' bar clipped), so the first image on the page looks unfinished rather than framed. _[home/desktop/01, hero image bottom edge y≈1135-1155]_
- J5 (8): The hero tells me what it is and offers two clear buttons, but nowhere on the home page does it say who maintains it or who should not use it yet (for example a shop needing a cash drawer, integrated card terminal or iPad till). The only maturity signal is the small 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' pill, and the footer says just 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.desktop.02 footer]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The hero calls VendurePOS an 'open-source till'. Only the fifth bullet of 'Before you start' says that building it yourself needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', a paid third-party licence, and it never says that this costs money. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5; home.phone.05]_
- J2 (8): The home card "Open and close the register" promises a clean Z-report day close, but Troubleshooting admits a refused till update "is never sent again" and for "Any other code: the till has no retry or skip"; the home page and Limitations never mention that register records can get stuck on the till. _[home/desktop/01 card vs troubleshooting/desktop/01 'Till updates line'; limitations/desktop/01]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The comparison table's demo cell says it sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' with no word on the home page about what wcpos.com is, so a third-party-looking domain appears unexplained on a VendurePOS page (the explanation only lives on Connect a till). _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J5 (7): Troubleshooting admits a dead end: for a refused till update with 'Any other code: the till has no retry or skip, and fixing the store does not make it send the update again. The Limitations page, which is meant to list what it does not do, never mentions this register-data trap, so the honest-looking limits list is incomplete. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 'Till updates line'; limitations.desktop.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor gets three differently named next steps for 'use it with my store'. The nav's 'Connect your store' goes to the till sign-in docs, which assume the plugin is already installed. 'try the till with your store' goes straight to the app's sign-in. 'Quick start' is the real first step. It is unclear what to click after the demo, and two of the three skip the plugin install. _[home.desktop.01 header nav and line under the hero buttons; tech.json home.desktop links (/docs/connect-a-till, https://app.vendurepos.com)]_ Fix: Point the nav item at /docs/quick-start and call it 'Quick start' (or 'Set up your store'). Change the line under the hero to link only the quick start, for example 'Your own store needs the plugin first: the quick start sets it up and ends with your first sale.'
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): The quick start, the page the home page sends you to, sets the till up with "a Vendure administrator" and never points to per-cashier accounts with the limited vendurepos-pos-till role, so a small shop is steered toward sharing an admin password at the counter. _[quick-start/desktop/02, step 4 'Enable bearer authentication']_ Fix: Add one sentence to quick start step 4: "For staff, give each cashier their own account with only the till role: see Give each cashier their own account", linking to /docs/connect-a-till#give-each-cashier-their-own-account.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): Refund instructions leave the developer to infer that settling the Vendure refund does not return funds through the external card terminal. _[shots/plugin-setup.desktop.03.jpg; text/plugin-setup.txt, Settle a Dashboard refund]_ Fix: Before the mutation, explicitly instruct the operator to return funds through the external terminal or hand back cash, then record the completed refund with settleRefund. Explain what reference to supply as transactionId and link the existing cash Pay out instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C1): The product screenshot, the first image anyone sees on the home page and the share card, is cut mid-element: on desktop through a fourth row of half-visible product tiles and the clipped 'Guest · 3 items' bar, and on og-image.png through the 'In Stock' pills and 'Add charge' button. It looks like an accidental crop, not a framed image. _[home/desktop/01 hero image bottom edge (y≈1135-1155); home.desktop-dark.jpg same spot; og-image.png bottom edge]_ Fix: Capture or crop the till screenshot so it ends on a row boundary (after the third tile row, with the cart footer whole), or fade the bottom edge with a mask gradient. Use the same crop for the OG card.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells 'every sale lands in Vendure once' and keeps a candid Limitations page, but Troubleshooting reveals that a till update the store refuses with any code other than the two named ones can never be resent or cleared, and later register updates queue behind it. The Limitations page leaves this out. A sceptic who finds it buried in Troubleshooting stops trusting the Limitations list as complete, and would tell a client the register side is not production-safe. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 'Till updates line' bullet 'N till updates need attention'; limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one bullet to Limitations, for example: 'A register update the store refuses (other than register_session_already_open or register_closure_number_invalid) cannot be resent from the till; later register updates for that register stay on the till', linking to the Troubleshooting section.
