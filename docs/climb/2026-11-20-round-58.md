## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero and both CTAs are clear, but the first selling point a visitor reads ('Keeps selling offline') spends half its text on loss cases in confusing terms: 'A reload needs the connection, and sales not yet sent are lost if this site's data is cleared'. A newcomer cannot tell whether 'this site' means vendurepos.com or the till. _[home.desktop.01 (first feature card); home.phone.02]_
- J2 (8): The home page never says what using the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com costs or whether it is free; the only hint is 'MIT licensed' in the footer and 'The till's source is MIT licensed' under Setting up the store, so a shop owner cannot tell if there is a subscription. _[home desktop 01-02 / phone 04-05]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): On the phone, about 120 px of dead space sits above the 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' badge before the H1 'Point of sale for Vendure'. The hero till screenshot's category chips are also cut off at 'Gif'. _[home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The only sign that this is a 0.x pre-release is a small 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' pill above the hero. The share card says nothing about pre-release or open source, so a link pasted into Slack looks like a finished product next to Shopify POS or Square. _[home.desktop.01 (hero pill); og-image.png]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That is an unexplained domain from a different brand, and together with 'Built with TallyUI' and the Tally* code names it makes a stranger wonder who actually runs this. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): The two proof cards ('Read the acceptance test', 'Read the 200-send test') link straight to test source files on GitHub (offline.spec.ts, replay.e2e.ts) with no visible pass result or date, so a non-developer gets code, not evidence that the claim holds today. _[home desktop 01 / phone 02, 'What it does today' cards]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/troubleshooting.txt]_
- J4 (9): The refund caveat (refunds stay Pending until two Admin API calls) appears three times on one page: in the comparison table row, in 'Before you start' and in the Limitations link. The repetition reads as padding, not candour. _[home.desktop.02, home.phone.04]_
- J5 (6): Three plugin releases came out in five days (0.1.0 on 2026-10-01, then 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 both on 2026-10-06), and there has been nothing in the six weeks since. The changelog admits a known bug: 'in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder, so the till's order history is refused'. Four pages document a manual workaround, but no fix has shipped. No maintainer, company or support contact is named anywhere, and the demo sends usage events to ph.wcpos.com without saying who runs it. A client would ask who stands behind this. _[changelog.desktop.01 (0.2.0 entry, 'Not in the release notes'); home.desktop.02 (demo table, footer)]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): The first proof point a first-time visitor reads leads with how sales get lost ('A reload needs the connection, and sales not yet sent are lost if this site's data is cleared'). 'This site' is ambiguous, so the caveat reads as a scare rather than a clear limit. _[home.desktop.01, 'Keeps selling offline' card; home.phone.02]_ Fix: Lead the card with the proof (25 sales, 20 offline, exactly 25 orders) and move the caveat to one plain line naming the place: 'Keep the till's tab open while offline; unsent sales live in that browser, so clearing its data loses them.'
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): For a shop owner the two questions that matter together, 'what if the internet drops' and 'how do I close the day', are never answered together: the docs do not say whether the register can be closed and the Z report printed while offline, or what the cashier should do when the till shows 'Closing — waiting for N orders'. _[at-the-till desktop 03-04 / phone 07-08]_ Fix: Add one sentence under 'If the internet drops' (and echo it in 'Count the drawer and close the day') saying whether closing finishes and the Z report prints while offline, and what to do on 'Closing — waiting for N orders' (wait, or close and let them send later).
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the supported store-wide tax strategy from the setup instructions. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start step 6]_ Fix: Add explicit guidance for mixed-pricing channels, including the supported strategy and a receipt-versus-order tax verification example. If this configuration is unsupported, state that directly beside the table.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The till screenshots on At the till use three different widths (about 836, 418 and 546 px), two of them double-framed and centred, with captions aligned to each image's edge instead of the text column. The page looks assembled, not designed. _[at-the-till.desktop.02, at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.desktop.04]_ Fix: Render every till screenshot through one figure component: text-column width (or one fixed max-width for portrait sheets, left-aligned), one 1 px border and radius, no outer padded card, and the caption flush with the text column's left edge. Crop the payment screen to the part that matters.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The plugin has had no release since 2026-10-06. It carries a known, documented bug (the till role lacks ReadOrder, so order history is refused) that is handled with a manual workaround on four pages instead of a fix, and no maintainer or support contact is named anywhere. To a sceptic comparing it with Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed, this reads as a project that might be abandoned. _[changelog.desktop.01 (0.2.0 entry, 'Not in the release notes' bullet); home footer]_ Fix: Ship plugin 0.3.1 that adds ReadOrder to the vendurepos-pos-till role, or state on the changelog when a fix is due. Add one line to the home footer or overview naming who maintains VendurePOS, where to get support, and who runs ph.wcpos.com.
