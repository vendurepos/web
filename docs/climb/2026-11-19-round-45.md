## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear ('Point of sale for Vendure', two buttons, a till screenshot), but the home page then ends in a developer checklist a newcomer cannot use: 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12 on the server that runs Vendure', 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', 'MySQL, MariaDB and SQLite are not supported'. A visitor who half knows what Vendure is gets semver ranges and env-var names before any plain 'is this for me?' line. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' list; home.phone.04-05]_
- J2 (8): The hero's one-line pitch leans on developer terms ('Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework') and on phone the top bar shows only 'Docs' plus a chevron, so the page a counter owner most wants ('At the till') is hidden behind the menu until the 'Before you start' footer paragraph. _[home.phone.01 (header and hero paragraph); home.phone.05 ('For cashiers, At the till covers…')]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The ten equal-weight "What it does today" cards push the demo-vs-your-store comparison table and "Before you start" about 2.5 screens down on phone, so the 'what to click next' context is buried below a uniform feature wall. _[home.phone.02-03]_
- J5 (8): The hero badge reads "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0", so a buyer reads the server plugin's version as the product's maturity. The till they will actually use is "till app version 0.1.0", and that number appears only on the Changelog page. _[home.desktop.01 hero badge vs changelog.desktop.01 second paragraph]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The demo row says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie', but nothing on the home page says who or what wcpos.com is. To a stranger, a VendurePOS demo reporting to an unrelated-looking domain reads as data going to a third party. Connect a till only adds that it is a 'self-hosted PostHog analytics server', and still never names the owner. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home.phone.04; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go']_
- J2 (7): The home comparison table says a Dashboard refund 'stays Pending until it is settled', which sounds like one click. Only Limitations and Plugin setup reveal that every refund needs two Admin API GraphQL calls (look up the refund ID, then the settleRefund mutation with a transactionId), which in practice means calling my developer for each refund. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Refund a sale at the till' vs limitations.desktop.01 bullet 7 and text/plugin-setup.txt 'Settle a Dashboard refund']_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The Pending-refund caveat appears three times on home (table row, "Before you start" bullet and its link text), which reads as repetitive rather than as an extra limit. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): The site never says who builds or runs VendurePOS, who operates the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com, on what terms or for how long, or where to report a bug. The only hint at an operator is the unexplained third-party domain "ph.wcpos.com" in the demo row. Every store's till loads from app.vendurepos.com, and "a reload ... cannot load the till until the connection is back". _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table and footer; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go'; limitations.desktop.01]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Step 4 says to sign in with 'a Vendure administrator whose role in the till's channel has TallyPosSell, ReadCatalog, ReadSettings, ReadCustomer and CreateCustomer ... Also add ReadOrder'. It gives no Dashboard path or snippet for creating that role, so a first-timer must leave the quick start (via 'Give each cashier their own account') to work out the one step that gates sign-in. _[quick-start.desktop.02 '4. Further down, the device-key role 'has the step 4 permissions except ReadOrder, so add ReadOrder… yourself', which is easy to miss and leaves a till whose order history is refused. _[quick-start.desktop.02 step 4; text/quick-start.txt 'Optional: a device key for each till']_
- J3 (8): The tax setup prescribes different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channel pricing, but tells stores mixing these modes only to 'account for the store-wide strategy', leaving the actual configuration decision unresolved. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_
- J4 (8): Step 8 "Sign in and make a sale" is a text-only a-i list with no screenshot of the sign-in screen or the synced till, so the guide never shows what success looks like. _[quick-start.desktop.03]_
- J5 (8): The store-side config change for @vendure/email-plugin ("swap its order-confirmation handler first") is not one of the config steps. It is a warning callout inside step 8 'Sign in and make a sale', after step 7 has already told you to restart Vendure, so a store that sends email has to go back, edit the config and restart again before its first sale. _[quick-start.desktop.03 '8. ## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): For someone who arrived from a search for 'Vendure point of sale', the home page turns into server-admin detail ('Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RXDB_PREMIUM', the database list) before it plainly says what they need to have. With the ten equal grey text cards above it, a newcomer who skims past the hero gets a wall of text and jargon instead of a clear 'try the demo' or 'you need a Vendure store' fork. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' (and the cards above it); home.phone.04-05]_ Fix: Replace the home 'Before you start' list with one plain sentence, e.g. 'You need your own Vendure 3.6+ store on Postgres; no store? Try the live demo.', linking to Overview > Requirements for the version and licence detail.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): Refunds are the one daily counter task the till can't do on a real store. The home page only says the Dashboard refund 'stays Pending until it is settled'. It does not say that settling means a developer running Admin API GraphQL (refund lookup plus settleRefund with a transactionId) for every refund, so an owner deciding whether staff can sell tomorrow finds out late. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04 table row 'Refund a sale at the till'; at-the-till.desktop.02 'Refund a sale']_ Fix: In the home table cell and the At the till 'Refund a sale' step, say plainly: 'settling it needs an Admin API call (settleRefund) by whoever runs your Vendure server; there is no Dashboard button', and link directly to Plugin setup's 'Settle a Dashboard refund' anchor.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing must guess how to choose the single store-wide order tax strategy. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: State the supported configuration or explicit limitation for stores mixing pricing modes. Include a concrete verification of receipt and Vendure order tax totals for each mode before taking live sales.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The home "What it does today" section is a wall of 10 identical grey cards in a 2-column grid with ragged heights. Proof-backed claims (offline, a sale lands once) get the same weight as minor features, and on phone the section takes about 2.5 screens. _[home.desktop.01-02 / home.phone.02-03]_ Fix: Show the two proof-backed cards larger with their test links. Turn the other eight into a compact 3- or 4-column list of one-line items, and cap prose width at about 70ch.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A consultant can't tell a client who stands behind this till. There is no maintainer or organisation, no support or issue-tracker link, and nothing on who operates app.vendurepos.com, what it costs or how long it will stay up. Yet every store's till is served from that host and cannot reload without it, and self-hosting needs a separate RxDB Premium licence the site doesn't explain. The only operator clue is an unexplained "ph.wcpos.com". _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' and footer; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go']_ Fix: Add a short 'Who runs this' block to the home page and Overview. It should name the maintainer, link the GitHub issues page for support, and say in one line who hosts app.vendurepos.com, whether it is free, and what happens if it goes away. It should also say that RxDB Premium is a paid third-party licence.
