## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The first thing above the headline is the badge "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0", which assumes the visitor already knows there is a plugin; someone who half knows Vendure only learns two lines later that a server plugin exists, and the British word "till" carries the whole hero sentence without being glossed. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 (hero badge and lead paragraph)]_
- J2 (8): The hero till screenshot contradicts itself on phone: the cart reads "Cart (4) · €59.59" while the customer line says "Guest · 3 items", and the quantity column header is cut to "Q...". A shop owner checking that the numbers add up notices it. _[home.phone.01, home.phone.02]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): At 390 px the hero lead paragraph ('VendurePOS is an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework...') runs to seven centred lines in large grey type, so the CTA pair sits below a slab of ragged centred text that is hard to read. _[home/phone/01]_
- J5 (9): On the phone the home header shows only the logo, a search icon and an unlabelled chevron, so Docs, Live demo and Quick start are hidden until the hero buttons; there is also no line saying who it is not for (for example 'not a Shopify POS / Square replacement with hardware'). _[home.phone.01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo sends "anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie": the domain belongs to a different brand and the home page never says whose server it is (only Connect a till calls it a self-hosted PostHog), so a newcomer reads it as an unexplained third party. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (8): The docs payment screenshot sells a test product called "Tally Fixture Mug", which makes the till look like a test rig rather than something a real shop uses. _[at-the-till.desktop.02]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): The home comparison table says the demo 'sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' without saying what wcpos.com is or how it relates to VendurePOS; a different brand's domain in the privacy row reads as unexplained. _[home/desktop/02 (table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'); home/phone/04]_
- J5 (7): The hero sells an 'open-source till', but self-hosting needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' with no word that this is a paid third-party licence or what it costs, and nothing says who runs app.vendurepos.com, the only alternative. Add the unfixed role defect: the changelog says 'Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder', and the last plugin release is 2026-10-06. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5; limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3; changelog.desktop.01 0.2.0 last bullet]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The quick start gives no estimate of the effort (nine numbered steps, two script files, a migration, CORS and tax strategy) before you begin, and step 2 opens with a naming caveat ("The plugin's code names say Tally, not VendurePOS") rather than progress, so a newcomer cannot tell up front whether this is a 10-minute or a 2-hour job. _[quick-start/desktop/01 intro and step 2]_
- J2 (7): The closing step, "Count the drawer and close the day", is text only: there is no screenshot of the close screen or the Z report, although payment and the cashier sheet both get one. Nothing marks which setup steps are 'hand this to your developer' or says how long setup takes, so I can't tell whether staff can sell tomorrow. _[at-the-till.desktop.03; quick-start.desktop.01]_
- J3 (8): The tax table requires different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive pricing, but 'A store mixing pricing modes must account for the store-wide strategy' leaves that installation case without a concrete configuration or explicit support boundary. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:794–823, Quick start step 6]_
- J4 (8): Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' is nine lettered prose steps (a-i) with no picture of the sign-in form or the opening-float screen, so the first look a developer gets at the screen they must fill in comes later, on At the till. _[quick-start/desktop/03-04; quick-start/phone/07-08]_
- J5 (8): Step 5 (CORS) adds a stray legacy note, 'A store that already allows https://vendurepos.vercel.app keeps it for as long as anyone still opens the POS at that address', which a new installer cannot act on. Device-key users must also hand-patch ReadOrder into the role the plugin's own 'New POS till key' button creates. _[quick-start.desktop.02 / quick-start.phone.05; quick-start 'Optional: a device key for each till']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): On the home page, the only statement about data leaving the browser in the demo names "ph.wcpos.com", a domain from another brand with no explanation of who runs it, so a visitor who has never heard of VendurePOS meets an unexplained third party exactly where they are judging whether to trust the demo. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04, 'Live demo or your own store' table, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_ Fix: In that cell, say whose it is, for example "to our own self-hosted PostHog analytics (ph.wcpos.com, run by the WCPOS team that builds VendurePOS), with no cookie", keeping the link to the Connect a till explanation.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): Limitations is one ungrouped list of 17 bullets that mixes counter limits ("does not open a cash drawer", card terminal, offline reload) with developer items ("RXDB_PREMIUM", "settleRefund mutation", "Postgres only"). A shop owner reading on the phone between customers cannot pick out what their staff will hit at the counter. _[limitations.desktop.01, limitations.phone.01]_ Fix: Split the page under two headings. "At the counter": no cash drawer, no card terminal link, printing, a reload needs the internet, no private windows, one tab per till, refunds via the Dashboard. "For your developer": Postgres only, RXDB_PREMIUM, settleRefund, device keys and versions. Put the counter list first, in plain words.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing must guess how to satisfy the two conflicting store-wide tax-strategy recommendations. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:794–823, Quick start step 6]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed-pricing channels and how to verify its totals. If that combination is unsupported, say so explicitly beside the tax table.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): The home page's phone hero image is a tall, cropped cart-only till screenshot (QTY header truncated to 'Q...', a button clipped by the totals border) that fills about 1.4 screens straight after the CTAs, so a phone visitor's first impression of the product is a cramped fragment of UI. _[home/phone/01-02]_ Fix: On phones, show a shorter crop or a capture made at a phone width that fits the column (e.g. products grid with the cart tab label), capped at about one screen tall with object-fit cover, so no headers are truncated and no buttons are clipped.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A buyer cannot judge lock-in. The site calls VendurePOS an open-source, MIT-licensed till, yet the only way to run it without a paid third-party RxDB Premium licence (price and terms not stated) is app.vendurepos.com, and no page says who operates that host or what happens if it stops. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; limitations.desktop.01; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'The till's source is MIT-licensed…']_ Fix: Next to the RXDB_PREMIUM line, say plainly that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence and link its pricing. Add one sentence naming who runs app.vendurepos.com and its continuity promise, for example 'static files, no server-side data; you can self-host the same build'.
