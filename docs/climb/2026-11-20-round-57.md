## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero itself is clear, but the lower half of the home page switches to server-admin detail a newcomer can't use: the refund row says "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", and "Before you start" lists "Node ^20.19 || >=22.12" and "RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)". _[home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' table, 'Before you start'); home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): Home tells me what it is ("Point of sale for Vendure", live demo, till screenshot) but never answers in plain words whether my two staff can sell with it tomorrow; instead the owner meets developer requirements such as "Node ^20.19 || >=22.12" and "RXDB_PREMIUM" in "Before you start". _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04-05, "Before you start"]_
- J3 (10): none found _[Home, desktop and phone: headline, prerequisites and demo/quick-start links]_
- J4 (8): On the phone the hero screenshot, captioned 'A sale in progress on the live demo till.', shows only the Products tab: two product cards, a category row cut at 'Gif', and the cart only as a 'Cart (3) · €20.09' tab label. Above the fold, phone readers see a product grid and never see the sale. _[home/phone/01]_
- J5 (8): The hero ('Point of sale for Vendure', 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0') says what it is, but nothing on home says who should not use it or how it differs from a hosted till (Shopify POS, Square) or another open-source one, so a buyer weighing options has no reason given to pick it. _[home/desktop/01-02]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the demo sends "anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com", but the home page never says who or what wcpos is. A visitor sees data going to an unrelated domain with no explanation; Connect a till explains it later as a self-hosted PostHog server. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (9): Honest to a fault, but the refund caveat is alarming and left unresolved for an owner: "No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". _[home "Live demo or your own store" table, refund row (home.desktop.02, home.phone.03)]_
- J3 (9): The quantified 25-sale and 200-send claims link to test source on main, without a dated run result identifying the tested revision. _[text/home.txt, What it does today; tech.json, home.desktop.links]_
- J4 (9): The card heading 'Keeps selling offline' promises more than its own body text, which says 'A reload needs the connection, and sales not yet sent are lost if this site's data is cleared'. The caveat is honest but it sits under a headline that reads as unconditional. _[home/desktop/01, 'What it does today' first card]_
- J5 (7): No person, company or contact is named anywhere; the footer gives only 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.', while the demo and the site send analytics to 'ph.wcpos.com', a domain the site never explains. Also, the latest plugin release, 0.3.0, is dated 2026-10-06 and still ships the role bug the changelog itself flags as 'Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder'. _[home/desktop/02 footer and demo table; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go'; changelog/desktop/01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the home page's 'Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start' block give a first-time visitor setup details they can't use yet (two Admin API calls to settle a refund, Node semver ranges, an RXDB_PREMIUM licence token, an unexplained ph.wcpos.com). It reads like the docs, not a pitch, and makes the product look harder to adopt than the demo suggests. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04]_ Fix: Cut the refund cell to 'No, refund in the Vendure Dashboard on plugin 0.3.0 (see Limitations)' and say who runs ph.wcpos.com in a few words. Shrink 'Setting up the store' to one line ('Vendure 3.6+ on Postgres, plus our npm plugin'), and leave the Node range and RxDB licence to the quick start.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): A shop owner cannot learn whether their counter setup will work: the site says "tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet" and refunds on their own store "stay Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", with no owner-level answer to either. _[home "Before you start" and refund row (home.desktop.02); limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a short "For shop owners" box on home or At the till: the exact counter hardware that is known to work (desktop Chrome + USB scanner + standalone card terminal), what to do today on an iPad, and a step-by-step Dashboard refund path the owner can follow without API calls.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the supported store-wide tax strategy from step 6. _[Quick start, step 6, Why these pairings; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:871]_ Fix: Document the supported configuration and verification steps for mixed pricing modes, or explicitly state that this setup is unsupported.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): The quick start's one decision table (step 6, which order tax strategy to pick) falls apart at 390 px. Each stacked card puts the unlabeled header chip 'taxOptions.orderTaxCalculationStrategy' on top of the value chip, so a phone reader sees two code chips per row and cannot tell the setting from its value, on the step that changes store-wide tax. _[quick-start/phone/06]_ Fix: In the stacked layout, give the second column the same bold-label treatment as 'Channel pricing' (for example 'orderTaxCalculationStrategy:' as plain bold text, then the value chip). Or replace the table with two lines, 'Tax-exclusive → new OrderLevelTaxCalculationStrategy()'. Drop the spaces inside '( pricesIncludeTax… )' at the same time.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): No person, company or contact is named anywhere on the site, while the demo and the site send analytics to 'ph.wcpos.com', a domain the site never explains. A sceptic advising a client cannot tell who stands behind a pre-release till whose last plugin release is six weeks old. _[home/desktop/02 footer and 'Live demo or your own store' table; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go']_ Fix: Add one line to the home footer and the Overview page naming who builds and maintains VendurePOS, how it is supported, and its relation to wcpos.com (the owner of ph.wcpos.com).
