## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear ("Point of sale for Vendure", Vendure explained in one clause, Live demo / Quick start), but the lower half of the home page switches to developer jargon a newcomer cannot parse: "Node ^20.19 || >=22.12", "RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)", and in the comparison table "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". The badge "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0" also assumes you already know there is a plugin. _[home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' Refund row, 'Before you start' list); home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): The hero and the 'What it does today' list speak to the freelancer (plugin 0.3.0, Vendure server, Postgres/Node requirements). Nothing tells the owner in one line whether the staff can sell tomorrow and who has to set what up first. On the phone, the header shows only Docs and Live demo; Quick start and At the till are hidden behind a chevron. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01 header]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Below the hero, 'What it does today' starts with two bordered cards ('Keeps selling offline', 'Every sale lands once', each with an outline button) and then drops to eight plain two-column heading+paragraph items. The section changes visual weight halfway through, so the eight items look like a lesser tier rather than more features. _[home.desktop, 'What it does today' section]_
- J5 (8): Home says what it is (a browser till for Vendure) and what it needs, but nowhere frames who should NOT use it or how it compares with Shopify POS / Square / Lightspeed (no card-terminal integration, no cash drawer, desktop Chrome only). A buyer only finds out on /docs/limitations; the home page has no one-line 'not for you if you need integrated card payments or a cash drawer'. _[home.desktop.01 / home.desktop.02 (feature list and 'Before you start')]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The demo row says "All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com" without ever saying who or what wcpos is. On a VendurePOS site an unexplained third-party domain reads as an odd, unexplained link. Elsewhere the honesty is strong: pre-release is stated, Limitations is linked, and claims are backed by 'Read the acceptance test' / 'Read the 200-send test'. _[home.desktop.02, table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): The offline story contradicts itself. The Troubleshooting text says 'everything at the till works offline except customer search'. At the till and Limitations say a reload needs the connection, and unsent sales are lost if site data is cleared. The search result for 'offline' surfaces the reassuring line first. _[troubleshooting text (search API hit for 'offline') vs at-the-till 'If the internet drops' and limitations]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The 'Refund a sale at the till' row of the 'Live demo or your own store' table has a third cell that runs to a 4-line paragraph. It crowds the column next to one-word 'Yes' cells and makes the honest caveat look like a footnote that escaped. _[home.desktop, comparison table, 'Refund a sale at the till' row]_
- J5 (7): No maintainer or owner is named anywhere: the footer reads only 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.', yet the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' with no explanation of who wcpos is. The changelog also admits a bug in the role the plugin itself creates ('Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder, so the till's order history is refused until you add it to the role') with no plugin release since 2026-10-06. And home's 'keyboard-wedge scanners work out of the box' oversells, because you must first add a custom barcode field and a migration. _[home.desktop.02 footer; home.txt demo row; changelog.desktop.01 / changelog.txt]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The quick start makes you choose between paths, and there is legacy noise along the way. Step 3 offers a generated migration, `npx vendure migrate` or the bundled migrations, with a warning not to mix them. Step 5 ends in a collapsed "If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app" note that means nothing to a new user. The permissions list for signing in sits under step 4 'Enable bearer authentication', four steps before the sign-in it applies to (step 8). _[quick-start.desktop.02 (step 3 'Other ways to run the migration', step 4 permissions), quick-start.desktop.03 (vercel.app collapsible)]_
- J2 (7): Quick start is nine developer steps (npm install, TallyPosPlugin, tsx migrations, tokenMethod, CORS_ORIGINS, a tax strategy table). There is no owner-level summary of how long it takes or what the freelancer must hand over to staff (till URL, logins). There are no scanner or receipt-printer setup steps beyond the browser print dialog. _[quick-start.desktop.01-05]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 prescribes different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but 'A store mixing pricing modes must account for the store-wide strategy' leaves that configuration unresolved. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings']_
- J4 (8): The quick start is a long page (steps 1-9, sub-steps a-i, 'Check it worked') with no image at all: no sign-in screen, no first sale, no synced order in the Vendure Dashboard. Success is described only as a text checklist. _[quick-start.desktop / quick-start.phone, steps 8-9 and 'Check it worked']_
- J5 (8): Quick-start step 3 makes you hand-save two TypeScript files (src/generate-migration.ts, src/run-migrations.ts) and run them with 'npx tsx' just to apply migrations. On top of that comes the ReadOrder role fix the plugin should have done itself, and a legacy collapsible 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' that is noise for a first install. _[quick-start.desktop.02 / quick-start.phone.03-04 (step 3); quick-start.txt]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the home page's 'Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start' list turn into a developer requirements sheet: semver ranges, an env var name (RXDB_PREMIUM), 'two Admin API calls', 'ph.wcpos.com'. A first-time visitor trying to tell whether this is for them hits jargon instead of a plain answer. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04]_ Fix: On the home page, replace 'Before you start' with one plain line ("Needs a Vendure 3.6+ store on Postgres and the free VendurePOS server plugin") linking to the docs Requirements. Cut the Refund cell to "Not yet: refund in the Vendure Dashboard", and move the Node range, the RXDB_PREMIUM detail and the Admin API detail into the docs.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The site gives two answers to the owner's key question 'what happens when the internet drops'. Troubleshooting says 'everything at the till works offline except customer search'. At the till and Limitations say a reload or closed tab needs the connection, and unsent sales can be lost. _[troubleshooting text vs at-the-till 'If the internet drops' / limitations]_ Fix: Make the Troubleshooting line say the same thing as At the till: 'Selling works offline as long as the till's tab stays open; do not reload or close it until the connection is back.' Put that sentence in the home 'What it does today' offline item too.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to choose the store-wide order tax strategy. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Document the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and how to verify its totals. If that setup is unsupported, state the limitation explicitly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The At the till figures do not share a grid. The payment screen is full column width (~836 px), the cashier sheet is ~418 px in a padded box and the closure is ~545 px inside a double frame. Two of the captions hang off the figure's left edge instead of the text column. It is the one page whose figures look assembled rather than designed. _[at-the-till.desktop, payment / cashier sheet / closure figures]_ Fix: Give every figure the same frame and width (full content column, one border, one radius), crop the cashier and closure captures tight to their content instead of padding them, and left-align every figcaption to the text column.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A sceptic cannot tell who stands behind VendurePOS or whether it is maintained. No person or company is named. The footer only says 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' Demo analytics go to an unexplained ph.wcpos.com. The plugin has shipped nothing since 0.3.0 on 2026-10-06, even though the changelog itself admits the role it creates lacks ReadOrder and breaks the till's order history. _[home.desktop.02 footer; changelog.desktop.01 / changelog.txt]_ Fix: Name the maintainer in the footer and on home (e.g. 'Built and maintained by WCPOS', with ph.wcpos.com stated as theirs) and add a support/issues link. Ship plugin 0.3.1 that adds ReadOrder to the role, so the caveat can be deleted from the four pages that repeat it.
