## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero never says who the product is for in plain words: the 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' badge and 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first' assume the visitor knows what a server plugin is and that a developer must install it, so a shop owner who searched 'Vendure point of sale' cannot tell whether this is something they can set up themselves. _[home/desktop/01 (badge above h1, line under the Live demo / Quick start buttons); home/phone/01]_
- J2 (8): The hero speaks to the developer, not the counter: 'Point of sale for Vendure' plus a subtitle about the Vendure plugin, and nothing in the first screen tells a shop owner whether two staff can sell on it tomorrow (the only owner-facing line is the small note 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'). _[home/desktop/01, home/phone/01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero product screenshot is cropped through the middle of the fourth product row, leaving a band of half-tiles (cut red/yellow icons) along its bottom edge just above the caption 'A sale in progress on the live demo till.' _[home/desktop/01 (also home.desktop-dark)]_
- J5 (8): Nothing near the top says who should NOT pick this over Shopify POS/Square/Lightspeed: integrated card terminals, cash drawers, direct receipt printers and non-Chrome browsers are unsupported, but you only learn that from the 'Before you start' bullets after ten equal-weight feature cards. _[home.desktop.01-02 (feature grid, then 'Before you start' bullets); home.phone.02-05]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): Nowhere on the home page does it say who makes or maintains VendurePOS. The only hint is an unexplained third-party domain in the comparison table, 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'. To a newcomer, an unnamed maker plus a domain on a different brand reads as something hidden (Connect a till explains ph.wcpos.com is self-hosted PostHog, but the home page does not). _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home footer 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.']_
- J2 (8): The page meant to list what it does not do ('What VendurePOS does not do yet') never says the till cannot be reloaded or reopened while offline; that warning appears only in At the till > 'If the internet drops' ('a reload, or closing and reopening the browser, needs the connection to load the till again'), while the home card just says 'With the till's tab left open'. _[limitations/desktop/01, limitations/phone/01 vs at-the-till text 'If the internet drops']_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The two proof cards ('Read the acceptance test', 'Read the 200-send test') link to test source files on GitHub rather than to a passing run, so the '25 sales ... exactly 25 orders' and '200 times makes one order' claims point to code, not to a result. _[home/desktop/01, 'What it does today' first two cards]_
- J5 (7): The site leans on 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed' but says self-hosting needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' without ever saying that is a paid third-party commercial licence or what it costs. Nothing says who runs app.vendurepos.com, on what terms, or whether it stays free, so the only free path is a hosted app from an unnamed operator. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Where your data goes'; limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor cannot find out who is behind VendurePOS. The home page names no maker, team or company, and the only outside name it shows is the unexplained analytics domain 'ph.wcpos.com' in the demo comparison row. Being asked to put an unknown-author pre-release till on your shop's server, with an unfamiliar third-party domain beside the demo, is what makes this persona hesitate. _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' and the footer; home/phone/04]_ Fix: Add one line to the footer or near 'Before you start' saying who builds and maintains VendurePOS (for example 'Made by the team behind WCPOS'). In the demo row, write 'to our self-hosted PostHog analytics at ph.wcpos.com'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The Limitations page, the one an owner reads to learn what could go wrong at the counter, leaves out the biggest offline risk: if the internet is down, a reload or a browser restart cannot load the till again, so a cashier who refreshes the page cannot sell until the connection returns. _[limitations/desktop/01, limitations/phone/01]_ Fix: Add one bullet near the top of Limitations: 'Offline, keep the till's tab open: a reload or a browser restart needs the internet to load the till again.' Link it to At the till > If the internet drops.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the supported store-wide tax strategy from the instructions. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:814-816]_ Fix: Add explicit guidance for mixed-mode stores: the supported strategy choice and how to verify receipt and Vendure totals for each pricing mode. If no supported pairing exists, state that limitation directly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The home page's middle is a flat wall of ten equal-weight grey cards. On a 390 px phone it runs about two and a half screens of identical boxes before the 'Live demo or your own store' table, and the two tested claims get no more emphasis than minor features. _[home/desktop/01-02; home/phone/02-03]_ Fix: Keep 'Keeps selling offline' and 'Every sale lands once' as two larger proof cards. Set the other eight as a compact two-column list of title plus one line, without card borders or fill.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The 'open-source, MIT licensed' pitch hides a cost. Running the till yourself needs a paid third-party RxDB Premium licence, and the site never says it is paid. The free alternative is app.vendurepos.com, with no word on who operates it, its terms, or its future. A sceptic advising a client reads this as vendor lock-in presented as open source. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' (RXDB_PREMIUM bullet); connect-a-till.desktop.01 MIT-licensed paragraph; limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3]_ Fix: In that home bullet and on Limitations, say plainly that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence from RxDB, and link its pricing. Add one sentence on who runs app.vendurepos.com and that it is free to use during pre-release.
