## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero leans on jargon a newcomer has to decode ('the open-source headless commerce framework', 'till'), and the second button 'Quick start' drops a non-developer straight into npm installs and TypeScript with no hint that a developer is needed; only the small grey line under the buttons hints at it ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'). _[home/desktop/01 hero paragraph and line under the CTAs; home/phone/01]_
- J2 (8): The home 'Before you start' list mixes what a counter needs (a browser, desktop Chrome, no cash drawer, no coupon codes) with server requirements a shop owner can't act on ('Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', Postgres, 'RXDB_PREMIUM'), so 'can my staff use this tomorrow?' is buried in developer detail. _[home.desktop.02 (Before you start); home.phone.04-05]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero is clear ('Point of sale for Vendure', Live demo / Quick start), but the real first step for a developer, 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first…', sits as small gray text under the buttons, and the phone header shows only the logo, search and a chevron, with no visible Docs entry. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and gives two clear CTAs, but nowhere on the home page says who it is for or not for (shop size, single vs multi-store, who it is an alternative to such as Shopify POS or Square). A buyer comparing tills has to infer fit from the 'Before you start' list. _[home/desktop/01 hero and home/desktop/02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (7): Nothing on the home page says who makes or maintains VendurePOS (footer: 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.'), yet the comparison table says the demo 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a second, unexplained brand. A first-time visitor reads that as an unknown third party collecting data. It is only explained on Connect a till ('a self-hosted PostHog analytics server'). _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' and footer; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): The site never says whether the till can run on the iPad or tablet a small shop is likely to have at the counter; the only line on devices is 'Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome', which leaves an owner guessing rather than reading a clear yes or no. _[home.desktop.02 (Before you start); limitations.desktop.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; shots/limitations.desktop.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The changelog prose exposes internal references such as '(ADR 0005)' and issue numbers that mean nothing to an outside reader, and they read as repo notes rather than release notes. _[changelog.desktop.01, changelog.phone.01]_
- J5 (7): The hero sells an 'open-source till', but 'To build and host it yourself instead of using app.vendurepos.com, you need your own RxDB Premium licence token' gives no cost and no free path. The only alternative is a hosted origin (app.vendurepos.com) whose operator, price, terms and uptime are never stated. The only hint of who is behind the project is the unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' in the demo row. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' bullet 6 and the 'Sales reach Vendure' row; limitations 'Setting up the store']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The new-user path through step 5 contains a collapsed legacy aside, 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app', which names a domain the reader has never seen and leaves them unsure whether it applies to them. Step 8 (sign in and make a sale) also has no picture of the sign-in screen or a synced order. _[quick-start/desktop/02 bottom; quick-start/phone/05]_
- J2 (8): The quick-start CORS step hides 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' in a collapsed details block. That host name appears nowhere else and reads like a leftover, and step 2's 'say Tally, not VendurePOS' adds a second unexplained product name. _[quick-start.desktop.02 (CORS step, step 2)]_
- J3 (8): The tax instructions prescribe different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but say only that a store mixing pricing modes 'must account for the store-wide strategy', leaving its configuration unresolved. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:844]_
- J4 (8): Step 4's permission list is a run-on paragraph of seven inline code chips ('TallyPosSell, ReadCatalog, ReadSettings, ReadCustomer and CreateCustomer … CreateOrder … ReadOrder') instead of a checklist, which is the step most likely to be set up wrong. _[quick-start.desktop.02, quick-start.phone.04]_
- J5 (8): The device-key path has a known defect that is patched over in prose rather than fixed. 'On plugin 0.3.0 the role it creates (vendurepos-pos-till) has the step 4 permissions except ReadOrder, so add ReadOrder to that role yourself.' A developer who misses that line gets a till that sells but whose order history is refused. _[quick-start 'Optional: a device key for each till'; connect-a-till 'Create and use a device key'; changelog 0.2.0 last bullet]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor never learns who is behind VendurePOS: the home page names no maker or maintainer, yet its comparison table says the demo sends usage events to 'ph.wcpos.com', an unexplained other brand, which reads as an unknown third party collecting data. _[home/desktop/02 comparison table and footer; home/phone/04-05]_ Fix: Add one footer line naming the maker (e.g. 'Made by the WCPOS team'), and in the table say 'to our own self-hosted analytics server (ph.wcpos.com)', linking to the Connect a till section that explains it.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): The till guide I would hand my two staff, At the till, is a 9,213 px phone page that threads owner and version caveats ('On plugin 0.3.0...', 'On the live demo...') through the cashier steps. 'Refund a sale' opens with a numbered item that is a statement, not a step, so staff can't quickly see what to do at the counter for open, sell, refund and close. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 / at-the-till.phone (Refund a sale, whole page)]_ Fix: Put a five-line 'First shift' checklist at the top of At the till (open register with float → scan and sell → take card on your terminal, enter amount → print receipt → count drawer and close with Z report). Move the plugin-0.3.0 and live-demo notes into one callout, so the Refund steps start with what the cashier actually does.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer whose channels mix tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing must guess how to satisfy the documented store-wide tax-strategy requirement. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:844]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed-pricing channels and how to verify its totals. If that combination is unsupported, say so explicitly beside the strategy table.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The 'What it does today' section is ten visually identical gray text cards with no icons, emphasis or grouping. The three proven claims (keeps selling offline, every sale lands once, paid/fulfilled at the till's price, two of them with test links) look the same as 'Barcode scanners', and on phone the section becomes about 1700 CSS px of the same box. _[home.desktop.01-02, home.phone.02-03]_ Fix: Promote the three proof cards (offline, every sale once, paid/fulfilled) to a 3-up row that carries their test links. Render the other seven as a compact two-column list without card chrome, single column on phone.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): 'Open-source, MIT' is the headline, but in practice a store runs a till served from app.vendurepos.com by an unnamed operator, or pays for an RxDB Premium licence of unstated cost to self-host. The site never says who runs the hosted till, what it costs, or what happens to a shop if it goes away. That is the question I would put to a client before recommending it over Square or Shopify POS. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' (RXDB_PREMIUM bullet) and the 'ph.wcpos.com' demo row; limitations 'Setting up the store']_ Fix: Add two sentences to 'Before you start': who operates app.vendurepos.com and that it is free with no account, and what an RxDB Premium licence costs or a link to its price. A one-line 'Who maintains VendurePOS' with the maintainer's name would also explain the ph.wcpos.com domain.
