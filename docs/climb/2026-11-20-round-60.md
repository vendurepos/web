## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but further down the home page 'Before you start' shows a newcomer developer-only detail like 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12' and 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', and the first feature card ('Keeps selling offline') spends most of its text on how sales can be lost ('a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them') before the reader knows why they would want it. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start'; home/desktop/01 'Keeps selling offline' card]_
- J2 (8): The hero tells a shop owner what it is, but the next step is only for a developer: 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first' sends you to a quick start that opens with npm and migrations. Nothing above the fold says 'staff guide: At the till', so you can't see how your cashiers would use it. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, line under the Live demo / Quick start buttons]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The centred hero lede runs four lines at 1440 px and the last line, 'connection drops.', sits alone as a short centred orphan under three long lines, which softens an otherwise clean hero. _[home/desktop/01 (hero paragraph under 'Point of sale for Vendure')]_
- J5 (8): No 'who this is for / who should not use it' line and no comparison with Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed: the page goes from the 'What it does today' features straight to 'Live demo or your own store' and setup, so a shopper comparing tills cannot tell why they would pick a pre-release Vendure till _[home.desktop.01-02]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The home page never says what the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com costs or how long it stays available, but it does say self-hosting needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token', so a newcomer is left wondering whether there is a hidden paid part. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet; home footer 'MIT licensed']_
- J2 (9): The home comparison table says the demo 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' and never says who wcpos is. On a VendurePOS site an unexplained third-party domain reads like something being slipped in; only Connect a till says it is a self-hosted PostHog server. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J3 (9): The quantified acceptance-run claims link to test source on main rather than a dated run result identifying the tested revision. _[text/home.txt, 'What it does today'; tech.json, home.desktop.links]_
- J4 (9): The hero badge reads 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0', but the till's own version ('till app version 0.1.0, built 20 Nov 2026') only appears deep in the Changelog intro, so the one version a visitor sees describes the plugin, not the app they are about to open. _[home/desktop/01 badge vs changelog/phone/01 intro]_
- J5 (7): Hero sells an 'open-source till', but 'Before you start' says 'you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' to build it yourself, a paid licence in a bottom bullet with no price; separately the till role's missing ReadOrder is admitted only as 'Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder' and is still unpatched six weeks after the 2026-10-06 release _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store'; changelog.desktop.01 0.2.0 notes]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor can't find out from the home page whether VendurePOS costs anything. It says 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', but the hosted till's terms are never stated, while the only money-related mention is a paid 'RxDB Premium licence token' for self-hosting. It reads as if a cost is being left unsaid. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' > 'Setting up the store']_ Fix: Add one plain line near the hero or in 'Before you start' saying what using app.vendurepos.com costs during pre-release, and that the RxDB Premium licence is only needed if you build and host the till yourself.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): The cashier guide (At the till) sends staff to Troubleshooting for sync notices, and that page mixes counter-staff steps with server-admin errorCodes and SuperAdmin GraphQL mutations on one long page (7 phone screens). Staff reading on a phone mid-shift can't tell what they're supposed to do and what to hand to the developer. _[troubleshooting.phone.01-07; at-the-till 'If the internet drops' link]_ Fix: Move 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way', the errorCode list and the plain-http/CSP notes into a 'For whoever runs your Vendure server' page or section at the end. Open Troubleshooting with a short cashier-only list: each header message and what to tap.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): A developer following the documented refund procedure can mark the Vendure refund settled without being explicitly instructed to return the customer's card payment through the external terminal. _[shots/plugin-setup.desktop.03.jpg, 'Settle a Dashboard refund'; text/at-the-till.txt, 'Refund a sale']_ Fix: Before settleRefund, explicitly instruct the operator to complete the refund through the external card terminal and retain its reference. Clarify that recording settlement in Vendure does not itself return money through that terminal.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The first content section after the hero, 'What it does today', puts two bordered cards with buttons and eight unbordered text items in one two-column grid. The right card ('Every sale lands once') has dead space above its button, so the section reads as two orphaned cards over a list, not one designed hierarchy, on desktop and in dark mode. _[home/desktop/01-02, home.desktop-dark]_ Fix: Pull the two proof cards into their own full-width row (or a single 'Proven by tests' band) above a clearly separate feature list. Alternatively, give all ten items the same treatment, and align the two cards' buttons with equal body heights.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Hero calls it an 'open-source till', but building or hosting the till yourself needs a paid RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM), mentioned only in a bottom 'Before you start' bullet with no price or explanation _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store'; limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: In the hero or at the top of 'Before you start', say plainly: the till's source is MIT, building it yourself needs a commercial RxDB Premium licence (give the rough cost and a link), and app.vendurepos.com is the free hosted till that needs no licence
