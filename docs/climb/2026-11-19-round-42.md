## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero says what it is but never says who it is for in plain words: the second paragraph spends its words on insider guarantees ('Every sale lands in your Vendure store once, as a normal order at the till's price') that a newcomer cannot weigh, instead of 'for shops that sell online with Vendure and also sell at a counter'. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, hero paragraph under 'Point of sale for Vendure']_
- J2 (8): The hero gives a shop owner only developer next steps ('Live demo' / 'Quick start'), and its lede leans on jargon ('the open-source headless commerce framework'). The cashier guide 'At the till' never appears as a hero action: it is in the top nav and the closing paragraph only, so 'can my staff sell with this tomorrow' has no obvious click. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01 (nav collapsed behind a chevron, so At the till is not visible at all)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero repeats its own second CTA: under the 'Live demo' / 'Quick start' buttons sits a grey note whose underlined 'quick start' link points to the same page, so three targets compete in the first fold where two would do. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 (note under the hero buttons)]_
- J5 (8): Home never says who builds or supports this or who runs the hosted till: the only maintainer signal is the footer 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' A sceptic weighing it against Shopify POS or Square gets no answer to 'who is behind this and will it be here next year?' _[home desktop/phone, hero to footer]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a third domain from an unexplained brand (wcpos). A first-time visitor wonders who that is; Connect a till explains it, but the home page does not. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (9): The only notable defect is that the hardest truth for an owner sits in Troubleshooting rather than on Limitations or home. Under 'Any other code' it says 'the till has no retry or skip, and fixing the store does not make it send the update again … Those closures and cash records do not reach your Vendure store from this till'. Everywhere else, limits are stated plainly and the claims link to acceptance tests. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, Till updates line, 'Any other code']_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): Proof is uneven across the feature grid: 'Keeps selling offline' and 'Every sale lands once' link to their tests, but the equally strong claim 'Paid, fulfilled, at the till's price' (and the other seven cards) carry no link or evidence, so the grid reads half-proven. _[home/desktop/01, 'What it does today' cards]_
- J5 (6): 'MIT licensed' is undercut by 'Building it yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM'. No price, link or reason is given, so in practice you depend on app.vendurepos.com, whose operator, cost and continuity are never stated. The demo also shows more than a real store gets: refund at the till is 'No, not on plugin 0.3.0' for your store, and the customer note is demo-only ('On plugin 0.3.0, Order details has no customer note'), yet the home comparison table leaves the note out _[home 'Before you start'; connect-a-till 'Where requests go'; limitations 'Setting up the store'; at-the-till]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): In the five-second read the hero says what VendurePOS is, but its supporting sentence is written in guarantees a newcomer does not yet care about ('lands ... once', 'at the till's price'). It never states the plain use case: a shop running its online store on Vendure that also sells in person and wants those sales in the same orders and stock. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero paragraph]_ Fix: Replace the last sentence of the hero paragraph with one plain line naming the audience and the outcome, e.g. 'For shops that sell online with Vendure and in person: counter sales go into the same Vendure orders and stock.' Keep 'lands once' for the feature card below.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): Nothing on the home page speaks to the owner or the counter staff. The hero actions are 'Live demo' and 'Quick start' (npm, migrations, CORS), and the page that answers 'can staff sell tomorrow, what if the internet drops, how do I close the day' (At the till) appears only as a nav link (hidden behind a chevron on phone) and in the final paragraph. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01 hero]_ Fix: Add a third hero link, 'For your staff: At the till', plus one line under it for owners: sell, keep selling offline with the tab open, close the day with a Z report.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The quick start leaves developers with mixed inclusive/exclusive channels to determine the required store-wide tax strategy themselves. _[/docs/quick-start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:872]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes, or explicitly identify that configuration as unsupported. Include a concrete check of the resulting POS and storefront totals.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): The home page's ten 'What it does today' cards are identical text-only grey boxes; on a phone they stack into two-plus screens of the same card, the longest unbroken run on the site, and on desktop they form a monotone 2x5 slab with a taller first row. _[home/phone/02-03 (and home/desktop/01-02)]_ Fix: Keep the two proof cards ('Keeps selling offline', 'Every sale lands once') as full cards with their test links, and turn the other eight into a compact two-column checklist (title plus one line each) that becomes a tight list on phone.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells itself as 'MIT licensed' open source, but building the till yourself 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM', a paid proprietary dependency with no price, link or explanation. The alternative is the hosted app.vendurepos.com, and the site never says who runs it, whether it stays free or what happens if it goes away. A sceptic comparing it with Square or Shopify POS reads this as lock-in dressed as open source. _[home 'Before you start' (desktop slice 02, phone slice 05); connect-a-till 'Where requests go'; limitations 'Setting up the store']_ Fix: Next to the RXDB_PREMIUM bullet, link RxDB Premium's licence and price and say why it is needed. Add one line saying who operates app.vendurepos.com, that it is free, and that the static build can be self-hosted if it disappears. Better: ship a self-build fallback on free RxDB storage.
