## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the next thing a newcomer reads, the home page's 'Before you start' list, is aimed at developers: a raw semver string 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and 'one tab per till and not a private window'. It reads as setup internals, not as who this is for. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04-05, 'Before you start']_
- J2 (8): The home page never says which counter device works. The only hint is "Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome", so an owner with a counter tablet or iPad can't tell whether their staff can sell on it tomorrow. The opening line also leans on developer jargon ("Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework"). _[home.desktop.02 "Before you start"; home.phone.01 hero]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): On phone the hero screenshot is the narrow till layout showing only the Products tab and product grid; the cart is reduced to a 'Cart (3) · €20.09' tab, so the caption 'A sale in progress on the live demo till.' sits under an image where no sale is visible. _[home/phone/01-02]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and offers 'Live demo' and 'Quick start', but nowhere on home is there a who-this-is-not-for line next to Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed (no integrated card terminal, no cash drawer, desktop Chrome only, Postgres only). Those deal-breakers are scattered through the 'Before you start' bullets at the bottom of the page instead. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The home table says the demo 'sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. The visitor has never heard of wcpos, and nothing on the home page says who that is or how it relates to VendurePOS. It reads like a third party slipped in. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J2 (9): Very honest overall: the offline test and the 200-send test are linked, and the limitations page is plain. The one gap is that the home "What it does today" cards lead with what works, and the refund gap shows only as one table row, "No, not on plugin 0.3.0". _[home.desktop.02 "Live demo or your own store" table]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The 'Keeps selling offline' card claims 'Our acceptance run makes 25 sales, 20 of them offline' but links only to the spec file ('Read the acceptance test'), not to a passing run, so the claim can't be checked from the page. _[home/desktop/01, 'What it does today' first card]_
- J5 (7): 'MIT licensed' is the footer headline, but 'Before you start' says self-hosting the till needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and never says that is a paid, proprietary licence or what it costs. Nothing says who runs app.vendurepos.com, on what terms or for how long. The only operator hint is an unexplained third-party domain, 'ph.wcpos.com'. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 6 and the demo table's last row; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The quick start is complete and ends with a clear 'Check it worked' list. In step 5, though, a collapsible 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' points at a domain never mentioned anywhere else, which reads as a leftover. It is also one more detour in a nine-step path that already includes migration scripts and tax strategies. _[quick-start.desktop.02 (bottom) / quick-start.phone.05]_
- J2 (8): The developer path is thorough and has success checks. But for the owner, a return can't be finished without the developer: "the till cannot refund. Ask whoever manages your Vendure store to refund the order in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund then shows as Pending until they settle it", and settling needs a GraphQL settleRefund mutation. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 "Refund a sale"; plugin-setup "Settle a Dashboard refund"]_
- J3 (9): Step 6 recommends different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but tells stores mixing these modes only that they 'must account for the store-wide strategy', leaving the configuration decision unresolved. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; shots/quick-start.phone.06.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Step 4 hides the till role's permissions in one dense sentence with seven inline code pills ('TallyPosSell, ReadCatalog, ReadSettings, ReadCustomer and CreateCustomer ... Also add ReadOrder'). On phone it runs to a 10-line paragraph where the permission you must not miss (ReadOrder) is easy to skip. _[quick-start/desktop/02 and quick-start/phone/05, '4. Enable bearer authentication']_
- J5 (8): The device-key path ships with a known gap. The plugin's own 'New POS till key' creates the vendurepos-pos-till role 'except ReadOrder', so after following the steps the till's order history is refused until you edit the role by hand. Step 4 also asks for a role with five named permissions plus ReadOrder but gives no Dashboard steps to create it on the quick-start page. _[quick-start text 'Optional: a device key for each till' and step 4 (quick-start.desktop.02)]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): Past the hero, the home page speaks to someone who already runs a Vendure server. Its 'Before you start' section leads with 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', Postgres versions and an 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. A visitor who only half knows what Vendure is cannot tell whether this is for them or what it takes to use it. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04-05, 'Before you start']_ Fix: Open 'Before you start' with one plain sentence: 'You need a Vendure store on Postgres; a developer adds our plugin, then any browser becomes a till.' Move the Node range and the RXDB_PREMIUM self-hosting note into the quick start.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): On plugin 0.3.0 a shop owner cannot finish a customer return. "the till cannot refund. Ask whoever manages your Vendure store to refund the order in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund then shows as Pending until they settle it", and settling needs a GraphQL settleRefund mutation in GraphiQL. Every return needs the developer, and the cashier page never gives staff a plain counter procedure. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 "Refund a sale"; limitations; plugin-setup "Settle a Dashboard refund"]_ Fix: Put a short owner-facing "Returns on plugin 0.3.0" box at the top of the At the till refunds section. It should say what staff do at the counter right now (give the money back, note the order code), who settles the refund, and link the developer steps. Move "Refund at the till (live demo)" off the cashier page, or label it clearly as demo-only.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with mixed tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing must still guess how to select the single store-wide order tax strategy. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Add an explicit recommendation and verification steps for mixed pricing modes, or clearly state that this configuration is not validated and identify the supported setup.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On the phone home page, the comparison table that answers 'demo or my store?' stacks with its hierarchy upside down. Row titles are regular weight and the repeated column labels 'Live demo:' / 'Your Vendure store:' are bold, so seven rows read as one undifferentiated list of bold labels and the important 'No, not on plugin 0.3.0' refund row doesn't stand out. _[home/phone/04]_ Fix: In the stacked layout, set each row title in semibold at a slightly larger size with a little space above it, and set the 'Live demo:' / 'Your Vendure store:' labels in regular muted text.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells 'MIT licensed' and 'open-source' but leaves a client with two undisclosed dependencies. One is a hosted till at app.vendurepos.com with no named operator, terms or support statement. The other is self-hosting that needs an 'RxDB Premium licence token', never said to be a paid proprietary licence. A sceptic advising a shop cannot tell what it will cost or who they depend on. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' (bullet 'The till's source is MIT licensed...'); connect-a-till.desktop.01; limitations 'Setting up the store']_ Fix: Add one plain line on home and in Limitations: 'RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence from RxDB (link to its pricing); app.vendurepos.com is run free by <named maintainer/company> with no SLA.'
