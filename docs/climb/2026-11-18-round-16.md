   add one. Show the Vendure config, cited from the app's own dev store and `barcode-field.ts`.

## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The hero has four equal-sized buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), and the top nav repeats 'Try with your store'. A newcomer can't tell how 'Try with your store' differs from 'Quick start'. It opens app.vendurepos.com, which only works once the plugin is installed; the small line underneath ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first') admits this. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons and top nav; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap to 2x2)]_
- J2 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'); 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' look like the same thing to a shop owner, and the line under them ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first') is the only clue to which comes first. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap to two rows)]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero carries four CTAs (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub) where three outlined secondaries have identical weight, so 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' compete for the second click; the caption under them has to explain that the store button needs the plugin first. _[home/desktop/01 hero button row; home/phone/01]_
- J5 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub). 'Try with your store' opens app.vendurepos.com, which cannot work until the plugin is installed, so a visitor who clicks it before reading the small caveat line lands on a sign-in screen that will fail. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons and the line 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The home page says 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed' without any caveat. Only the Limitations page says 'Building the till yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM', a paid dependency a visitor reading 'open-source' would not expect. _[home.desktop.02 footer vs limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3]_
- J2 (7): Nowhere does the site say which devices or browsers the till is meant to run on (iPad Safari? an Android tablet? a Windows PC with Chrome?). It also never warns that offline sales not yet sent exist only in that one browser's storage ('kept in SQLite databases inside this browser'), so a cleared browser or a dead tablet could lose them. _[text/limitations.txt (only 'Web till only for now' and the private-window line); text/connect-a-till.txt 'Stored in this browser']_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The payment-screen screenshot on At the till shows a cart line named 'Tally Fixture Mug', a test-fixture product name, in an image captioned 'The payment screen on the live demo till.' _[at-the-till/desktop/02 screenshot, first cart line]_
- J5 (6): The home page sells an 'open-source till' that is 'MIT licensed', but only Limitations and Connect a till say that building the till yourself 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM', a paid proprietary dependency. Nothing says who runs app.vendurepos.com, whether it is free, or whether it will stay up. Alongside this, three features (till refunds, customer note, ReadOrder in the till role) wait on 'the plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet' with no date, and the newest changelog entry is 2026-10-06, six weeks before capture. A sceptic reads that as either a stalled project or a hosted lock-in. _[home.desktop.02 footer 'MIT licensed'; limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Building it yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token'; changelog.desktop.01 '0.3.0 (2026-10-06)']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.6)
- J1 (8): Before step 1, the quick start gives two addresses for the same app ('The hosted POS is live at https://app.vendurepos.com. https://vendurepos.vercel.app serves the same build'), and step 5 brings vercel.app up again. Step 8 also lists the Vendure checks twice: item h, then the 'Check it worked' list straight after. _[quick-start.desktop.01 intro paragraph; quick-start.desktop.03 step 8 (item h and 'Check it worked')]_
- J2 (7): Barcode scanning depends on 'an existing single text ProductVariant custom field, such as barcode', but neither the quick start nor Connect a till says how to create that field if the store has none. A shop with a scanner is left guessing, or the field is left blank and scanning by barcode is switched off. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8c; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Enter your store details']_
- J3 (7): Quick start's staff and device-key instructions omit the complete cashier permission list and the manual ReadOrder addition that Connect a till explicitly requires for the current 0.3.0 role. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 4 and Optional: a device key for each till; text/connect-a-till.txt, Give each cashier their own account]_
- J4 (8): Step 8, the point where the till takes its first real sale, has no image of success: item h ('check Vendure for the POS prices, settled tally-pos payment, delivered fulfilment, stock reduction and tallySaleAt') is repeated straight away as the five-bullet 'Check it worked' list, and neither shows the synced order in the Vendure Dashboard. _[quick-start/desktop/03 step 8; quick-start/phone/07-08]_
- J5 (8): Quick start step 4 says the signing-in administrator needs to read the catalogue and settings and have TallyPosSell or CreateOrder. ## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): The hero presents four equal-weight next steps, and 'Try with your store' sends a first-time visitor to the app's sign-in. That sign-in can't work until someone has installed the plugin on a Vendure server, so a visitor who doesn't know Vendure is offered a dead end next to the one action that suits them, the live demo. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero buttons and top nav 'Try with your store']_ Fix: Keep 'Live demo' as the primary button and 'Quick start' as the secondary one. Remove 'Try with your store' from the hero and nav, or rename it 'Open the app (plugin required)' and show it only after the quick start.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): A shop owner deciding whether staff can sell tomorrow cannot find out what to run the till on (which tablets, computers and browsers are supported or tested). They also aren't warned that unsent offline sales live only in that browser, so clearing site data, using a different browser or losing the device before sync loses those sales. _[text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Where your data goes'; home 'Before you start']_ Fix: Add one line to Before you start and Limitations naming the tested browsers and devices (for example 'Tested on Chrome on Windows/Android and Safari on iPad'). Add a bullet to Limitations: 'Sales not yet sent live only in this browser: don't clear site data or switch browsers until the header shows nothing waiting to send.'
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Following Quick start alone leaves a developer without the full cashier permissions or the documented ReadOrder workaround for plugin 0.3.0, so the role setup does not cover the till's order list. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 4 and Optional: a device key for each till; shots/connect-a-till.desktop.02.jpg]_ Fix: Link step 4 directly to the complete cashier-role instructions and state that plugin 0.3.0 requires adding ReadOrder manually. Repeat that requirement in the optional device-key instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C3): The quick start's payoff step never shows what success looks like. Step 8 is a nine-item lettered list a–i whose item h is then repeated as the 'Check it worked' bullets, with no screenshot of the synced order in the Vendure Dashboard. On a 9,947 px phone page made of prose and code, the one moment a developer needs visual confirmation is plain text, and it is stated twice. _[quick-start/desktop/03 step 8; quick-start/phone/07-08]_ Fix: Drop item h from the a–i list and put one captioned screenshot of the synced POS order in the Vendure Dashboard under 'Check it worked' (prices, tally-pos settled, fulfilment delivered, tallySaleAt visible), keeping the five checks as its caption.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site leads with 'open-source till' and 'MIT licensed', but the till can only be self-built with a paid RxDB Premium licence. Nothing says who operates the hosted app.vendurepos.com or on what terms. Several promised features wait on an undated 'plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', and the last release was six weeks before capture. A consultant would warn a client that the open-source promise has a catch and that the project's momentum is unproven. _[home.desktop.02 footer and 'Before you start'; limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3; changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one line to the home page 'Before you start': 'The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free to use (run by …); self-building the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence', linked to Limitations. Also give the next plugin release a target date or a link to its tracking issue wherever 'the release after 0.3.0' is mentioned.
