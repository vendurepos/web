## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.0)
- J1 (7): The home page never says what Vendure is or links to it: the hero says "Point of sale for Vendure" and "signs in to your own Vendure store", but nowhere does it say it is an open-source headless commerce framework (tech.json home links include no vendure.io; that link only appears on /docs). A visitor who half knows Vendure has to guess whether this is for them. The hero also gives four buttons of nearly equal weight (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub), and the line under them has to warn that "Try with your store" will not work without the plugin. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01; tech.json pages.home.desktop.links]_
- J2 (8): Four buttons of near-equal weight ("Live demo", "Quick start", "Try with your store", "GitHub") wrap 2+2 on the phone, and the hero image of the till below them is too small to read at 390 px: the product names, cart lines and total can't be made out, so on a phone between customers I can't see what my staff would be looking at. _[home.phone.01 (buttons and hero screenshot)]_
- J3 (9): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; text/home.txt]_
- J4 (8): The hero has four CTAs at nearly equal weight ('Live demo' filled, then 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub' as identical outlined boxes). On a 390 px screen they wrap into a ragged 2+2 block: the second row ('Try with your store' + 'GitHub') is wider than the first, so the next step after the demo is not clear. _[home.desktop.01 hero button row; home.phone.01 button block]_
- J5 (8): The hero gives four buttons of near-equal weight ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), and on a phone they wrap 2+2. 'Try with your store' sends a visitor to app.vendurepos.com, which cannot work until the plugin is installed; the warning about that is a small grey line underneath. _[home.desktop.01 hero CTAs; home.phone.01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The product is VendurePOS, but the visitor meets other names without explanation: the home footer says "Built with Tally UI", step 2 of the quick start is "Add TallyPosPlugin", and the routes are /tally/v1/... Nothing says how Tally relates to VendurePOS, so it reads like a rebrand or a wrapper around someone else's product. _[home.desktop.02 footer; quick-start.desktop.01 step 2]_
- J2 (8): The offline promise ("Sales made with the network off wait on the till", "Unsent orders ... wait on that device") never says what happens to unsent sales if that browser's data is cleared or the device breaks before it reconnects, and Limitations doesn't say either; that is the first risk I'd ask about. _[home.desktop.01 'Keeps selling offline'; text/troubleshooting.txt 'Refresh prices and stock'; text/limitations.txt]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/plugin-setup.txt]_
- J4 (8): The quick start's opening paragraph names a second host, 'https://vendurepos.vercel.app serves the same build', and step 5 has to explain keeping it in CORS. On a page whose job is the first install, that reads as leaked infrastructure, and code names like TallyPosPlugin and tally-pos are never reconciled with the VendurePOS brand. _[quick-start.desktop.01 intro paragraph; quick-start.desktop.02 step 5]_
- J5 (6): The site asks you to type a Vendure administrator email and password into a hosted app ('Open https://app.vendurepos.com ... your administrator email and password'), and even mentions a second host ('https://vendurepos.vercel.app serves the same build'). Nowhere does it say who runs that host, that credentials go only to your store, or whether you can self-host the till. That is the first question I would ask before recommending it to a client. _[quick-start step 8 and intro paragraph (quick-start.desktop.01/03); connect-a-till 'Open your till']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.6)
- J1 (7): The path to a first sale is cluttered with edge cases. Step 7, "Prepare the channel and stock", is one ~150-word paragraph of stock-allocation rules ("Short stock is topped up so the sale goes through, at the strategy's first location or...", "A per-sale locationId is refused"). The intro and step 5 also bring in a second hosted address, https://vendurepos.vercel.app. The few actions you actually need to take are buried among caveats. _[quick-start.desktop.02-03 step 7; quick-start.desktop.01 intro paragraph]_
- J2 (8): The quick start opens with two addresses for the till ("The hosted POS is live at https://app.vendurepos.com. https://vendurepos.vercel.app serves the same build.") and step 5 says to keep allowing the vercel one, so it's unclear which address staff should bookmark at the counter. _[quick-start.desktop.01 intro paragraph; quick-start.desktop.02 step 5]_
- J3 (8): Staff-account setup omits ReadCustomer and CreateCustomer and describes catalogue/settings permissions generically, leaving the explicit till permission list buried in the changelog. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 4; text/connect-a-till.txt, 'Sign in with a staff account'; text/changelog.txt, 0.2.0]_
- J4 (7): Step 7 'Prepare the channel and stock, then restart Vendure' is a solid nine-line paragraph about stockLocationStrategy, top-ups and PaymentSettled, with no list or callout. The one action a developer needs (assign products and stock locations to the channel, keep the manual fulfilment handler, restart) is buried in edge-case prose. _[quick-start.desktop.02 bottom / quick-start.desktop.03 top; quick-start.phone.05-06]_
- J5 (8): Step 3 offers three ways to migrate (a tsx generate script, 'npx vendure migrate', or 'the plugin's bundled migrations' described in the README) without saying which one to pick. Step 8 also nests its own list numbered 1–9 directly above the '9. Troubleshoot' heading, so step numbers collide. _[quick-start.desktop.01 step 3; quick-start.desktop.03 step 8/9]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who searched "Vendure point of sale" and only half knows Vendure gets no one-line explanation of Vendure and no link to it from the home page. The hero assumes you already run a Vendure server. The visitor has to work out from the requirements list further down ("Vendure 3.6 or later on Postgres") whether the product fits them. _[home.desktop.01 hero subhead; home.phone.01]_ Fix: In the hero subhead, call Vendure "Vendure, the open-source TypeScript commerce platform" and link that phrase to vendure.io. Give the hero one primary action (Live demo) and one secondary (Quick start), and move "Try with your store" and GitHub out of the hero button row.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): Refunds are plainly listed as "not yet", but no page tells staff what to do when a customer brings something back today. A returned item at the counter is the first moment where my staff would be stuck, and neither At the till nor Limitations gives a workaround or says how it affects the drawer count and Z report. _[text/limitations.txt 'Refunds: not yet.'; home.desktop.02 'Not yet: refunds and coupon codes.'; at-the-till.desktop.01-02 (no returns section)]_ Fix: Add a short 'A customer wants a refund' line to At the till and Limitations that says what to do today (for example, refund the order in the Vendure Dashboard and record any cash handed back with Paid out so the drawer count still matches).
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): A developer resolving a partially kept sale must guess whether applied or rejected is appropriate because the recovery instructions provide neither decision rules nor the checks required before resolving it. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, corresponding section]_ Fix: Document the checks on the existing order and the conditions for choosing each resolution, including what each outcome changes. Add a copyable tallyNeedsAdminCommands query exposing the information needed for that decision.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a phone the quick-start code blocks are cut off at the right edge, exactly where the lines matter: the plugin line ends at 'Tally', the CORS origin line at "'ht", and the taxOptions lines in mid-class-name. The only scroll cue is a faint shadow, so a developer reading the install path on a 390 px screen sees broken config. _[quick-start.phone.02, quick-start.phone.04, quick-start.phone.05]_ Fix: Write the long snippets as one item per line (break the plugins array, the cors origin array and the taxOptions values onto separate lines), or turn on line wrapping for code blocks below 640 px so no line is wider than the 390 px viewport.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The recommended path is to sign in to the hosted app.vendurepos.com (or a vercel.app mirror) with a Vendure administrator's email and password. The site never says who operates that host, that credentials and orders go straight from the browser to your own store, or how to run the till yourself. A sceptical adviser would stop there. _[quick-start.desktop.01 intro and quick-start.desktop.03 step 8; connect-a-till 'Open your till']_ Fix: Add a short 'Where your data goes' note to the Overview and quick start step 8: the till is a static app, credentials and orders go only from the browser to your store, nothing is sent to vendurepos.com. Link to how to self-host the till from the MIT source, or state plainly that self-hosting is not documented yet.
