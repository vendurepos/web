## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 7.6)
- J1 (7): The home page never shows the till: no screenshot or image of the product anywhere above or below the fold, so a visitor who half knows Vendure has to read ten text cards or leave the site (Live demo) just to see what 'an open-source till that runs in the browser' looks like; Vendure itself is never explained or linked on the home page either. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero and 'What it does today']_
- J2 (7): The home page has no picture of the till: the hero is a heading, a paragraph and four same-sized buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), then ten text cards. A shop owner can't see what staff would use at the counter without leaving for the demo. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01 (hero and 'What it does today')]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero has four near-equal buttons ("Live demo", "Quick start", "Try with your store", "GitHub") and no picture of the till anywhere above or below the fold. To see what the product looks like you have to leave the site, and "Quick start" and "Try with your store" read as two competing next steps. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap 2+2)]_
- J5 (7): A point-of-sale landing page with no picture of the till anywhere: the hero is a headline, a paragraph and four equal-weight buttons ("Live demo", "Quick start", "Try with your store", "GitHub"), then ten identical text cards. Unlike Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed pages, you have to click away to see what the cashier screen looks like. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 (hero and 'What it does today')]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The quick start names two hosts for the till, 'The hosted POS is live at https://app.vendurepos.com. https://vendurepos.vercel.app serves the same build.', and step 5 tells stores to keep allowing the vercel.app origin, which makes a newcomer wonder which address is the official one and reads like an unfinished migration. _[quick-start.desktop.01 intro paragraph; quick-start.desktop.02 step 5]_
- J2 (8): Neither the home page nor the Limitations page says which counter hardware works. 'Print the receipt from the browser' never says whether a thermal receipt printer or cash drawer works, or which tablets and browsers are supported. 'Refunds: not yet' sits only on Limitations, behind the small 'what it does not do yet' link at the bottom of the home page. _[home.desktop.01 card 'Cash, card and receipts'; limitations.desktop.01; home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/plugin-setup.txt]_
- J4 (8): The Limitations page is one flat list of 12 equal-weight bullets. Hard gaps ("Refunds: not yet.", "Coupon codes: not yet.") sit at the same level as behaviour notes ("an open till picks up a price change within about a minute"), so the real gaps don't stand out. _[limitations.desktop.01 / limitations.phone.01]_
- J5 (7): The home page's 'What it does today' and 'Before you start' never say that it has no refunds, no coupon codes and no integrated card terminal, which rule it out for most real shops. Those only appear behind the small 'See what it does not do yet' link on the Limitations page, so the home page reads as complete when it is not. _[home.desktop.01-02 vs limitations.desktop.01 ('Refunds: not yet.', 'Coupon codes: not yet.', 'The till does not talk to the terminal.')]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.6)
- J1 (7): Step 4 'Enable bearer authentication' is one line of required config buried under optional material (API keys, the New POS till key dialog, sign-in with Device key, a pnpm Vite-plugin fix), so the required path and the optional device-key path are mixed in one numbered step; step 8 then lists the same Vendure checks twice (in the paragraph and again under 'Check it worked'). _[quick-start.desktop.02 step 4; quick-start.desktop.03 step 8]_
- J2 (8): Quick start step 8, 'Sign in and make a sale', packs sign-in, the opening float, scanning, tender, printing and the offline test into one dense paragraph. The optional device-key setup takes up most of step 4 ahead of the main path. _[quick-start.desktop.03 (step 8 paragraph 'Wait for the catalogue and settings...'); quick-start.desktop.02 (step 4)]_
- J3 (8): Staff-account setup names catalogue/settings reads and TallyPosSell or CreateOrder but omits ReadCustomer and CreateCustomer, leaving the complete till permissions to be discovered in the changelog. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 4; text/connect-a-till.txt, Sign in with a staff account; text/changelog.txt, 0.2.0]_
- J4 (7): Step 8 "Sign in and make a sale" is two dense prose paragraphs (store URL, channel token, barcode field, float, scan, tender, receipt, sync check, offline test) where numbered sub-steps belong. The intro sentence "https://app.vendurepos.com. https://vendurepos.vercel.app serves the same build." has two URL pills split by a full stop, so it's unclear which one to open. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8; quick-start.desktop.01 intro; quick-start.phone.07]_
- J5 (8): Step 3 asks the developer to write two TypeScript entry files and run them 'with your project's existing TypeScript runner' without naming a command. It then says 'the README describes that alternative' with no link to that README (quick-start's link list has no README URL, while plugin-setup does link it). _[quick-start.desktop.01 (step 3, last paragraph) / text/quick-start.txt]_

### C4 Docs quality (mean 7.4)
- J1 (8): Changelog entries cite internal references that go nowhere for a reader: '(ADR 0005)', '(ADR 0004)', '(#116)', '(#117)' appear as plain unlinked text, and terms like 'order.create v5' are never explained in the docs. _[changelog.desktop.01, 0.3.0 and 0.2.0 sections]_
- J2 (8): Selling offline, the owner's main worry, has no page of its own. It is a two-line section, 'Sell while offline', inside Troubleshooting. 'At the till' never says what staff see when the connection drops, or what happens to unsent sales if the browser data is cleared. The changelog is written for developers ('order.create v5 ... (ADR 0005)', 'lineTax: { none: true, classes: true }'). _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 'Sell while offline'; at-the-till.desktop.01; changelog.desktop.01]_
- J3 (7): Limitations says sales take stock from the channel's default location, while quick start says they use Vendure's allocation strategy and the changelog explicitly advertises multi-location allocation. _[text/limitations.txt; text/quick-start.txt, step 7; text/changelog.txt, 0.1.0; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt]_
- J4 (7): The user-facing pages describe every till screen only in words, with bold UI labels ("Cash in the drawer to start", "Parked (n)", "Card payment approved", header notices quoted in text) and not one screenshot. A reader can't match the docs to the screen, and the troubleshooting header states are monospace pills, not images of the header. _[at-the-till.desktop.01; troubleshooting.desktop.01 "Read the sync status in the header"]_
- J5 (7): The changelog and Plugin setup sell 0.3.0 as 'lets the plugin record fees, shipping and custom lines', but no user-facing page (At the till, home, Limitations) says whether or how the till uses them. Changelog entries also cite '(ADR 0005)', '#116' and '#117' as plain text with no links. _[changelog.desktop.01 (0.3.0 entries) and plugin-setup.desktop.01 vs at-the-till.desktop.01]_

- J1 (7): The home page is entirely text: ten identical grey cards in a 2x5 grid plus a table, with no image, icon or visual anchor, so it reads like a generic docs template rather than a product page; the docs sidebar's grey panel also stops at about 900 px, leaving a white strip below it in the full-page captures. _[home.desktop.01 cards; docs.desktop.01 / quick-start.desktop.01 sidebar bottom]_
- J2 (7): The whole site is text and grey boxes, with no product image, icon or illustration anywhere. On the home page the 'Live demo or your own store' table has a narrow first column that wraps 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' while the 'Live demo' column holds wide empty space. _[home.desktop.01 (cards and comparison table); home.desktop-dark.jpg]_
- J3 (9): The overview sidebar highlights both Docs and Overview as active destinations, giving the current-page indicator an unnecessary duplicate. _[shots/docs.desktop.01.jpg]_
- J4 (7): The home page is ten identical grey text cards in a 2x5 grid with no imagery, icons or change of rhythm, followed by a comparison table whose widths are off: the "Live demo" column is ~430 px wide for one-word "Yes" cells while the first column wraps "Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline" onto two lines. That table is also borderless while the docs tax table is a bordered box, so table styles are inconsistent. _[home.desktop.01 (cards y=578-1284, table y=1450-1743); quick-start.desktop.03 tax table]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor from search never sees the product: the home page has no screenshot of the till, so in the first minute it is only text cards and four equally weighted buttons, and the only way to see the till is to leave for the demo. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01, hero section]_ Fix: Put one real screenshot of the till (cart and catalogue, in light and dark) directly under the hero buttons, linked to the live demo, with a one-line note that Vendure is an open-source headless commerce platform.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): Nowhere on the site, home page or 'At the till' docs, is there a picture of the till. A shop owner asking 'can my staff sell with this tomorrow?' only gets paragraphs about buttons ('Pay cash', 'Close register', 'Print the Z report') and has to open the demo to see anything. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero; at-the-till.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one screenshot of the till mid-sale (cart, tender buttons, sync status in the header) under the hero buttons, and a screenshot of the Z report in 'Count the drawer and close the day'.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The documentation gives conflicting answers about whether POS sales deduct stock only from the channel's default location or follow Vendure's allocation strategy across multiple locations. _[text/limitations.txt; text/quick-start.txt, step 7; text/changelog.txt, 0.1.0; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt]_ Fix: Reconcile these passages around the actual allocation behavior, distinguishing server-side allocation from the till's inability to select a per-sale location. Carry the same wording into llms-full.txt.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): A point-of-sale product is presented with no picture of the till anywhere on the site. The home page goes straight from the hero to a wall of ten identical grey text cards, and the At the till docs describe screens only in words, so the page has no visual anchor and the product's look is never shown. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01-03; at-the-till.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one framed screenshot of the till (cart plus tender panel, with light and dark variants) between the hero buttons and "What it does today", and reuse crops of it in At the till for the register, tender and header-status states.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The home page lists ten things it does but none of the dealbreakers a shop owner checks first: no refunds, no coupon codes, and card payments keyed in by hand from a separate terminal. A buyer comparing it with Square or Shopify POS learns this only by finding the Limitations page, which looks like burying the limits. _[home.desktop.01-02 ('What it does today', 'Before you start') vs limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one line under 'Before you start' (or a short 'Not yet' card beside the feature grid): 'Not yet: refunds, coupon codes, integrated card terminals (card is recorded from your own terminal).', linked to Limitations.
