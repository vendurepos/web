### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The home page's 'Live demo or your own store' table puts admin jargon in front of a newcomer: the refund cell reads 'The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls; see Plugin setup', four lines of internals in an otherwise Yes/Yes comparison. _[home/desktop/02 (table row 'Refund a sale at the till'); home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): On the phone the top bar shows only "VendurePOS", "Docs" and "Live demo", so "For cashiers: At the till", the page my staff need, is only reachable from the hero button or after opening Docs. Otherwise "Point of sale for Vendure" plus "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0" and "Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first" tells me in five seconds what this is and what I need. _[home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero puts three calls to action side by side: two buttons ("Live demo", "Quick start") and an underlined text link "For cashiers: At the till". A note under them then links "quick start" again, so the eye has four targets before it reaches the screenshot. On phone the text link drops onto its own line below the buttons. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): On the home page, the one sentence about what the demo sends names an unexplained outside domain, 'ph.wcpos.com'. A visitor who knows neither VendurePOS nor WCPOS can't tell who collects the data or who stands behind the project. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04, table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_ Fix: In that cell, add a few words saying who runs ph.wcpos.com and how it relates to VendurePOS (for example 'usage analytics run by the VendurePOS team at WCPOS'), and link it to the existing 'Where your data goes' section.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): A shop owner can't find a plain-language answer to "my closure or cash movement didn't reach Vendure, what now?". The answer is in developer terms: "till update", "refused for its number", the errorCodes register_session_already_open and register_closure_number_invalid. It is split across Limitations and Troubleshooting rather than sitting in At the till's close-the-day section. The manager-approval "till's limit" is never located either. _[limitations.desktop.01; troubleshooting.desktop.01; at-the-till.desktop.04]_ Fix: Add a short box under At the till's "Close the day" section headed "If the closure doesn't reach your store": keep the printed Z report, what the till shows, and who to call (your developer). Link Troubleshooting for the errorCodes. Also say where the over/short limit for manager approval is set.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels is warned about the shared tax strategy but given no actionable configuration or verification path. _[Quick start, step 6 and Why these pairings; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt]_ Fix: Add a mixed-channel instruction stating the supported strategy choice or explicit support boundary. Include a concrete receipt-versus-order tax-total check for each pricing mode.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The At the till cashier-sheet screenshot is the one screenshot that looks unfinished. On desktop it sits as a small centred card with a large gap above it and a left-hung caption, unlike the full-width payment screenshot on the same page. On phone it carries a grey strip down its left edge and a misaligned "Sync now". That makes the docs' screenshots look inconsistent and the till look half-rendered. _[at-the-till.desktop.03; at-the-till.phone.07]_ Fix: Re-capture the cashier sheet cropped tight to the sheet's own border, with no overlay edge. Render it with the same figure component, width and caption alignment as the payment screen, and make all captions on the site use one alignment.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The hosted till that every store uses (app.vendurepos.com) is an unversioned rolling build ('till app version 0.1.0, built 20 Nov 2026 from … commit 7bd49a1') with no release notes, and the only alternative, self-hosting, depends on a paid RxDB Premium licence whose cost is never stated. A client cannot tell what changes under their cashiers or what leaving would cost, so I would advise against it for a live shop. _[changelog/desktop/01 intro; home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store']_ Fix: Add a 'Till app' section to the Changelog with dated entries and a sentence on how app.vendurepos.com updates (and whether a store can pin a version). Add one line linking RxDB Premium's licence page with its price, so the self-host route has a known cost.
