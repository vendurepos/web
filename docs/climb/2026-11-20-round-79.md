## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero leans on words a newcomer may not know: the badge says "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0" before the visitor knows there is a plugin, and the intro calls Vendure a "headless commerce framework" and the product a "till" without saying what either means. The first feature card, "Keeps selling offline", opens with its caveats ("a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them") before the reader knows why offline selling matters. _[home.desktop.01 (hero badge, intro paragraph, 'Keeps selling offline' card)]_
- J2 (8): The hero's two buttons are "Live demo" and "Quick start" (a developer install); the owner/cashier guide appears only as the closing sentence "For cashiers, At the till covers selling, taking payment and closing the day.", and on phone the header shows just Docs and Live demo, with Quick start and At the till hidden behind the chevron. _[home/desktop/01 (hero CTAs), home/phone/01 (header nav), text/home.txt last paragraph]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero lede under 'Point of sale for Vendure' is a 60-word, four-line centred paragraph (six ragged lines on phone). Centred body copy that long is slow to scan in five seconds, and a second centred note under the buttons ('Your own store needs the plugin…') adds more grey text to the fold. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and who it is for, but nothing on the home page tells a buyer who should NOT pick it over Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed (no hardware integrations, desktop Chrome only, no card-terminal link); that is only implied by scattered limits further down. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.txt 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the demo "sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com". wcpos.com is named nowhere else on the site, so a first-time visitor sees an unexplained third-party domain receiving data and may start to distrust the site. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (9): The demo-vs-own-store table's refund row says the refund "stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", which leaves a non-developer owner unsure who settles refunds day to day; separately, the home "Barcode scanners" card mentions only keyboard-wedge scanners while At the till also offers "the device's camera". _[home/desktop/02 (refund row), home/phone/04; at-the-till text]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The comparison table's demo cell says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. A third-party-looking domain (wcpos) is named with no word on why a VendurePOS demo reports there, which is the one line that reads as something unexplained. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J5 (7): The site never says who builds or maintains VendurePOS, whether app.vendurepos.com is free and will stay up, or why demo analytics go to ph.wcpos.com; and 'MIT licensed' / JSON-LD isAccessibleForFree sit next to 'you need your own RxDB Premium licence token' without saying that is a paid commercial licence, so the 'open-source' till can in practice only be run from the project's hosted app. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store' + footer 'MIT licensed'; home table 'ph.wcpos.com'; connect-a-till.txt 'Where requests go'; tech.json home jsonLd]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): Step 5 ends with a collapsed note, "If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app", about a domain the guide never introduces. It reads like a leftover from an old deployment and makes the reader wonder which origin is the right one. _[quick-start.desktop.03 top (step 5); quick-start.phone.06]_
- J2 (8): Quick start step 3 has the reader create src/generate-migration.ts and src/run-migrations.ts, then says "include it in config.dbConnectionOptions.migrations" without showing that config edit, which is the one guess the freelancer has to make; step 5's collapsed "If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app" is also unexplained at first read. _[quick-start/desktop/01-02 (step 3), quick-start/desktop/03 (step 5 disclosure)]_
- J3 (8): The tax instructions prescribe different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but tell mixed-mode stores only to 'account for the store-wide strategy', leaving the configuration decision unresolved. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:893]_
- J4 (9): Five blocks come before '1. Install the plugin': the 'Try it first' callout, three paragraphs (hosted URL, @vendure/create, Node) and the 'Before a sale on a live store' warning. Step 1 starts about 740 px down on desktop and only on the second phone slice. _[quick-start.desktop.01; quick-start.phone.01-02]_
- J5 (8): Scanning needs a ProductVariant 'barcode' custom field, but the quick start only mentions it at step 8c ('how to add one'), after the step 3 migration has been generated and run, so a developer who wants scanning has to generate and run a second migration and restart; it belongs before step 3. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8c; connect-a-till.txt 'If your store has no such field']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C5): A first-time visitor who skims for under a minute gets one product screenshot and then a long run of plain grey text. Eight of the ten features have no card or visual, and the table and bullet lists below are text only. Nothing beyond the hero shows the claims, such as a Z report, a receipt or the offline 'orders waiting to send' state, so the page reads as a spec sheet rather than a product. _[home.desktop.01 bottom through home.desktop.02; home.phone.02-04]_ Fix: Give the eight plain features the same card treatment as the first two and add two or three small screenshots from the demo (offline banner, Z report, receipt) beside the matching features.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): A shop owner who wants to know "can my staff sell with this tomorrow and how do we close the day" gets no button for it: both hero CTAs go to the demo or the developer quick start, the cashier guide "At the till" is only a sentence at the bottom of home, and on phone it is hidden behind the nav chevron. _[home/desktop/01, home/phone/01]_ Fix: Add a third hero link, "For your staff: At the till", beside Live demo and Quick start, and show At the till in the phone header instead of behind the chevron.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to reconcile the two recommended strategies with the single store-wide setting. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:893]_ Fix: Document the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and how to verify totals in both channels. If that configuration is unsupported, state the restriction explicitly.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a 390 px screen the hero image, the first visual a phone visitor sees, does not show what its caption promises: it is the Products tab with two cropped tiles and a chip clipped to 'Gif', and the cart holding the €20.09 sale is hidden behind a tab. _[home.phone.01]_ Fix: For phone, use a capture of the Cart tab (three lines, total, Checkout €20.09), or crop to show the cart. Or change the phone caption to match what is shown.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A sceptic advising a client cannot tell who stands behind VendurePOS, whether the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free and supported, or what self-hosting costs: 'MIT licensed' is headlined while the only way to build the till yourself needs an RxDB Premium licence the site never says is paid, and the ph.wcpos.com domain hints at an unnamed parent project. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store' and footer; connect-a-till.txt 'Where requests go']_ Fix: Add one 'Who runs this' line to the home page and Overview naming the maintainer, saying app.vendurepos.com is free with no account, and stating that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence, so self-hosting the till has that cost.
