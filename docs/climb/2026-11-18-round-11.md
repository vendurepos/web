## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero has four equal-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). 'Try with your store' goes straight to app.vendurepos.com, which is useless until the plugin is installed. The site admits this only in the small line under the buttons ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'), so a newcomer has three competing next steps, one of them a dead end. _[home.desktop.01 (hero buttons) and home.phone.01 (buttons wrap 2+2)]_
- J2 (8): The home page never links to the staff guide 'At the till' (selling, refunds, 'Count the drawer and close the day'): the hero buttons are Live demo / Quick start / Try with your store / GitHub, and the 'Registers and Z reports' card has no link, so an owner asking 'can my staff sell and how do I close the day' has no next click. _[home.desktop.01/02; tech.json home links (no /docs/at-the-till)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero gives four buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). Three of them look the same, so 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' compete as the second choice. On a phone the row wraps 2+2, and the second row ('Try with your store', 'GitHub') is wider than the first, so the button block looks ragged. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons; home.phone.01 button rows]_
- J5 (8): The home page never says who builds or maintains VendurePOS or who operates app.vendurepos.com (the only names are the footer's 'Built with Tally UI' and 'For Medusa, see MedusaPOS'). It also never says who should not use it, or how it compares with Shopify POS, Square or the WooCommerce/Odoo tills. On the phone the hero product screenshot is too small to read. _[home.desktop.01 and the footer; home.phone.01; text/home.txt]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That domain has no visible connection to VendurePOS. Neither the home page nor Connect a till ('a self-hosted PostHog analytics server') says who wcpos is, so a stranger sees their demo data going to an unexplained third-party domain. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go']_
- J2 (8): Refunds depend on 'the plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', repeated on home, limitations and at-the-till with no version number or expected date, and the site never says plainly whether a receipt printer or cash drawer works beyond 'print the receipt from the browser'. _[home text 'Before you start'; limitations; at-the-till (Refund, Receipt)]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The package is @vendurepos/plugin, but quick-start step 2 says 'Add TallyPosPlugin' and then uses /tally/v1, tally-pos and TallyPosSell. The page never says why the names differ, so the rename looks unfinished. Only the Overview has a passing 'built on TallyUI'. _[quick-start.desktop.01 step 2 heading; text/quick-start.txt]_
- J5 (5): The home page sells it as 'MIT licensed', but Limitations says 'Building the till yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM'. Self-hosting the till therefore needs a paid proprietary licence; otherwise you depend on the hosted app.vendurepos.com, run by an unnamed operator. The home page's 'Before you start' list and its 'Live demo or your own store' table never mention this. _[limitations.phone.01 (third bullet) and text/limitations.txt vs text/home.txt footer 'MIT licensed']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): Step 3 of the quick start gives three ways to migrate: a hand-written src/generate-migration.ts run with tsx, 'npx vendure migrate' for @vendure/create projects, and the plugin's bundled migrations ('Do not also run the plugin's bundled migrations ... the README describes that alternative'), which Plugin setup then lays out in full. The reader has to choose without a stated recommendation. _[quick-start.desktop.01 step 3; plugin-setup.desktop.01 'Configure the plugin and migrations']_
- J2 (8): Quick start step 8 signs in with an admin email and password. The cashier role needs ReadOrder added by hand ('which that role gains only in the plugin release after 0.3.0; until then, add it to the role yourself'), but that is said only on Connect a till, so a freelancer following the quick start for a cashier login hits it unwarned. _[quick-start.desktop.03 / phone.07-08 step 8; connect-a-till 'Give each cashier their own account']_
- J3 (8): Observed: Quick start's device-key instructions omit the manual ReadOrder permission adjustment that Connect a till explicitly requires for the recommended plugin 0.3.0. _[text/quick-start.txt, Optional: a device key for each till; shots/connect-a-till.desktop.02.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Step 3 (migration) puts two commands, 'npx tsx src/generate-migration.ts' and 'npx tsx src/run-migrations.ts', inline in a long paragraph full of code chips (eight in the run-it paragraph alone) between two code blocks. Every other command gets a Terminal block. On a phone the run command even breaks across two lines. _[quick-start.desktop.01 step 3 paragraph 'Run it with npx tsx…'; quick-start.phone.03 top]_
- J5 (7): Quick-start step 4 says the till sign-in needs catalogue/settings read plus TallyPosSell or CreateOrder. Connect a till says 'The till's order list needs ReadOrder, which that role gains only in the plugin release after 0.3.0; until then, add it to the role yourself'. A developer who follows only the quick start gets a till whose Orders list fails. _[text/quick-start.txt step 4 vs text/connect-a-till.txt]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor gets four equal-weight hero buttons. 'Try with your store' opens the app's sign-in, which cannot work without the server plugin, and the warning about that is a small grey line below the buttons, not on the button itself. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero button row]_ Fix: Keep 'Live demo' as the primary button and make 'Quick start' the only secondary one. Move 'Try with your store' to the end of the quick start (or label it 'Open the app (plugin required)'), and leave GitHub in the header.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): The home page has no route to 'At the till', the one page that answers a shop owner's real questions (how staff sell, refund, hand over and close the day with an X/Z report); every home link points at developer setup, GitHub tests or npm. _[home.desktop.01/02 hero buttons and 'Registers and Z reports' card; tech.json home links]_ Fix: Make the 'Registers and Z reports' card link to /docs/at-the-till, and add one line under 'Before you start': 'For staff: At the till — selling, refunds and closing the day.'
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The needs_admin instructions leave the developer without documented criteria for choosing applied versus rejected when recovering a partially retained sale. _[shots/troubleshooting.desktop.02.jpg, Resolve a sale the store kept part-way]_ Fix: Add a complete tallyNeedsAdminCommands query and explain which persisted order, payment and stock state warrants each resolution. State how to verify recovery before retrying the sale.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a 390 px phone, the till screenshots that should prove the product (home hero, At the till payment and cashier sheet) are 1440-wide captures shrunk to about 325 px. Every label, price and button in them is illegible, so on a phone the visual proof turns into grey noise. _[home.phone.01 hero screenshot; at-the-till.phone.03 payment screenshot]_ Fix: At narrow widths, serve a cropped or phone-width capture of the till through <picture>/srcset (the cart and checkout pane for the hero, the payment pane for At the till), and make each screenshot open full size on tap.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Home sells an 'MIT licensed' open-source till, yet building it yourself needs a paid RxDB Premium licence ('needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM'), and the alternative is a hosted app.vendurepos.com whose operator is never named. A sceptic finds this on the Limitations page and reads it as a hidden lock-in, which is the point at which they would advise a client against it. _[text/limitations.txt / limitations.phone.01 vs home footer 'MIT licensed' and home 'Before you start']_ Fix: Add one line to the home page's 'Before you start' list, next to 'MIT licensed': 'Use the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com (run by <name>), or build it yourself with a paid RxDB Premium licence'. Name the maintainer in the footer.
