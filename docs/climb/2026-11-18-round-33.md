## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear ("Point of sale for Vendure", Live demo / Quick start), but the home page's "Before you start" list drops raw developer jargon on a newcomer: "RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)", "one tab per till and not a private window", "Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome", with nothing saying what RxDB is. _[home/desktop/02 (Before you start), home/phone/05]_
- J2 (8): The hero tells me what it is ('Point of sale for Vendure', 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0') but nothing above the fold says what I must ask my developer to do before staff can sell; on phone the hero till screenshot is cropped so the 'Add charge' button under 'Order discount' is cut off. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Below the clear hero the feature grid's first row (two cards carrying links) is taller than every row under it, so the grid rhythm breaks right where the eye lands after the hero; the 'Before you start' bullets then repeat the docs Overview requirements in a dense list _[home.desktop.01-02]_
- J5 (8): The home page says clearly what this is ("Point of sale for Vendure", "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0"), but it never says who should not use it or how it compares with a hosted till (Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed). It also names no maintainer or company: the footer only says "MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS." The ten identical "What it does today" cards give no sense of which features matter most. _[home.desktop.01-02 (hero, 'What it does today'), home footer]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The footer and hero say "MIT licensed" and "open-source", but a bullet then says self-hosting needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token" and never says whether that costs money or that app.vendurepos.com is free. It reads like a hidden catch. The demo row also sends usage events to "ph.wcpos.com", a different brand that is never introduced. _[home/desktop/02, Before you start bullet 5 and the Live demo table's last row]_
- J2 (8): Honest about refunds, but in owner terms it falls back to developer language: a refund 'stays Pending until it is settled' via 'the Admin API's settleRefund mutation', and the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' with no plain word on what that is. _[limitations.desktop.01 / text/limitations; home 'Live demo or your own store' table (home.desktop.02)]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The at-the-till payment screenshot shows a product named 'Tally Fixture Mug', a test-fixture name that does not match the demo catalogue shown on home and reads as an internal artefact _[at-the-till.desktop.02]_
- J5 (6): The footer says "MIT licensed", and the site presents the till as something you can build and host yourself, yet 'Before you start' says "To build and host it yourself ... you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)". No page says this is a paid, proprietary licence or what it costs. Other doubts: three releases in five days (0.1.0 on 2026-10-01; 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 both on 2026-10-06), then nothing for six weeks. The known "no ReadOrder" role bug is documented in four places instead of being fixed in a 0.3.1. The demo has refund at the till, which the table marks "No, not on plugin 0.3.0", with no note on when or whether it will be released. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; limitations; changelog; home 'Live demo or your own store' table]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor reads "open-source" and "MIT licensed", then a home-page bullet says self-hosting needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)". It never says what RxDB Premium is, whether it costs money, or that using app.vendurepos.com needs no licence, so the free open-source claim looks like it hides a catch. _[home/desktop/02, Before you start bullet 5 (also home/phone/05)]_ Fix: Rewrite the bullet in plain words, e.g. "Using the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free. Building and hosting the till yourself also needs a paid RxDB Premium licence; see Connect a till." Or move it off the home page into Connect a till.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till's 'Count the drawer and close the day' section, the page I would hand staff to learn closing up, has no screenshot of the close screen or the Z report; the page's only pictures are the payment screen and the cashier sheet, so I cannot see what closing the day looks like before trying it. _[at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.phone.06 onward]_ Fix: Add a screenshot of the closure/count screen and of the resulting Z report under 'Count the drawer and close the day', and reword 'leaves the drawer open for the next account' to 'leaves the register session open for the next cashier'.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Stores with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels receive conflicting strategy recommendations without an actionable configuration for their shared store-wide setting. _[Quick start step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings'; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg]_ Fix: Document the supported strategy for mixed-pricing channels, or explicitly state the restriction. Include a verification example showing expected totals in each pricing mode.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): Phone hero till screenshot is cropped: cart header truncated to 'Q...' and the 'Add charge' button cut off under 'Order discount', so the first product image on a phone looks broken _[home.phone.01]_ Fix: Serve a phone-specific capture of the till (or a taller crop) that ends above the button row with no truncated header, via <picture> media sources
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): "MIT licensed" is the headline licence claim, but building and hosting the till yourself needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)", and no page says that licence is paid and proprietary or what it costs. A sceptic who finds this after reading the footer will distrust the rest of the open-source framing. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' and home footer; limitations]_ Fix: Next to "MIT licensed" and in 'Before you start', say plainly that self-building needs a commercial RxDB Premium licence, link to its pricing, and say that the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com needs no licence of your own.
