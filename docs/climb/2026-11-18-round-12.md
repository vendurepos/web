## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero offers four near-equal buttons ("Live demo", "Quick start", "Try with your store", "GitHub"). "Try with your store" opens the app, which cannot work until the plugin is installed, so the page needs a small-print line under the buttons ("Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first") to undo it. Vendure itself is only a link, never glossed in a few words for a visitor who half knows it. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01, hero button row and the caption under it]_
- J2 (8): Four equal-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub') under a hero that says the till 'signs in to your own Vendure store' and 'the quick start sets it up' leave a non-developer owner unsure whether 'Try with your store' works without their freelancer. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero has four CTAs and three of them ('Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub') are identical outlined buttons of equal weight. 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' read as the same next step, so after the black 'Live demo' there is no clear second action. _[home.desktop.01 hero button row; home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero has four near-equal buttons ("Live demo", "Quick start", "Try with your store", "GitHub"), and "Quick start" and "Try with your store" overlap. Nothing on the page says who this is not for or how it compares with Shopify POS, Square or a WooCommerce till. A buyer comparing options gets no framing beyond "Point of sale for Vendure". _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.txt]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The home page says "open-source" and "MIT licensed", but only Limitations and Connect a till say that "Building the till yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM". A visitor finds the paid dependency late, and it reads as hidden. _[home footer ('MIT licensed') vs limitations/desktop/01 bullet 3 and connect-a-till/desktop/01 'The till's source is MIT-licensed…']_
- J2 (8): Refunds at the till depend on 'the plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', repeated on home, At the till, Limitations and Connect a till with no date, so an owner cannot tell when till refunds arrive or whether to plan on settling refunds by hand in the Vendure Dashboard. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Refund a sale at the till'; at-the-till 'Refund a sale'; limitations]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): On the home comparison table the live demo 'sends anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That is an unexplained third-party domain on a VendurePOS page, and the explanation (a self-hosted PostHog) only appears deep in Connect a till. _[home.desktop.02 table, last row; home.phone.04]_
- J5 (6): The home footer says "MIT licensed" and the site presents an open-source till. The only place it says "Building the till yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM" is deep in Limitations and Connect a till, with no cost or explanation. The site also promises "the plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet" with no date, while the last release was 2026-10-06. The maintainer behind ph.wcpos.com is never named. _[home.txt footer; limitations.txt; connect-a-till.txt; at-the-till.txt; changelog.txt]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.6)
- J1 (8): Step 3 of the quick start gives three migration routes in one paragraph: the tsx script, "npx vendure migrate", and "the plugin's bundled migrations… the README describes that alternative". The Node requirement ("^20.19 || >=22.12") appears only on Plugin setup, not in the quick start's prerequisites or the home "Before you start". _[quick-start/desktop/01 step 3; plugin-setup/desktop/01 'Check your store']_
- J2 (7): The path to staff selling runs through developer steps (Quick start step 3 migration scripts, step 6 tax strategies), and giving each cashier their own account, including adding the ReadOrder permission by hand 'until the next release', is not in the quick start but on Connect a till. _[quick-start steps 3-9; connect-a-till 'Give each cashier their own account']_
- J3 (8): Quick start installs plugin 0.3.0 and describes creating a device key without mentioning the manual ReadOrder addition that Connect a till says this version needs for the till's order list. _[text/quick-start.txt, steps 4 and Optional: a device key for each till; text/connect-a-till.txt, Give each cashier their own account]_
- J4 (8): Step 3 of the quick start offers three migration routes in one wall of prose: tsx scripts, 'npx vendure migrate', and 'Do not also run the plugin's bundled migrations'. Step 8 then lists sync checks twice: item h and the separate 'Check it worked' bullet list. _[quick-start.desktop.01 step 3; quick-start.desktop.03 step 8 h vs 'Check it worked']_
- J5 (7): Quick start step 4 says the admin needs to read the channel's catalogue and settings and have "TallyPosSell (or CreateOrder)". Connect a till says the role also needs ReadCustomer, CreateCustomer and ReadOrder, and that ReadOrder is "gained only in the plugin release after 0.3.0; until then, add it to the role yourself". A least-privilege user built from the quick start alone is therefore under-specified. On 0.3.0 a till refund also "stays Pending until someone settles it by hand". _[quick-start.txt step 4 vs connect-a-till.txt; at-the-till.txt refund]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor sees four peer buttons and cannot tell "Quick start" from "Try with your store". The latter opens app.vendurepos.com, which only works after a server-side plugin install, and the hero has to add a caveat line to stop people from clicking it. The next step should be obvious, and it is hedged instead. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01, hero buttons]_ Fix: Take "Try with your store" out of the hero, leaving Live demo (primary), Quick start and GitHub, and keep it in the top nav and at the end of the quick start. Add a short gloss after the Vendure link, e.g. "(the open-source TypeScript commerce platform)".
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till, the cashier page, says nothing about selling when the internet drops: no word that sales keep working and send later, what the 'N orders waiting to send' header line means, or that customer search and adding a customer need a connection. A shop owner reading it cannot answer 'what happens when the internet drops' without finding Troubleshooting. _[at-the-till (all slices); covered only in troubleshooting 'Sell while offline']_ Fix: Add a short 'If the internet drops' section to At the till: sales, discounts, parking and receipts keep working and send by themselves when the connection returns; the header shows how many orders are waiting; customer search and adding a customer need a connection; link to Troubleshooting 'Sell while offline'.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The quick start's plugin 0.3.0 device-key instructions omit the manual ReadOrder permission addition documented elsewhere as necessary for the till's order list. _[text/quick-start.txt, Optional: a device key for each till; text/connect-a-till.txt, Create and use a device key]_ Fix: Add the 0.3.0 ReadOrder caveat directly to the quick start's device-key instructions. Link its staff authentication step to the complete cashier-role permission list.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): The first screen on a phone ends in four near-equal buttons that wrap 2+2 into mismatched rows ('Live demo | Quick start' / 'Try with your store | GitHub'). The hierarchy falls apart exactly where a visitor decides what to do next. _[home.phone.01 hero button row (also home.desktop.01)]_ Fix: Cut the hero to one primary ('Live demo') and one secondary ('Quick start'). Demote 'Try with your store' and 'GitHub' to text links in the line below, and on phone make the two buttons full-width and stacked.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells an "MIT licensed" open-source till, but building the till yourself "needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM". That is a paid third-party licence, disclosed only on Limitations and Connect a till, with no price. A competitor-aware buyer would call this open-source overselling and a de facto lock-in to the hosted app.vendurepos.com. _[home.txt footer ("MIT licensed"); limitations.txt; connect-a-till.txt]_ Fix: State it on the home page under "Before you start" and beside the footer licence line, for example: "The hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free; building the till from source needs a paid RxDB Premium licence (link, price)." Name who maintains the project and runs ph.wcpos.com in the same place.
