## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The first feature card under the hero, 'Keeps selling offline', spends most of its text on caveats ('a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them'), so the headline benefit reads like a warning to a newcomer before any plain benefit lands. _[home/desktop/01 (and home/phone/02), 'What it does today' first card]_
- J2 (8): The hero says what it is ('Point of sale for Vendure', 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0') but nowhere says what the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com costs or who runs it, so a shop owner cannot tell at a glance whether using it is free. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Too much centred text in the hero: under 'Point of sale for Vendure' sits a four-line centred lead paragraph, and below the buttons a second, two-line centred note ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first...'). The CTAs end up sandwiched between two grey text blocks instead of anchoring the fold. _[home.desktop.01 (hero)]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is but not who should NOT pick it over Square/Shopify POS; the deal-breakers a buyer compares on (no cash-drawer kick, no card-terminal integration, iPad untested, real-store refunds via Admin API) only appear in 'Before you start' at the bottom of the page, and the share card ('Sell from the browser, keep selling offline...') drops 'open-source' and 'pre-release' entirely. _[home.desktop.01 hero vs home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; og-image.png]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo 'sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' with no word on who wcpos is. A newcomer sees a domain under a different brand getting their data and nothing explains it until Connect a till. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J2 (8): The fact that matters most to a counter with a tablet, 'tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet', is buried mid-sentence in the 'Before you start' bullet about browsers instead of being stated as a plain limit. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; home.phone.04]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The changelog's 0.3.0 and 0.2.0 bullets are full of internal references, '(ADR 0005)' five times and '(ADR 0004)' three times, plus '#116' and '#117'. To a visitor this reads like a raw commit log, not release notes. _[changelog.desktop.01]_
- J5 (7): Nowhere on the site says who builds or supports VendurePOS (no maintainer, company, support channel or issue link in footer/overview), yet the demo and site send analytics to an unexplained third-party domain 'ph.wcpos.com'; together with the plugin's last release being 0.3.0 on 2026-10-06 and a known defect carried in two releases ('Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder'), a sceptic cannot tell whether this is backed or a side project. _[home.desktop.02 footer and 'Live demo or your own store' table; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go'; changelog.desktop.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): In the home page table the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a domain under a brand the visitor has never heard of, with no explanation on the page. For someone who has just arrived, that is the one line that reads like hidden tracking by a third party. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04, 'Live demo or your own store' table, 'Sales reach Vendure' row]_ Fix: On the home page, say who owns it in plain words (for example 'to our own self-hosted analytics, with no cookie') and keep the ph.wcpos.com domain and event list on Connect a till, which the link already points to.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): For an owner's own store, the site says a refund 'stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls; see Plugin setup' and points to a developer page, so there is no procedure the owner or staff can follow for a return without calling the freelancer. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Refund a sale at the till'; at-the-till 'Refund a sale']_ Fix: Add a short who-does-what box to at-the-till 'Refund a sale' (cashier: Pay out cash or refund on the terminal; manager: refund in the Dashboard and run this copy-paste settle step), and link the home table row to that box instead of Plugin setup.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): Version-pinned installation relies on an unversioned supplementary plugin reference, leaving its applicability to plugin 0.3.0 unclear. _[text/plugin-setup.txt, 'Check your own code'; text/quick-start.txt, optional device key; tech.json links]_ Fix: Pin the full plugin README link to plugin-v0.3.0. Link the quick start's pnpm guidance directly to the existing Connect a till instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C3): The path from install to a working till is all text. Quick start (10,580 px on phone) and Connect a till show no picture of the till's sign-in screen or of a synced POS order in the Vendure Dashboard, so a developer has nothing to compare against at the two moments that decide whether setup worked. The only product images in the docs are on At the till. _[quick-start.desktop.03-04 / quick-start.phone.07-08 (step 8 and 'Check it worked'); connect-a-till.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one screenshot of the 'Sign in to Vendure' screen to step 8 (and to Connect a till), and one of the synced order in the Dashboard (tally-pos settled, fulfilment delivered) to 'Check it worked'. Serve the phone crop on phone, as At the till already does.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site never says who maintains VendurePOS, how it is supported or how long it will be maintained. The only clue is an unexplained analytics domain, ph.wcpos.com. The plugin's last release was 0.3.0 on 2026-10-06, and that release still carries a ReadOrder role bug. A consultant cannot tell a client whether this till is backed or abandoned. _[home.desktop.02 footer ('MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.'); connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go'; changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one line to the footer and the Overview naming the maintainer (and that ph.wcpos.com is theirs), where to get support (GitHub issues link), and the next planned plugin release or its status.
