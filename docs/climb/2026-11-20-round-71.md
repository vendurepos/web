## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The first feature card a newcomer reads, "Keeps selling offline", spends most of its text on failure caveats ("a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them"), so the headline benefit reads like a warning before it reads like a benefit. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.02, 'What it does today' first card]_
- J2 (8): The hero ('Point of sale for Vendure', an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework, with a note about the plugin on your Vendure server) is written for the developer. Nothing up top tells the owner, in plain words, 'your staff sell from a browser; your developer sets it up once'. The cashier guide 'At the till' is reachable only from the top nav and the closing paragraph. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Hero sub-line wraps to leave the orphan "till in and makes a sale." centred alone on line two; on phone the header shows only "Docs" and "Live demo", so the hero's second CTA "Quick start" is missing from the bar and hidden in the menu _[home.desktop.01 (hero sub-line); home.phone.01 (header)]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and who it is for, but nothing near the top says who should not pick it over Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed (no integrated card terminal, no cash drawer, iPad untested, no refunds at the till on a real store); those deal-breakers first appear in 'Before you start' at the very bottom of the page. _[home/desktop/01 hero vs home/desktop/02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo "sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com" without ever saying what wcpos is, so a stranger sees an unrelated third-party domain on a VendurePOS page with no explanation of who owns it. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_
- J2 (8): The refund row reads 'No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls'. It is honest, but to an owner it says refunds are half-finished and need a developer, and it is phrased in jargon. A smaller mixed signal: 'tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet', while the phone hero shows a phone-layout till. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start'; home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): Comparison cell starts "No: the demo store lives in your browser…"; once stacked on phone it reads "Live demo: No: the demo store…", a double colon that looks like a template glitch _[home.phone comparison table, Live demo row; home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): The headline says 'open-source till' and the footer says 'MIT licensed', but self-hosting the till 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', a paid licence whose cost is never stated. That leaves the project's own app.vendurepos.com as the only free route, and no page says who runs it, whether it stays free or what happens if it goes away. The demo's analytics go to an unexplained third-party domain, ph.wcpos.com, and no maintainer or organisation is named anywhere. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Where requests go'; home footer]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The home page tells first-time visitors that the demo sends usage events to ph.wcpos.com, an unexplained third-party domain, which makes a stranger wonder who else is behind the product just as they decide whether to click 'Live demo'. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04, comparison table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_ Fix: Name it in place, for example "anonymous usage events to our self-hosted PostHog (ph.wcpos.com, run by the WCPOS team that builds VendurePOS), with no cookie", or keep only "anonymous usage events, no cookie" and leave the domain on the Connect a till page.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): No page answers the owner's first question, 'can my staff sell with this tomorrow?', in owner terms. The quick start is seven developer server steps (npm, TallyPosPlugin, tsx migrations, bearer auth, CORS_ORIGINS, tax strategy, channel/stock) before 'sign in and make a sale', with no time estimate and no split between what the developer does once and what the counter needs. _[quick-start.desktop.01-03; home.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a short 'For the shop owner' box at the top of the quick start and under the home hero. It should say: your developer does steps 1-7 once (about N minutes); your staff need desktop Chrome, one open tab, your existing card terminal and a keyboard-style barcode scanner; then link 'At the till' for staff and 'Limitations' for what it does not do (no terminal link, no cash drawer, refunds in the Dashboard).
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The quick start leaves the tax-strategy choice unresolved for a store with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels, despite specifying that the setting also affects the webshop. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start step 6]_ Fix: Document the supported configuration for mixed-pricing channels, or explicitly state the unsupported combination. Include the expected rounding behavior and a verification example covering both channel types.
- **J4 Designer** (C3): The quick start never shows its success states. The "Sign in to Vendure" screen and the synced order in the Vendure Dashboard are described only in words, so a developer cannot tell at a glance whether their till is working _[quick-start step 8 and "Check it worked"; connect-a-till]_ Fix: Add two screenshots at the same framed width as the hero: the "Sign in to Vendure" form at step 8, and the POS order in the Vendure Dashboard order list at "Check it worked" (and on connect-a-till), each with a one-line caption aligned to the body text edge
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells an 'open-source, MIT licensed' till, but self-hosting needs a paid RxDB Premium licence of unstated cost. The only free path is the project's hosted app.vendurepos.com, and no page says who operates it, what it costs or will cost, or how long it will be supported. No maintainer is named, and demo telemetry goes to an unexplained ph.wcpos.com. I would warn a client that this is a lock-in and abandonment risk. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store'; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Where requests go'; docs overview]_ Fix: Add a short 'Who runs this and what it costs' block to the Overview and home 'Before you start': who maintains it, that app.vendurepos.com is free (and for how long or on what terms), the rough cost of an RxDB Premium licence for self-hosting, and what ph.wcpos.com / WCPOS is.
