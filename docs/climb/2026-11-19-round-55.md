## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear and the screenshot shows a till at once, but below the fold the home page switches to server internals that a newcomer who half-knows Vendure cannot follow: 'the Vendure plugin saves that id with the order in one step', 'settles it with two Admin API calls', 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. _[home.desktop.01 ('Every sale lands once' card), home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' refund row, 'Setting up the store' list)]_
- J2 (8): The first line a shop owner reads after the H1 explains Vendure as 'the open-source headless commerce framework', and the badge above it reads 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0'. Both are developer terms. 'Till', 'keeps selling when the connection drops' and the two buttons do make the purpose clear. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, hero badge and lede]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): At 390 px the hero screenshot is the phone till: two product cards, a category chip row cut off at "Gif", and the cart collapsed into a "Cart (3) · €20.09" tab. The caption below says "A sale in progress on the live demo till." but no sale is visible. _[home.phone.01, hero image and its caption]_
- J5 (8): The first screen never gives the hardware envelope a buyer comparing against Square or Shopify POS checks first. It does not say the till is tested only in desktop Chrome, does not drive the card terminal and opens no cash drawer. Those facts sit under 'Before you start' near the bottom of the page. _[home.desktop.01 hero vs home.desktop.02 'Before you start' / home.phone.04]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): Before step 1, a 'Before a sale on a live store' warning sends the reader to two other pages (Set up email, Check your own code). ## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the home page explains the product through server internals: 'saves that id with the order in one step', 'two Admin API calls', 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RXDB_PREMIUM'. A first-time visitor who only half knows what Vendure is gets setup detail instead of the plain reasons to try the demo. _[home.desktop.01-02 ('Every sale lands once' card, refund row of the comparison table, 'Setting up the store' list)]_ Fix: Keep the home page's 'Before you start' and table cells to plain outcomes ('needs Vendure 3.6+ on Postgres'; 'refunds go through the Vendure Dashboard for now') and link to Limitations and Plugin setup for the Node range, the Admin API refund steps and the RxDB licence note.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): For a shop owner, the key question is what staff will see when the internet drops. The docs answer it only in prose with monospace placeholders ('N orders waiting to send', '· sending…'). Nowhere on the site is there a picture of the till's offline or unsent-orders header, so staff cannot learn to recognise the one state where reloading or clearing data loses sales. _[at-the-till.desktop.03 / at-the-till phone 'If the internet drops']_ Fix: Add one captioned screenshot of the live demo till header showing e.g. '3 orders waiting to send · sending…' under 'If the internet drops', with the warning 'do not reload while this shows' next to it.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the supported shared tax strategy from the installation instructions. _[Quick start, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:843-872]_ Fix: Add a concrete supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and explain its rounding consequences, or explicitly state that this configuration is unsupported.
- **J4 Designer** (C1): On a 390 px phone the hero image under "Point of sale for Vendure" shows two product cards and a collapsed "Cart (3) · €20.09" tab, with the chip row cut off at "Gif". The caption "A sale in progress on the live demo till." promises a sale the reader cannot see. _[home.phone.01, hero screenshot and caption]_ Fix: Serve a phone-specific hero capture with the cart sheet open (items, total and Pay button visible) and the chip row not clipped. Failing that, recaption the phone image to match what it shows.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): I cannot recommend a till to a client without knowing who stands behind it, and this site never says. It names no maintainer or company and gives no support or issue channel, no roadmap or dates for the gaps it admits (refunds at the till, coupons), and a plugin changelog that went quiet after a five-day burst of releases ending 2026-10-06. The site is honest about limits but silent on continuity, and continuity is what separates a project from a toy for a POS buyer. _[home.desktop.02 footer; changelog.desktop.01; limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a short 'Who maintains this' block on the home page and docs Overview. It should name the maintainer, link GitHub Issues as the support channel, and give the next plugin release with what it brings (refunds at the till) and a rough date.
