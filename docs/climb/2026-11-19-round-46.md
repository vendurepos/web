## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the home page's closing 'Before you start' section turns into a developer checklist for a first-time reader: 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and 'Vendure 3.6 or a later 3.x release on Postgres (tested on 3.7.3)' repeat the docs' Requirements word for word. The hero's own gloss, 'the open-source headless commerce framework', also leaves 'headless' unexplained. _[home/desktop/02 (Before you start), home/phone/04-05; hero sentence in home/desktop/01]_
- J2 (8): The hero opens with "open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework": jargon a shop owner does not read past, while the plain answer they need ("keeps selling when the connection drops") comes second. On the phone, the header shows only "Docs" and "Live demo"; Quick start and At the till are hidden behind the chevron. _[home.desktop.01 hero copy; home.phone.01 header]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero is text-heavy for a five-second read: a 4-line centred paragraph ("VendurePOS is an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework. It is made for shops...") plus a second 2-line note under the buttons ("Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first...") sits between the H1 and the screenshot, pushing the product shot below the fold on desktop. _[home.desktop.01 (hero), home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): Hero says 'Point of sale for Vendure' with 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' but never says who it is for or not for, what it costs, or who maintains it; no comparison with Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed, so a buyer weighing alternatives has to infer the trade-off _[home.desktop.01 hero and 'What it does today']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the live demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a domain from a different brand that the home page never explains. A newcomer reading about VendurePOS sees data going to an unexplained third-party-looking host. Connect a till does explain it is a self-hosted PostHog server. _[home/desktop/02, 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (8): The refund story differs from page to page. Home says the refund "stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", but Limitations names a single "settleRefund mutation". An owner cannot tell how much work the freelance dev is being asked to do. _[home.desktop.02 table row "Refund a sale at the till"; limitations text, refunds item]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): Only two of the ten feature claims carry proof: "Keeps selling offline" and "Every sale lands once" get boxed cards with test links, while "Paid, fulfilled, at the till's price" and "Line and order discounts" ("the order in Vendure matches the receipt's totals") are plain text with nothing to click. _[home.desktop.01-02, What it does today]_
- J5 (6): 'MIT licensed' / open-source till, yet 'To build and host it yourself… you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)': a paid proprietary dependency with no cost stated; no maintainer or company named anywhere while analytics go to an unexplained ph.wcpos.com; changelog 0.2.0 says the role 'does everything a till does' then 'this role has no ReadOrder, so the till's order history is refused'; plugin unreleased since 2026-10-06 _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' + footer; changelog.desktop.01 0.2.0 entry]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): For a visitor who arrived from a search, the home page stops selling and starts configuring once past the features. The 'Before you start' section asks them to parse Node semver ranges, an RXDB_PREMIUM licence token and plugin-version refund caveats, and the comparison table talks about 'two Admin API calls' and ph.wcpos.com. All of that is docs-level detail that makes a simple product feel complicated. _[home/desktop/02 ('Live demo or your own store' refund row and 'Before you start'); home/phone/04-05]_ Fix: Cut 'Before you start' on the home page to three plain lines: Vendure 3.6+ on Postgres, install one npm plugin, open the hosted till in a browser. Link to Overview › Requirements for versions and self-hosting. In the table, shorten the refund cell to 'No, refund in the Vendure Dashboard (see Plugin setup)'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): Refunds on your own store: the till cannot refund on plugin 0.3.0. At the till tells staff to ask "whoever manages your Vendure store" and says the refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with Admin API calls. The settle steps sit only in Plugin setup, and home ("two Admin API calls") and Limitations ("settleRefund mutation") disagree on what that takes. A shop owner cannot see who does a refund at the counter or how long the customer waits. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 "Refund a sale"; home.desktop.02 refund row; limitations refunds item]_ Fix: In At the till's "Refund a sale", say in one plain paragraph who does the refund, what the customer is told and how long it stays Pending, and link straight to plugin-setup#settle-a-dashboard-refund. Make home and Limitations agree on the exact Admin API steps.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess which global order-tax strategy to select. _[Quick start, step 6; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Add an explicit mixed-channel recommendation with a worked verification example, or state that this configuration is not covered by the installation instructions. Put this guidance beside the strategy table.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): Quick start step 4, the step every developer must get right, puts the till role's eight permissions as inline code pills in running prose. On a 390 px phone that becomes a five-line wall of pills with ragged breaks ("TallyPosSell, ReadCatalog, ReadSettings, ReadCustomer and CreateCustomer. TallyPosSell (or CreateOrder...) ... Also add ReadOrder"), and you can't see at a glance which permissions are required and which are optional. _[quick-start.phone.04 / quick-start.desktop.02, 4. Enable bearer authentication]_ Fix: Replace the sentence with a short bulleted list (or one code block) of the role's permissions: TallyPosSell, ReadCatalog, ReadSettings, ReadCustomer, CreateCustomer, ReadOrder. Follow it with one line noting that CreateOrder can stand in for TallyPosSell.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The till is sold as 'MIT licensed' open source, but self-hosting needs a paid RxDB Premium licence token whose cost is never stated, and no maintainer or company is named anywhere on the site _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' and footer 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI.']_ Fix: State plainly that app.vendurepos.com is free to use, that self-hosting the till needs a paid third-party RxDB Premium licence (give the price or a link to it), and name who maintains VendurePOS (and what ph.wcpos.com is) on home and in the footer
