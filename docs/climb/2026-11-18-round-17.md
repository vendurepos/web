## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero offers four near-equal buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub). 'Try with your store' goes straight to app.vendurepos.com, a sign-in that cannot work until the plugin is installed. The warning about that ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first') sits in small grey text under the buttons, after a newcomer may already have clicked. _[home/desktop/01 hero buttons; home/phone/01]_
- J2 (8): 'Try with your store' is a peer hero button next to 'Live demo', but on a store without the plugin it leads nowhere useful; the only warning is a small grey line under the buttons ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'), and nothing tells a non-developer owner to hand the quick start to their developer. _[home/desktop/01 hero buttons; home/phone/01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home desktop and phone slices]_
- J4 (8): The hero has four buttons, one black 'Live demo' and three identical outlined ones ('Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), so the developer's real next step (Quick start) looks no more important than GitHub, which is already the icon in the header. _[home.desktop.01 (button row under the lede, y~425); home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is ("Point of sale for Vendure", "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0") but never says who should not use it or how it compares with what a Vendure merchant would otherwise pick (Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed). Four equal-weight CTAs (Live demo / Quick start / Try with your store / GitHub) compete, and "Try with your store" stalls until the plugin is installed. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The home page says 'MIT licensed' and 'open-source', but only the Limitations and Connect a till pages admit that 'Building the till yourself needs your own RxDB Premium licence token'. The home page's 'Before you start' list never mentions this paid dependency, so the open-source claim looks partly hidden. _[home/desktop/02 footer and 'Before you start' vs limitations/desktop/01 bullet 3]_
- J2 (8): Refunds and the customer note at the till depend on 'a plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', repeated on home, At the till, Connect a till and Limitations, with no date or version number, so an owner cannot tell whether refunds at the till are weeks or months away. _[home/desktop/02 comparison table 'Refund a sale at the till'; at-the-till.txt 'Refund a sale' and 'Order details'; limitations.txt]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): The demo row in the home comparison table says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', and Connect a till says this website reports to the same server. A different brand's domain appears without a word on who wcpos is, which makes a careful reader wonder whose product this is. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Where requests go']_
- J5 (6): Refunds and the customer note work in the live demo, and the At the till description promises "refund a till sale and close the day", but both need "the plugin release after 0.3.0", which has no date or milestone. The changelog's last release is 0.3.0 on 2026-10-06, six weeks before capture, which a sceptic reads as stalled. The footer says "MIT licensed", yet building the till needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM" (Limitations). No maintainer or company is named anywhere I could see. _[home.desktop.02 comparison table; at-the-till.phone.01 description; changelog.desktop.01; limitations.desktop.01; home footer]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.6)
- J1 (7): 'Quick start' is nine steps and about 6,000 px long, with no time estimate. Store-wide tax-strategy choices (step 6) and a dense reference paragraph, 'How POS sales take stock … A per-sale locationId is refused', are mixed in with the steps. Step 9 drops into 'VENDUREPOS_WEB_ALLOW_LAN_HTTP … adds http: to connect-src and img-src'. A newcomer can't tell which parts are required and which are background. _[quick-start/desktop/02-03 (steps 6, 7, 9); quick-start/phone/05-08]_
- J2 (8): Quick start step 8a signs the till in with 'your administrator email and password' and never links to 'Give each cashier their own account' (Connect a till), so the obvious path ends with staff sharing the owner's admin login; the cashier role also lacks ReadOrder until the next plugin release. _[quick-start/desktop/03 step 8a; connect-a-till.txt 'Give each cashier their own account']_
- J3 (8): The quick start's device-key recipe omits the manual ReadOrder addition that Connect a till explicitly requires for the pinned plugin 0.3.0. _[text/quick-start.txt, Optional: a device key for each till; text/connect-a-till.txt, Create and use a device key]_
- J4 (8): Step 8 runs a nine-item lettered list (a–i), whose item h already says 'check Vendure for the POS prices, settled tally-pos payment, delivered fulfilment, stock reduction and tallySaleAt'. A separate 'Check it worked' bullet list then repeats the same five checks, so success is defined twice and the step reads long. _[quick-start.desktop.03 (step 8, items a–i plus 'Check it worked'); quick-start.phone.07-08]_
- J5 (7): The quick start is thorough but long (9 steps plus lettered sub-steps a–i; 9 phone slices). A gotcha lives only on Connect a till: the till's order list needs ReadOrder, which the vendurepos-pos-till role "gains only in the release after 0.3.0", so it must be added by hand. The quick start's step 9 check ("order.create v4 and register v1") does not surface that. _[quick-start.desktop.01-04, quick-start.phone.01-09, connect-a-till text]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): For someone who just arrived, the hero gives four equal-weight choices, and one of them ('Try with your store') opens a sign-in that only works after a server plugin install the visitor doesn't know about yet. The caveat sits below the buttons in small text, so the most tempting button for a curious store owner is a dead end. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 hero buttons]_ Fix: Keep Live demo as the primary button and make Quick start the only secondary one. Drop 'Try with your store' from the hero, or relabel it 'Open the app (needs the plugin)' and move it after the caveat line.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): A customer brings something back tomorrow and my till cannot refund it on plugin 0.3.0; the docs only say to refund in the Vendure Dashboard where it 'stays Pending until someone settles it by hand', with no steps for settling it or handling the cash in the drawer. _[at-the-till/desktop/02 'Refund a sale'; home/desktop/02 comparison table]_ Fix: Under 'Refund a sale', add a short 'On plugin 0.3.0' list: the Dashboard clicks to refund the order and mark the refund settled, plus recording the cash with Pay out on the till. Name the plugin version that brings refunds to the till.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Following the quick start's device-key instructions misses the manual ReadOrder permission addition documented as necessary for plugin 0.3.0's till order list. _[text/quick-start.txt, Optional: a device key for each till]_ Fix: Add the version-specific ReadOrder instruction immediately after creating the key, and link directly to Connect a till's role instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C1): The hero's four-button row gives Quick start, Try with your store and GitHub identical outlined styling next to one black Live demo, so there is no clear second step for a developer. At 390 px the row breaks into an uneven 2+2 block of mismatched widths. _[home.desktop.01 button row; home.phone.01]_ Fix: Drop 'GitHub' from the hero row (it is already in the header and footer) and keep Live demo (primary) plus Quick start and Try with your store. On phones, stack them at full width.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Refunds at the till are shown working in the live demo and promised in the At the till description ("refund a till sale and close the day") and llms.txt. They actually need "the plugin release after 0.3.0", which is undated and unlinked, while the newest release is 0.3.0 from 2026-10-06, six weeks earlier. A competitor-aware sceptic advising a client would call that demo-ware on a stalled project. _[at-the-till.phone.01 / at-the-till.desktop.02, home.desktop.02 comparison table ("Not yet: it needs the plugin release after 0.3.0"), changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Ship the release, or give it a target date and link its milestone or issue wherever "release after 0.3.0" appears. Drop "refund a till sale" from the At the till description and llms.txt until it ships. Add a dated "latest activity" line or a roadmap link to the changelog so six quiet weeks don't read as abandonment.
