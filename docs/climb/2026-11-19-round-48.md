## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the first two highlighted cards under 'What it does today' are written for engineers. 'Keeps selling offline' spends most of its text on caveats ('A reload needs the connection, and sales not yet sent are lost if this site's data is cleared'), and 'Every sale lands once' talks about an id saved 'with the order in one step' and has a 'Read the 200-send test' button. So the first thing a newcomer reads below the hero is about data loss and test harnesses, not what a shop gets. _[home/desktop/01 (cards below the screenshot); home/phone/02]_
- J2 (8): The hero is clear ('Point of sale for Vendure… keeps selling when the connection drops'), but 'Before you start' opens with developer requirements (Node '^20.19 || >=22.12', Postgres, RXDB_PREMIUM licence token) ahead of the counter facts I care about ('desktop Chrome only', 'iPad not tested', 'no cash drawer opening'). There is no short 'what your staff get' summary near the top. _[home.desktop 'Before you start' section]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Three blocks of text sit between the H1 and the product image (a 4-line centred lede, the CTA pair, then a 2-line note 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first...'), so on a phone the till screenshot starts below the first screen. _[home.phone.01 / home.desktop.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says "Point of sale for Vendure" and "made for shops that sell online with Vendure and also sell in person", but the home page never says who should NOT use it or how it compares with Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed. There is no maintainer or about line either, so a sceptic cannot tell who stands behind it. The footer's "Built with TallyUI" and the analytics host ph.wcpos.com (connect-a-till) go unexplained. _[home.desktop.01 / home.txt hero and footer]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor who scrolls past the clear hero meets two highlighted cards written for engineers. 'Keeps selling offline' leads into caveats about reloads and 'sales not yet sent are lost if this site's data is cleared', and 'Every sale lands once' talks about ids, 'one step' and a '200-send test'. The first impression of the product turns into data loss and test jargon instead of what a shop gains. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/02, 'What it does today' cards]_ Fix: Rewrite the two cards as plain shop outcomes, e.g. 'Internet drops? Keep selling: sales send themselves when it is back.' and 'Never a double order, even if a sale is sent twice.' Keep the test links as small proof links, and move the reload and cleared-data caveat into the 'Before you start' list next to the existing Limitations link.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): On my own store (plugin 0.3.0) staff cannot refund at the till, and the docs explain the workaround in developer language: the refund 'stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls: a query for the refund's ID, then the settleRefund mutation'. A shop owner reading this between customers has no plain answer to 'a customer wants their money back, what do my staff do right now?' _[home 'Live demo or your own store' table, refund row; Limitations refund bullet; At the till 'Refund' section]_ Fix: Add a plain three-line 'When a customer wants a refund' box on At the till and the home table: give cash back with a Pay out quoting the order code, then tell whoever runs your store to settle it. Move the Admin API and settleRefund detail to Plugin setup only and link to it from there.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing must guess which store-wide order tax strategy to configure. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Add explicit guidance for mixed pricing modes: a tested configuration and expected rounding behavior, or a clear statement that this combination has no supported configuration.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The home page's main feature section uses two styles: two bordered cards whose CTA buttons sit at different heights, then eight unboxed heading-and-text pairs. It is the clearest polish fault on the page a visitor sees first. _[home.desktop.01, 'What it does today']_ Fix: Pin the card buttons to the card bottom (flex column with margin-top:auto) so they align. Then either give the eight smaller features a matching light card style or visually separate the two proof cards as their own 'Proven by tests' row.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The live demo refunds at the till and takes a Customer note, but the only released plugin (0.3.0, 2026-10-06) does neither, and the site gives no version or date for when real stores get them. A sceptic reads this as a demo that oversells what you can actually install. _[at-the-till.txt 'Refund at the till (live demo only)' and 'On plugin 0.3.0, Order details has no customer note'; home 'Live demo or your own store' table]_ Fix: Add a 'Customer note' row to the home 'Live demo or your own store' table and to Limitations. For both refunds at the till and customer notes, name the plugin version that brings them to real stores, or link a roadmap entry with a target date. Better still, ship them so the demo and the installable plugin match.
