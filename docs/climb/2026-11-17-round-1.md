## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 7.2)
- J1 (7): The home page has no picture of the till at all: under 'Point of sale for Vendure' a newcomer gets a paragraph, four equal-looking buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub') and ten text-only cards. They must leave the site to see what the product looks like, and the cards use jargon such as 'idempotency ledger', 'Z report' and 'Keyboard-wedge'. 'Vendure' itself is never explained or linked in the hero. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01 (hero and 'What it does today' grid)]_
- J2 (7): The home page never shows the till itself: no screenshot of the cart, payment or Z report anywhere, so a shop owner cannot see what staff would use without leaving for the demo; and the four equal-weight buttons 'Live demo / Quick start / Try with your store / GitHub' don't say how 'Try with your store' differs from 'Quick start'. _[home.desktop.01 hero and 'What it does today' grid; home.phone.01 button rows]_
- J3 (8): The hero offers 'Try with your store' before explaining that it requires a server plugin and Postgres; those prerequisites appear below the feature cards and comparison table. _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg and home.desktop.02.jpg]_
- J4 (7): The hero gives four buttons of near-equal weight ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). 'Try with your store' is never explained in the hero, and there is no picture of the till anywhere on the page, so a POS product's first screen is text only. On phone the buttons wrap as 2+2 rows of unequal widths, centred raggedly. _[home.desktop.01 hero (y~370-420); home.phone.01 (buttons at y~890-1090)]_
- J5 (7): Nowhere on the site is there a picture of the till itself: home shows ten text-only feature cards and a Yes/No table, and 'At the till' describes buttons ('Pay cash', 'Parked (n)', 'Close register') that a reader never sees, so a buyer comparing with Shopify POS or Square cannot judge what the product looks like without leaving for the demo. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01-03; at-the-till.desktop.01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.4)
- J1 (8): The strongest proof claim, 'Our acceptance run makes 25 sales, 20 of them offline, and checks that Vendure holds exactly 25 orders', has no link to the test, a run log or CI. Neither does 'The same sale sent 200 times makes one order', so both read as assertion rather than evidence. Otherwise the limits are stated plainly: the Pre-release badge, the Limitations page and the demo/your-store table. _[home.desktop.01, cards 'Keeps selling offline' and 'Every sale lands once']_
- J2 (8): The offline promise stops at 'Unsent orders and the register wait on that device': nowhere does Limitations or Troubleshooting say what happens to unsent sales if that till is lost, breaks, or has its browser data cleared while offline. _[troubleshooting 'Refresh prices and stock' / 'Sell while offline'; limitations.desktop.01]_
- J3 (8): The claims about 25 sales and 200 submissions lack a direct link to the cited acceptance run or reproducible test. _[text/home.txt, 'Keeps selling offline' and 'Every sale lands once'; tech.json home.desktop.links]_
- J4 (8): Minimum plugin versions disagree: Troubleshooting's header-notice list says 'Run plugin 0.1.0 or later', while the quick start pins 0.3.0 and needs the plugin to 'advertise order.create v4 and register v1', and the Changelog says v5 arrived in 0.3.0. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 'Act on a header notice' bullet 5; quick-start.desktop.04 'Missing / old plugin']_
- J5 (5): The only till path documented is the hosted app: 'Open https://app.vendurepos.com. Enter the store URL ..., your administrator email and password', yet no page says who runs app.vendurepos.com (or 'https://vendurepos.vercel.app'), whether credentials or order data pass through it, or how to run the 'open-source till' yourself; the only hint is an unexplained 'VENDUREPOS_WEB_ALLOW_LAN_HTTP ... exports served over plain HTTP' line in Troubleshoot. _[quick-start.desktop.03 (step 8) and quick-start.desktop.04 (step 9); connect-a-till.desktop.01]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.0)
- J1 (7): The home hero's 'Try with your store' button and the nav link with the same label go straight to https://app.vendurepos.com, before the visitor has installed the plugin, run the migration and opened CORS. Following the obvious button leads to a till that cannot connect. Inside the quick start, step 3 also gives no single command to run: the reader must write two TypeScript entry files and run them 'with your project's existing TypeScript runner'. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons and header nav; quick-start.desktop.01 step 3]_
- J2 (7): Mandatory step '4. Enable bearer authentication' also holds the whole optional device-key setup (Dashboard 'New POS till key', pnpm vendureDashboardPlugin config), repeating Connect a till; step 9 adds VENDUREPOS_WEB_ALLOW_LAN_HTTP / connect-src jargon; and the intro sends you to two app URLs (app.vendurepos.com and vendurepos.vercel.app). _[quick-start.desktop.02 (step 4), quick-start.desktop.04 (step 9), quick-start.desktop.01 intro]_
- J3 (7): Recovery instructions offer applied or rejected for a needs_admin sale without explaining the checks that determine which resolution to choose or what applied actually does. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way'; shots/troubleshooting.desktop.01.jpg and troubleshooting.desktop.02.jpg]_
- J4 (7): Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale', the step that produces the working till, is two dense prose paragraphs (store URL, credentials, channel token, barcode field, float, tender, print, sync check, offline test). It is not a numbered list, so the order of actions on the sign-in screen has to be dug out of running text. Step 3 also asks you to write and run two TypeScript entry files without naming a command. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8 (y~1100-1400); quick-start.phone.07-08]_
- J5 (7): Step 4 says to sign in as an administrator who 'has the plugin's TallyPosSell permission (or CreateOrder ...)' but never says how to give a staff role TallyPosSell for email sign-in (the role is only created for device keys via 'New POS till key'), and the bundled-migration route is sent off-site ('the README describes that alternative') although Plugin setup covers it. _[quick-start.desktop.02 (step 4), quick-start.desktop.01 (end of step 3)]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor arriving from 'Vendure point of sale' never sees the product. The home page is all text: a hero paragraph, four equal-weight buttons and ten jargon-heavy grey cards. Deciding whether this is worth a look means leaving for the demo, and 'Try with your store' sits next to 'Live demo' as if it were just as easy, though it needs the plugin installed first. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01 (hero to the end of the 'What it does today' grid)]_ Fix: Put a screenshot of the till mid-sale (cart, tender, receipt) directly under the hero buttons and link it to the demo. Demote 'Try with your store' out of the hero, or relabel it 'Open the till (plugin required)'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): There is not a single picture of the till on the whole site (home or At the till), so a shop owner deciding whether staff can sell with it tomorrow has to read text that names buttons like 'Pay cash', 'Card payment approved' and 'Close register' without ever seeing the screen. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01-02; at-the-till.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one screenshot of the till mid-sale (cart plus tender) under the home hero, and one each of the tender screen and the Z report in At the till.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The docs disagree about whether sales debit only the channel's default stock location or follow Vendure's allocation strategy, including multiple locations. _[shots/limitations.desktop.01.jpg; text/quick-start.txt step 7; text/changelog.txt version 0.1.0]_ Fix: Make the Limitations statement agree with the documented allocation behavior. Explicitly distinguish server-side stock allocation from the till's inability to select a location per sale, and align the corresponding llms-full.txt passage.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a phone, the quick start's step 6 tax table, the one place that says which orderTaxCalculationStrategy to use, is clipped at the right edge ('new OrderLevelTaxCalcul', 'DefaultOrderTaxCalculati') with no sign that it scrolls. Its left column crushes '(pricesIncludeTax: false)' onto three lines. _[quick-start.phone.05 (bottom) and quick-start.phone.06 (top)]_ Fix: Replace the two-row table with two labelled lines or short code blocks ('Tax-exclusive → new OrderLevelTaxCalculationStrategy()', 'Tax-inclusive → new DefaultOrderTaxCalculationStrategy()'), or stack the table into label/value rows below 640 px so nothing is clipped.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A client is told to type their Vendure administrator email and password into app.vendurepos.com, a hosted site whose operator, data handling and self-host alternative are never stated anywhere on the site; for a sceptic advising a retailer that is the reason to say no, however honest the Limitations page is. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8; connect-a-till.desktop.01 'Open your till']_ Fix: Add a short 'Where your data goes' note on Overview and Quick start: who serves app.vendurepos.com, that the till talks only to your store from the browser (if true) and nothing is stored on the host, and one link or paragraph on hosting the till yourself.
