## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The first and biggest feature card, 'Keeps selling offline', gives four of its six lines to caveats ('a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them'), so the headline benefit reads like a warning before a newcomer knows what the product is. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/02, 'What it does today' first card]_
- J2 (9): The hero sub-copy under 'Point of sale for Vendure' runs to about 4 lines on desktop and 6 on phone before the 'Live demo' / 'Quick start' buttons, so the one-line answer to 'can my staff sell with this?' is diluted by plugin-version and server caveats. _[home desktop.01 and home phone.01, hero]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Hero is clear and well composed (Pre-release pill, H1, Live demo / Quick start, real till screenshot); only nit is the lede running long beside a dense screenshot, so the eye has no single resting point _[home.desktop.01, hero lede]_
- J5 (8): On phone the hero proof is the till in a phone layout (Products / Cart (3) tabs), yet the text two screens down says 'tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet', so the first image suggests a device the site will not stand behind; and nothing near the top says who should not pick this over Shopify POS or Square (no hardware, no card integration). _[home.phone.01 hero image vs home.phone.04 'Before you start']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor cannot find out who is behind VendurePOS. The only hint is the unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' analytics domain in the live-demo row of the comparison table, which makes an unknown open-source project look less trustworthy, not more. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row, and the home footer]_ Fix: Add one line to the footer (and the docs Overview) naming who builds and maintains VendurePOS. Turn 'ph.wcpos.com' into a short phrase saying whose analytics host it is.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): A shop owner can't complete a refund without their developer. On plugin 0.3.0 every refund 'stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls; see Plugin setup'. Neither the home Refund row nor At the till's 'Refund a sale' links the exact settling section, or says plainly who does it, for every refund, and how long the order sits Pending. _[home desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' Refund row; at-the-till desktop.03 'Refund a sale' step 1]_ Fix: Link the Refund row and At the till step 1 straight to Plugin setup #settle-a-dashboard-refund. Add one plain sentence: 'Each refund needs your developer (or whoever runs your Vendure server) to run two calls; until then the order shows Pending. Card money is returned on your terminal and cash with Pay out, so the customer isn't kept waiting.'
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C7): The visible changelog identifies the deployed till precisely, but llms-full.txt omits that information, leaving machine readers without the same version context. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Changelog; text/changelog.txt:24]_ Fix: Include the hosted till's version, build date and commit paragraph in the full documentation export, including the distinction between till and plugin versions.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): Home 'What it does today' mixes two bordered cards with buttons ('Keeps selling offline', 'Every sale lands once') with eight unboxed text items, and 'Every sale lands once' has ~70 px of dead space above its 'Read the 200-send test' button _[home.desktop.01-02, What it does today]_ Fix: Give all ten features one treatment: either a uniform card grid, or all unboxed with an inline 'Read the test' link on the two proven items; bottom-align card buttons so no dead band appears
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A buyer cannot tell who stands behind this till: no maintainer, company, support channel or funding is named anywhere, and the home page's only hint is the unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' analytics domain. For a POS that holds a shop's unsent sales in one browser, an unknown bus factor is the first reason I would advise a client against it. _[home.desktop.02 footer and 'Live demo or your own store' table]_ Fix: Add one line to the home footer and the Overview saying who maintains VendurePOS (e.g. the WCPOS team, which also runs ph.wcpos.com), where to file issues and get support, and how the project is funded.
