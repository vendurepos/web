## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The first feature card a newcomer reads, 'Keeps selling offline', leads with caveats in dense grey text ('a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them') and a test count before saying plainly why offline matters, so the selling point reads as a warning. _[home/desktop/01 'What it does today' first card; home/phone/02]_
- J2 (8): The hero opens with the badge "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0" and the line "the open-source headless commerce framework". That is developer jargon. Before the shop owner has learned what the till does for the counter, they are asked to parse plugin versions and "headless". _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): On the phone the hero screenshot is the till's phone layout cropped to the tab bar and two product cards (Canvas Apron, Chocolate Biscuits), so the caption 'A sale in progress on the live demo till.' sits under a picture with no visible cart or total; the sale only shows as the 'Cart (3) · €20.09' tab label. _[home/phone/01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says who it is for ("made for shops that sell online with Vendure and also sell in person") but nowhere says when a client is better off with a hosted till such as Shopify POS or Square, so the 'who should not use this' half is left to the Before you start bullets further down. _[home/desktop/01 (hero) and home/desktop/02 (Before you start)]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The site never says who makes VendurePOS, and the only clue a visitor sees is an unexplained third-party domain in the comparison table: 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'. A stranger is left asking what WCPOS is and why demo data goes there. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): The refund burden is real and is stated honestly. But it sits inside a table cell: "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls." An owner who skims the table can miss that refunds need their developer. _[home.desktop.02 (comparison table, refund row)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The comparison table says the demo 'sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a third domain the site never explains next to vendurepos.com. The wording is honest, but the unexplained domain is the one line a careful reader would stop on. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J5 (8): The footer on every page says "MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI." with no caveat, while self-hosting the till needs "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)", a commercial licence whose cost is never stated; the caveat appears only in the home Before you start list and in Limitations. _[footer on all pages (home/desktop last slice); home text line 85; limitations text line 40]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Before step 1 the quick start sends readers to two other pages ('Before a sale on a live store … see Set up email … read Check your own code'), and a leftover legacy toggle 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' sits in step 5. A newcomer can't tell whether either one applies to them. _[quick-start/desktop/01 warning callout; quick-start/desktop/03 collapsible under step 5]_
- J2 (8): The path has a manual gotcha: "On plugin 0.3.0 the role it creates (vendurepos-pos-till) has the step 4 permissions except ReadOrder, so add ReadOrder to that role yourself." The owner has to pass a hand-fix in Vendure roles to their freelancer before staff can sell. _[quick-start (Optional: a device key) / connect-a-till]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 prescribes different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but says only that a store mixing modes 'must account for the store-wide strategy', leaving the configuration decision unresolved. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start / Why these pairings]_
- J4 (8): 'Check it worked' describes success as five text bullets ('the payment is tally-pos, settled; the fulfilment is delivered...') with no screenshot of the synced order in the Vendure Dashboard. On a 10,580 px phone page, the end state is the one place a picture would remove all guessing. _[quick-start/desktop/04 and quick-start/phone/08]_
- J5 (8): The Dashboard's own "New POS till key" button creates a role that does not work fully: "On plugin 0.3.0 the vendurepos-pos-till role has no ReadOrder: add it yourself, or the till sells but its order history is refused". The docs state this honestly, but a developer following the button path has to apply a manual fix. _[quick-start (device key paragraph); connect-a-till/desktop/02]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor can't find out who is behind VendurePOS: no maker, team or company is named on the home page, and the only hint is an unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' receiving demo usage events. That reads as a mystery third party right where trust is being decided. _[home/desktop/02 comparison table, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; footer 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI.']_ Fix: Name the maker once in the footer or hero (for example 'Made by the WCPOS team'), and in the table say 'to our own self-hosted analytics (ph.wcpos.com)'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till is the page an owner hands to staff, and it reads partly like a developer page. It opens with a plugin-version caveat ("Some steps depend on the store's VendurePOS plugin. With no plugin, or one too old to sell…"). It carries a demo-only refund section. It tells cashiers to "choose your initial at the right of the bar", but the control is an unlabeled round letter avatar. A cashier reading on a phone meets store-setup caveats before "Open the register". _[at-the-till.phone.01 / at-the-till.desktop.01 / at-the-till.desktop.03 / at-the-till.phone.05]_ Fix: Move the plugin-version note to the end of the page or into Troubleshooting. Collapse "Refund at the till (live demo only)" into a one-line note. Name the control as it looks: "tap the round letter button at the top right of the till and pick your name".
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both inclusive and exclusive pricing must guess which store-wide tax strategy to use. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start / Why these pairings]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes, or explicitly state that this setup is unsupported. Add receipt-versus-Vendure tax and total checks for each supported mode to 'Check it worked'.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The figures on At the till (the only docs page with product images) are inconsistent: 836 px full-column, about 384 px centred and about 512 px centred, with a dead band above the centred ones and captions that move between the text column and the image edge. _[at-the-till/desktop/02-04]_ Fix: Give every docs figure one rule, for example max-width 560 px, left-aligned to the text column, no top padding inside the frame, with the caption always under the image's left edge. Re-export the cashier sheet and closure at that width.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): To a sceptic advising a client, the "MIT licensed" footer on every page reads as fully open source, but the only route to a self-hosted till needs a commercial RxDB Premium token whose price and terms the site never states. It is the open-core catch, and it is disclosed in only two bullets. _[footer on all pages; home/desktop/02 Before you start ("you need your own RxDB Premium licence token")]_ Fix: Change the footer to "MIT licensed · self-hosting the till needs a paid RxDB Premium token", linking to one Limitations line that names who sells the token and what it roughly costs (or says the hosted app.vendurepos.com avoids it).
