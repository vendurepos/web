- J2 (8): The guide my cashiers need is not in the hero or the top nav (Docs, Live demo, Connect your store); the only home-page pointer is the last paragraph, 'For cashiers, At the till covers selling, taking payment and closing the day.', below the feature cards, the comparison table and Before you start. _[home.desktop.02 (last paragraph above footer); home.phone.05]_
- J3 (9): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): On a phone the hero lead under "Point of sale for Vendure" runs to 7 centred lines of large grey body text, and the till screenshot under it shows a truncated "Q..." column header in the cart table. _[home.phone.01]_

- J2 (8): The home page never says what device or browser to put on the counter; only Limitations admits 'Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome; other browsers are not tested yet.', so 'A web till for now' in Before you start reads as 'any browser'. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' vs limitations.desktop.01 second bullet]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): Right after a clear hero, a first-time visitor hits feature cards written in engineer language ("idempotency ledger", "The same sale sent 200 times makes one order", "Keyboard-wedge scanners", "counted float", "Z report") that do not say why it matters to a shop, so the under-a-minute skim ends on jargon instead of a benefit. _[home/desktop/01 'What it does today' (Every sale lands once, Barcode scanners, Registers and Z reports cards)]_ Fix: Lead each card with the plain benefit ("A flaky connection never creates a duplicate order", "Any USB barcode scanner works", "Count the cash drawer and print an end-of-day report") and keep the technical detail as the second sentence or behind the test link.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): A shop owner deciding whether staff can sell tomorrow can't learn from the home page what to put on the counter: 'Before you start' only says 'A web till for now', and the facts that matter (tested only in desktop Chrome, private Safari windows cannot run it, one tab per till, printing through the browser dialog, no cash-drawer kick) are on the Limitations page only. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04 'Before you start' vs limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one 'At the counter' bullet to Before you start: 'A computer running desktop Chrome (other browsers untested), a keyboard-wedge scanner, any printer the browser can use, your own card terminal; the cash drawer opens by hand.' Link it to Limitations.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): A developer investigating rejected register updates reaches a dead end: the docs explain the log and blocked queue but not how to recover or escalate. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, Act on a header notice → Till updates line; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, corresponding section]_ Fix: Add the supported next step after inspecting the rejection code. If recovery requires maintainer help, explicitly say so and link the support destination with the diagnostic information to include.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The home "What it does today" section is a uniform 2×5 wall of ten equal-weight grey cards. Only the first two link to proof ("Read the acceptance test", "Read the 200-send test"), so the page's strongest evidence has no visual priority, and on a phone it becomes a three-screen single-column stack. _[home.desktop.01-02, home.phone.02-03]_ Fix: Promote the two proven claims (offline sales, lands-once sends) to a featured pair of larger cards with their proof links, and set the other eight as a compact two-column checklist (icon plus one line each) that collapses to tight rows on a phone.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): I could not advise a client to run their tills on this, because the site never says who builds it, who runs app.vendurepos.com (which receives their Vendure admin password) or where to get help. The only hint is an unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' analytics host. With the last plugin release on 2026-10-06 and no app changelog, a buyer cannot tell whether the project is maintained. _[home/desktop/02 footer and 'Live demo or your own store' table; docs/desktop/01 Overview]_ Fix: Add one line to the home footer and the Overview: who maintains VendurePOS (and its relation to WCPOS), who operates app.vendurepos.com, and where to report problems (GitHub issues link or chat), plus the date of the last app deploy.
