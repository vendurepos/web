## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The first thing under the hero shot, the 'Keeps selling offline' card, spends most of its six lines on caveats ('a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them') before the reassurance, so a newcomer skimming for what this does gets a dense wall of qualifications in the most prominent card; and the hero line still leans on 'headless commerce framework' without a plainer gloss. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/02, 'What it does today' first card]_
- J2 (8): On a phone the header shows only "Docs", "Live demo", search and a chevron, so "Quick start" and the "For cashiers: At the till" link, the page an owner most needs, sit behind the chevron or further down the page. _[home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home desktop, phone and dark screenshots]_
- J4 (8): On phone the hero till screenshot is cropped after the first row of products (category pills cut at "Gif"), so the cart the caption "A sale in progress on the live demo till." promises is not visible _[home.phone.01]_
- J5 (9): On the phone hero the third call to action 'For cashiers: At the till' wraps to its own line below the two buttons, so the first screen ends up with three different next steps. _[home/phone/01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' — a different brand's domain, never explained anywhere on the home page, so a newcomer wonders who wcpos is and why their data goes there. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J2 (8): The "Live demo or your own store" table says refunds work in the demo ("Yes") but for your own store "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls": honest, but it is developer jargon that a shop owner cannot act on and that makes the gap sound bigger than it is. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The "Refund a sale at the till" row in "Live demo or your own store" carries a four-line paragraph in one cell, making that table row far taller than its neighbours and burying the honest caveat in prose _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): Nowhere on the site says who maintains VendurePOS or how to report a bug or get help: no issue tracker, support channel or maintainer name, only a GitHub icon. Troubleshooting's last resort for an unknown errorCode is to 'send the copied line to whoever runs your Vendure server'. For a till that can lose unsent sales, that is a bus-factor red flag I would raise with a client. _[troubleshooting/desktop/01 ('For the store owner' paragraph); home/desktop/02 footer]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): The first thing a newcomer reads after the hero is the 'Keeps selling offline' card, and it is mostly a paragraph of data-loss caveats, so the product's headline promise reads as hedged and hard to skim in the first minute. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/02, 'Keeps selling offline' card]_ Fix: Cut the card to the promise plus the 25-sale proof ('Sales made offline go to Vendure when it reconnects; our test makes 25 sales, 20 offline, and finds exactly 25 orders'), and move the reload and cleared-data caveat into a one-line 'Keep the tab open' note that links to Limitations.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The home page's refund row for "Your Vendure store" reads "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls; see Plugin setup." To a shop owner this sounds like refunds are broken and need a programmer every time, at the moment they are deciding whether to trust the till. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04 ("Live demo or your own store" table)]_ Fix: Say it in owner terms, for example "No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard (your developer sets up settling once)", and link to At the till › Refund a sale instead of Plugin setup.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The first-sale instructions display the hosted till address as unlinked code, so the developer must copy it or navigate to another page to open the till. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 8; tech.json, quick-start.desktop.links]_ Fix: Make https://app.vendurepos.com a clickable link in step 8 and the introductory hosted-POS sentence.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): Mixed card treatment in "What it does today": two bordered cards with buttons ("Keeps selling offline", "Every sale lands once") followed by eight unboxed features in the same section, so the lower eight read as a lesser section, and the right card has a gap above its button _[home.desktop.01]_ Fix: Give all ten features one treatment, or set the two proof cards apart as their own row with matched heights (button pinned to the card bottom) and render the other eight as a uniform grid under a separate heading
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site never says who stands behind the project or where to go when something breaks: there is no maintainer, issue-tracker link or support channel. The demo's analytics go to ph.wcpos.com, a different brand that the home page never explains. A pre-1.0 till that can lose unsent sales therefore reads as a possible solo side project with no support route, and I would advise a client against putting it on a live counter. _[home/desktop/02 (footer and the 'ph.wcpos.com' table row); troubleshooting/desktop/01]_ Fix: Add one 'Who maintains this and how to get help' line, on the home page above the footer and at the end of Troubleshooting. It should name the maintainer, link GitHub Issues for bugs, and say in a few words how ph.wcpos.com relates to VendurePOS.
