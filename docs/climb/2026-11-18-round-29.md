For Medusa, see MedusaPOS' names two more products with no word on what they are. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' bullet 5 and footer; home.phone.05]_

That only appears in the last section, "Before you start", so a shop owner cannot tell at a glance whether their counter setup will work. _[home/desktop/01 hero paragraph; home/desktop/02 'Before you start']_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_

The section reads as a uniform slab and nothing tells the eye where to start. It is followed by "Before you start", a dense 6-bullet wall that repeats the comparison table above it. _[home desktop slices 01-02; phone slices 02-03]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): In its 'Live demo or your own store' table, the home page tells a first-time visitor that the demo sends usage events to 'ph.wcpos.com', a domain from another brand that the page never explains. That plants doubt about who is behind VendurePOS and where data goes, right when the visitor is deciding whether to click Live demo. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04, table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_ Fix: Name it in place, e.g. 'anonymous usage events to our self-hosted PostHog (ph.wcpos.com, run by WCPOS, the team behind VendurePOS), no cookie', and link to the Connect a till 'Where requests go' section.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The home page promises the till "keeps selling when the connection drops" without the condition that staff must not reload or close the tab while offline: the till cannot load again until the connection returns. A cashier who refreshes during an outage loses the till mid-shift, and the owner only learns this from a paragraph deep in At the till. _[home/desktop/01 hero and 'Keeps selling offline' card; condition only in at-the-till/desktop/03 'If the internet drops']_ Fix: Add one sentence to the home 'Keeps selling offline' card: "Keep the till's tab open: a reload or browser restart needs the connection back." Repeat it as the first bullet of a short list at the top of 'If the internet drops'.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The offline troubleshooting entry, surfaced first by the captured offline search, omits that the app cannot reload or reopen without connectivity, although At the till documents this limitation. _[shots/troubleshooting.desktop.02.jpg, Sell while offline; text/endpoint_api_search_query_offline.txt]_ Fix: Add the keep-tab-open and no-offline-page-cache warning directly under Sell while offline, with a link to At the till's fuller explanation.
- **J4 Designer** (C1): Home "What it does today" is a wall of ten identical grey cards with equal weight, no icons and no lead item, so the page's main selling section has no hierarchy. On phone it becomes about two screens of identical stacked boxes. _[home desktop slices 01-02; phone slices 02-03]_ Fix: Promote the 3 strongest capabilities to a larger lead row, each with an icon or small till crop. Collapse the other 7 into a compact 2-column checklist, and on phone show the 3 lead cards plus a 'See all features' disclosure.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site leads with 'open-source till' and 'MIT licensed', but running the till anywhere except the project's own app.vendurepos.com needs an 'RxDB Premium licence token' that is never identified as a paid third-party licence or priced, and the operator of app.vendurepos.com (and of the ph.wcpos.com analytics) is never named. A sceptic would read this as open source in name only and advise a client against depending on it. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' bullet 5; limitations/desktop/01 bullet 3; connect-a-till/desktop/01]_ Fix: In the home 'Before you start' bullet and on Limitations, say plainly that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence from RxDB (link and rough price). Add one line naming who runs app.vendurepos.com and ph.wcpos.com, with a support or issues link.
