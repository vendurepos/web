## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the home table's refund cell for your own store reads "No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". That is admin-API jargon on the marketing page and makes a newcomer think the product is half-built. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, row 'Refund a sale at the till'; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): The hero opens on developer language ("the open-source headless commerce framework") rather than what staff do at the counter. The top nav (Docs / Live demo / Quick start / At the till) has no Limitations link; 'what it does not do yet' is only in the body and the footer. _[home.desktop.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home.desktop.01.jpg; home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): Three blocks of grey copy stand between the H1 and the product shot: a 4-line lede (6 lines at 390 px), the button row, then a second 2-line note "Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first..." under the buttons. At phone width the screenshot only starts at about y=1160 px, so the first screen is all text. _[home.desktop.01 (hero), home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and who it is for, but nothing above the fold says who should not pick it over Shopify POS or Square (no integrated card terminal, no cash drawer, desktop Chrome only, refunds outside the till). Those deal-breakers only appear in 'Before you start' at the bottom of the page. _[home.desktop.01 hero vs home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The home table says the demo sends "anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com". A visitor who has never heard of wcpos sees a different brand's domain with no explanation on this page; it is only explained on Connect a till as "a self-hosted PostHog analytics server". _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline', Live demo column; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): The comparison row "Refund a sale at the till" reads Live demo "Yes" against Your Vendure store "No, not on plugin 0.3.0 ... settles it with two Admin API calls". Staff who practise on the demo learn a refund flow the real till lacks, and the cell never says what to do at the counter instead. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The one mixed signal is the comparison table. The live demo answers "Yes" to "Refund a sale at the till" while a real store gets a 4-line "No, not on plugin 0.3.0..." cell. The demo visibly does more than the product does, and only the table says so. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, Refund row]_
- J5 (7): No maintainer is named anywhere: the footer reads only 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.', with no support or issue link. The demo also sends analytics to 'ph.wcpos.com', a domain the site never explains. The changelog shows three plugin releases in five days and nothing since 2026-10-06, so a buyer cannot tell whether a team stands behind it. _[home.desktop.02 footer; home table row 'Sales reach Vendure…'; changelog.desktop.01]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The 'Quick start' is about 6,900 px of nine steps. It includes writing two of your own TypeScript migration scripts (src/generate-migration.ts and src/run-migrations.ts) and a collapsed leftover, "If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app", which points at an old domain the reader has never seen. _[quick-start.desktop.01-03 (step 3 and the step 5 disclosure); quick-start.phone.06]_

- J3 (8): The tax instructions prescribe different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but tell mixed-pricing stores only to 'account for the store-wide strategy' without explaining how. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, step 6 and 'Why these pairings']_

Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' (a-i) and 'Check it worked' describe the sign-in screen and the synced Vendure order in words only, so there is no picture of what success looks like. _[quick-start.desktop.03-04, step 8 and 'Check it worked']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): Past the hero, the home page speaks to someone already running Vendure. The 'Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start' put admin-API refund settlement, an unexplained ph.wcpos.com domain, Node semver ranges and an RXDB_PREMIUM licence token in front of a visitor still deciding whether to try the demo. _[home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start'); home.phone.04-05]_ Fix: On the home page, cut the refund cell to "Not yet on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard" and the demo cell to "No: the demo store lives in your browser (privacy note)", linking to the details. Move the Node and RXDB_PREMIUM lines into the docs Overview requirements.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The home comparison row "Refund a sale at the till" tells a shop owner only that the refund "stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". It never says what staff do when a customer wants money back at the counter, and the demo they practise on shows "Yes". _[home.desktop.02]_ Fix: Add one line to that cell: "At the counter: hand back cash with Pay out and refund cards on your terminal; see At the till › Refund a sale", and link it.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to satisfy the conflicting store-wide tax-strategy recommendations. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Quick start, step 6 and 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Document the supported configuration for mixed-pricing channels and a concrete check of totals in each mode. If this setup is unsupported, state that explicitly.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): At 390 px, the quick start's code blocks soft-wrap with continuation lines indented exactly like real nested lines. generate-migration.ts and the taxOptions block read as different code from what they are, and these are the snippets a developer copies and checks by eye on the main install path. _[quick-start.phone.03, quick-start.phone.06]_ Fix: Either reformat the long snippets so no line exceeds ~42 characters (break at argument and property boundaries), or keep horizontal scroll for code blocks below 640 px instead of soft wrap.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Nothing on the site says who builds or supports VendurePOS: no maintainer, no company, no issue tracker or support link. The demo's analytics go to an unexplained third-party-looking domain (ph.wcpos.com). The only signs of life are a plugin changelog that stops at 2026-10-06 and a till build hash. A sceptic advising a client cannot judge whether this is a maintained product or a weekend project. _[home.desktop.02 footer and comparison table; changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one footer and Overview line naming who maintains it and how (e.g. 'Maintained by <maker of WCPOS>, issues at github.com/vendurepos/app/issues'), and say in the demo row that ph.wcpos.com is that maintainer's own analytics server.
