## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero lead explains Vendure only as 'the open-source headless commerce framework', which tells a visitor who half knows Vendure nothing; and the first thing above the H1 is the badge 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0', which names a plugin before the page has said what the plugin is. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01, hero badge and lead paragraph]_
- J2 (8): The hero subhead talks to developers ("…the open-source headless commerce framework…"), not to a shop owner asking 'can my staff sell with this tomorrow'. On phone the top nav shows only "Docs" and "Live demo", so the staff guide "At the till" is not one tap away. _[home.desktop.01 hero subhead; home.phone.01 top nav]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Hero helper line under the CTAs breaks with an orphan second line 'till in and makes a sale.', leaving a ragged tail under an otherwise tight hero _[home.desktop.01 (hero)]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and who it is for, but nothing above the fold says who should NOT pick it. Someone comparing it with Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed only finds out it is browser-only, has no card-terminal integration, opens no cash drawer and is untested on tablets in 'Before you start', at the bottom of slice 02. _[home.desktop.01 hero vs home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a different brand's domain with no word on home about who wcpos is. A newcomer reads that as an unexplained third party collecting data. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): The limits are stated plainly. One nit: the refund row says "Live demo: Yes" but "Your Vendure store: No, not on plugin 0.3.0 … stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", so the demo shows something the owner's own till cannot do. _[home.desktop.02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, refund row]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): Comparison table cell for 'Refund a sale at the till' under 'Your Vendure store' wraps to four lines, the one dense spot in an otherwise clean, honest comparison _[home.desktop.02 (comparison table)]_
- J5 (7): A known plugin bug is shipped as a documentation workaround instead of a fix. The changelog admits 'Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder, so the till's order history is refused', and the same caveat is repeated in quick-start, connect-a-till (twice) and changelog. Yet the last plugin release is 0.3.0 on 2026-10-06: three releases in five days, then six weeks with no fix. That reads as a project that patches its docs, not its code. _[text/changelog.txt 0.2.0 last bullet; changelog.desktop.01; quick-start 'Optional: a device key'; connect-a-till 'Give each cashier their own account']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Step 4 says 'Sign in with a Vendure administrator whose role in the till's channel has these permissions' and lists six permission names, but it never says where or how to create that role and administrator in the Dashboard. That is sent off to another page ('Give each cashier their own account'), so step 4 cannot be finished from the quick start alone. _[quick-start/desktop/02, '4. Enable bearer authentication']_

But step 3 asks for two hand-made files plus tsx to run the migration, and every refund on 0.3.0 needs the freelancer to run settleRefund, so the owner still depends on a developer after day one. _[quick-start.desktop.02 step 3; limitations.phone.01 refund bullet]_

- J4 (8): Quick start is long and dense: nine steps plus collapsibles, and the bundled-migration alternative sends the reader off to Plugin setup mid-path; long TOC entries such as '3. ## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who arrives from 'Vendure point of sale' without really knowing Vendure gets 'the open-source headless commerce framework' as the only explanation, under a 'plugin 0.3.0' badge, so in the first five seconds it reads as a developer tool rather than a till for their shop. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01, hero]_ Fix: Gloss Vendure in plain words, e.g. 'Vendure, the open-source software that runs your online shop', and change the badge to 'Pre-release' alone or 'Pre-release · free and open source'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): The 'At the till' staff guide mixes live-demo-only features and plugin-0.3.0 caveats into the cashier steps ("Refund at the till (live demo only)"; "On plugin 0.3.0, Order details has no customer note. On the live demo, type a Customer note…"), so staff reading it cannot tell what their own till actually does. _[at-the-till.desktop.01-03, 'Refund at the till' and 'Order details' sections]_ Fix: Write 'At the till' only for a store on plugin 0.3.0: give the refund steps as 'manager refunds in Dashboard → Pay out cash on the till → refund card on terminal', and move the demo-only extras into one 'On the live demo you will also see…' callout or the home comparison table.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Mixed inclusive/exclusive channel pricing is acknowledged without explaining how to choose the required store-wide tax strategy. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:846–875]_ Fix: Add explicit guidance for stores mixing channel pricing modes, stating the supported configuration or its limitation. Include a concrete check comparing the till's tax and grand total with the resulting Vendure order.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): Home 'What it does today' reads as two different components: two bordered cards ('Keeps selling offline', 'Every sale lands once', the right with ~70px empty space above its 'Read the 200-send test' button) sit directly above eight unboxed two-column items _[home.desktop.01]_ Fix: Give all ten items one treatment: either make the two proof items a full-width callout row with buttons aligned to their text, or put the eight items in matching cards; top-align buttons instead of pinning them to the card bottom
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Docs on four pages work around the device-key role's missing ReadOrder permission, and the changelog admits it was left out of the release notes. With no plugin release since 2026-10-06, a sceptic reads it as a pre-release project whose known bugs live on as caveats instead of being fixed, which is the main reason to warn a client off. _[text/changelog.txt 0.2.0 last bullet; quick-start 'Optional: a device key'; connect-a-till 'Create and use a device key']_ Fix: Ship a 0.3.1 plugin that adds ReadOrder to vendurepos-pos-till and remove the four caveats. Until then, add one line to the changelog naming the issue and saying when the fix is due.
