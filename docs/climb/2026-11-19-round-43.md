## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero headline, the two buttons and the till screenshot make the product clear in seconds, but the home page's 'Before you start' block then lists developer details a newcomer cannot parse: 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', 'one tab per till and not a private window'. _[home desktop 02 / phone 04-05, 'Before you start']_
- J2 (8): Hero speaks to developers ("the open-source headless commerce framework"); the note "Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first…" leaves a shop owner with no next step for them, and At the till is only linked at the page bottom ("For cashiers, At the till covers…") and in the nav. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[home.desktop.01.jpg; home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero is heavy on text. A five-line centred lead paragraph, two buttons and a note line all sit above the till screenshot, so the product image starts low on the first screen. Below it, 'What it does today' is ten identical grey cards with no icons or visual anchor, and the section reads as a uniform block. _[home.desktop.01, home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (9): The hero badge says only 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0'; that the till app itself is version 0.1.0 is only revealed deep in the Changelog ('till app version 0.1.0, built 19 Nov 2026'), so the first screen understates how early the till is. _[home/desktop/01 badge vs changelog/desktop/01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero and footer say 'open-source' and 'MIT licensed', but the same page then says building the till yourself 'you need your own RxDB Premium licence token', and the demo row names an unexplained third-party domain ('anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'). To a newcomer both read as a hidden catch. _[home desktop 02 / phone 04-05: 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row, and 'Before you start' bullet 6]_
- J2 (9): Honest, but the refund limit is scattered: "Vendure keeps a refund of a POS order Pending until an administrator settles it with the Admin API's settleRefund mutation" — a shop owner only learns across Limitations and At the till that every counter refund needs their developer. _[limitations.desktop.01, at-the-till.desktop.02]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt; text/changelog.txt]_
- J4 (9): Only small faults. The 'Live demo or your own store' table puts the long refund caveat in a single cell, so one row is much taller than the rest. Otherwise the claims are specific and the limits are stated plainly. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (7): Nowhere on the site says who builds or maintains VendurePOS, how to report a bug or get help (no issues/support/contact link), and the demo's analytics host 'ph.wcpos.com' appears with no explanation of what WCPOS is; a buyer cannot judge whether this is a funded project or a weekend repo. _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure' and footer; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): The quick start is ordered and complete, with a 'Check it worked' list, but step 5 includes an unexplained collapsible, 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app', about a domain the reader has never seen, and step 6 asks them to choose Vendure tax-calculation strategies before they have made a single sale. _[quick-start desktop 02 (bottom) and 03 (top) / phone 05-06]_
- J2 (8): Quick-start step 8 mixes developer and cashier steps; nowhere lists what the developer must hand the counter (app URL, store URL, channel token, one cashier login with the TallyPosSell role plus ReadOrder, barcode field name), and 0.3.0 device keys need ReadOrder added by hand. _[quick-start.desktop.03 (step 8)]_
- J3 (8): The tax instructions prescribe different global strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but tell stores mixing pricing modes only to 'account for the store-wide strategy', leaving their configuration unresolved. _[quick-start.desktop.03.jpg, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:845–874]_
- J4 (7): Quick start has no screenshot of what success looks like: neither the till sign-in screen nor a synced order in the Vendure Dashboard. 'Check it worked' is a text-only checklist, so a first-time reader cannot compare their screen with the expected one. _[quick-start.desktop.03-04, step 8 'Sign in and make a sale']_
- J5 (8): The plugin's own 'New POS till key' button creates a role without ReadOrder, so the device-key path ships broken order history and the reader must patch the role by hand ('add ReadOrder to that role yourself'); a known bug documented as a workaround in two releases instead of fixed. _[quick-start text 'Optional: a device key for each till'; changelog/desktop/01 0.2.0 last bullet 'Not in the release notes']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor reads 'open-source', 'MIT licensed' and 'nothing to install', then hits 'you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and 'usage events to ph.wcpos.com' on the same home page with no explanation, and starts to wonder about paid lock-in and who WCPOS is. _[home desktop 02 / phone 04-05, 'Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start']_ Fix: Say in one plain sentence that the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com is free to use and the RxDB licence only matters if you build the till yourself (or move that bullet to Connect a till), and add a few words saying what ph.wcpos.com is (the project's own analytics server).
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): No clear who-does-what handoff for a shop owner: nothing lists what the freelancer must give the counter before staff can sell; quick-start step 8 mixes developer and cashier steps. _[quick-start.desktop.03 (step 8), home.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a short 'Before your staff sell' box on Connect a till / At the till, linked from the home hero note: app URL, store URL, channel token, one cashier login per staff member with TallyPosSell + ReadOrder, barcode field name.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both inclusive and exclusive prices must guess how to choose the required store-wide tax strategy. _[Quick start, step 6 and 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:845–874]_ Fix: Add an explicit mixed-pricing case stating the supported configuration and how to verify totals in both channel types. If that configuration is unsupported, state that limitation directly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The figures on At the till have no single treatment. One runs full width with no padding (the payment screen), one is about 420 px and centred inside a padded frame (the cashier sheet), and one is about 545 px in another padded frame (closure). On phone the cashier sheet also shows a grey scrim strip on its left edge. The docs look assembled rather than designed. _[at-the-till.desktop.02, at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.desktop.04, at-the-till.phone.06]_ Fix: Define one figure component for every docs screenshot. Crop each image tight to the UI so it carries no baked-in padding or scrim. Give every figure the same frame (1 px border, the same radius, no inner padding) and the same width rule: full content width for wide screens, and a single fixed max-width (e.g. 480 px), centred, for phone-shaped sheets. Add a short caption under each.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): There is no sign of who stands behind VendurePOS or where a user gets help: no maintainer or company named, no issue tracker or support link, and the only hint (demo events sent to 'ph.wcpos.com') is unexplained. Combined with the last plugin release being 6 Oct with a known permissions bug left as a manual workaround, a sceptic would advise a client that this may be a one-person side project with no support path. _[home/desktop/02 footer and live-demo table; changelog/desktop/01]_ Fix: Add one line to the home footer and Overview naming who builds and maintains it (and that ph.wcpos.com belongs to them), with links to GitHub Issues for bugs and a support channel.
