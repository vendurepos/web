## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons, and 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' look like the same thing to a newcomer. 'Try with your store' opens app.vendurepos.com, which cannot work until the plugin is installed, and the only warning is the small grey line underneath ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'). _[home/desktop/01 hero buttons; home/phone/01]_
- J2 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons, "Live demo", "Quick start", "Try with your store" and "GitHub". Nothing tells a non-developer owner how "Try with your store" differs from "Quick start", or which one to press first. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01, hero button row]_
- J3 (9): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): The hero offers three overlapping entry points ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store') and needs a caption line underneath ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first: the quick start sets it up.') to explain that 'Try with your store' won't work yet, so the CTA row needs a footnote to make sense. _[home/desktop/01 (hero button row and the caption under it)]_
- J5 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), and 'Try with your store' looks as prominent as Quick start even though it cannot work until the plugin is installed. The only warning is the small grey line under the buttons: 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, hero buttons]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)

A first-time visitor sees an unknown brand's domain in the privacy line, and the explanation (a self-hosted PostHog) only appears on Connect a till. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (8): Refunds at the till and the Customer note work in the live demo, but the page says they need "the plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet" and gives no date. The newest changelog entry is still 0.3.0 (2026-10-06), so an owner can't tell when the demo will match what they can install. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 "Refund a sale"; changelog.desktop.01 newest entry 0.3.0]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The phrase 'the plugin release after 0.3.0' comes back on home, At the till, Connect a till and Limitations (refunds, customer note, ReadOrder) with no version number or date, so the store column of the comparison table promises things against a release nobody can track. _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Refund a sale at the till'; at-the-till/desktop/02 'Refund a sale'; limitations/desktop/01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): In its first seconds a newcomer has four equal choices: Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store and GitHub. 'Try with your store' sounds like the easy option but opens an app that fails without a server plugin, and the warning is the small grey caption below the buttons. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 hero]_ Fix: Keep 'Live demo' as the primary button and 'Quick start' as the secondary one. Take 'Try with your store' out of the hero (it is already in the top nav) or relabel it 'Open the app (plugin required)'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): Troubleshooting tells the reader "Ask the store owner to look at the sync log in this till's Settings", but the reader is the store owner. When register changes fail to send, the owner gets no steps: "till updates" isn't defined, and the page says nothing about what the sync log shows or how to fix or discard a refused update. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01, "N till updates need attention" line]_ Fix: Address the owner directly. Define till updates as register changes (opening float, pay in/out, closures). Say what the sync log in Settings shows for a refused update and the exact action (e.g. which button resends or discards it and when to do each). Use one name, "Send again" or "Retry", throughout.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): A developer handling a needs_admin sale must guess which existing order, payment and stock state justifies applied versus rejected before executing the recovery mutation. _[shots/troubleshooting.desktop.02.jpg, 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way']_ Fix: Add a runnable listing query and a short decision procedure explaining what to inspect before each resolution. State what each resolution changes and how to verify recovery before retrying the sale.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The 'Live demo or your own store' table, the one place the home page sets demo against real store, has unbalanced columns: the longest cell (the demo's 'No: ... ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie') wraps onto 4 lines in the narrowest column, while the store column next to it holds 'Yes' in ~440 px of empty space. The row looks broken, and the most important caveat on the page is the hardest to read. _[home/desktop/02 table, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_ Fix: Give the table fixed column widths (for example 30% / 35% / 35%), or shorten that cell to 'No: the demo store lives in your browser' and move the analytics sentence into the intro line above the table.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site promotes 'open-source', 'MIT licensed' and 'Source on GitHub', but self-hosting the till requires a paid third-party RxDB Premium licence. That fact is buried in Limitations and Connect a till, so a client who cannot or will not buy one depends on the hosted app.vendurepos.com run by an unnamed maintainer. The home page and Overview never say this, which is the kind of thing that would make me advise a client against adopting it. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start' and footer; docs.desktop.01 'Next steps' 'Source on GitHub'; limitations.desktop.01 bullet 3]_ Fix: Add one line to the home page's 'Before you start' and the Overview's Requirements: 'Use the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com, or build it yourself with a paid RxDB Premium licence'. Link it to Limitations, and name who runs the hosted app.
