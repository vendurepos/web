## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero leans on jargon a searcher may not know ('till', 'the open-source headless commerce framework') without a plain-words line, and the first feature card 'Keeps selling offline' opens with a dense block of caveats ('a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them') before the reader knows why offline matters. _[home/desktop/01 hero paragraph and 'Keeps selling offline' card]_
- J2 (8): The hero's two buttons are 'Live demo' and 'Quick start' (a developer guide of npm installs and migrations); the staff guide 'At the till' only appears in the desktop top nav and in a sentence near the footer ('For cashiers, At the till covers selling...'), and on phone the header shows only 'Docs' and 'Live demo' with the rest behind a chevron. _[home.phone.01 header and hero buttons; home.desktop.02 closing paragraph]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): On phone the in-app category chip row in the hero till screenshot is cut mid-word ('Gif') at the screenshot frame edge, so the first image reads as clipped rather than intentionally cropped. _[home.phone.01 (hero screenshot, chip row)]_
- J5 (8): The home page never says who should not use it or how it differs from a hosted till (Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed): the deal-breakers for a buyer (no card-terminal integration, no cash drawer, iPad and tablets 'not tested yet', one tab per till) sit only in the third bullet of 'Before you start' near the bottom, not near the hero promise 'made for shops that sell online with Vendure and also sell in person'. _[home/desktop/01 hero vs home/desktop/02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): Nowhere on the home page does it say who builds or maintains VendurePOS (footer only says 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI.'). The only clue to an owner is an unexplained third-party domain in the comparison table: 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row, and footer]_
- J2 (9): The demo row says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' without saying what wcpos.com is or why a VendurePOS demo reports to another product's domain, which leaves an owner wondering who gets the data. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home.phone.04]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The product screenshots disagree on clock format: the payment screen shows 'Last synced 10:06 PM' while the cashier sheet shows 'Register · open since 22:06'. A sharp reader notices that two captures of the same till don't match. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 vs at-the-till.desktop.03]_
- J5 (7): The site says 'MIT licensed' and 'open-source' in the hero and footer, but self-hosting the till 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. It never says this is a paid third-party commercial licence or what it costs. It never names who runs app.vendurepos.com, the default till every merchant is sent to, so a client cannot judge the lock-in. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet; connect-a-till/desktop/01; limitations 'Setting up the store']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): 'Check it worked' starts with 'Once the till shows the order as synced' but the quick start never says what 'synced' looks like on the till (the header text 'N orders waiting to send' / Orders status 'Synced' is only in Troubleshooting), and step 8 has no screenshot of the sign-in screen or a synced sale. _[quick-start/desktop/04 'Check it worked'; quick-start/desktop/03 step 8]_
- J2 (8): Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' and 'Check it worked' have no screenshot of the sign-in screen or of what 'the till shows the order as synced' looks like, so the success state at the till is described only in words. _[quick-start.desktop.03-04 / quick-start.phone.07-08]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 prescribes different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but only says a store mixing pricing modes 'must account for the store-wide strategy', leaving that configuration decision unresolved. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:873–875]_
- J4 (8): Step 8 packs nine lettered sub-actions (a–i) into one step with no image of the sign-in screen or of the synced order in the Dashboard, and 'Check it worked' is text only, so the densest step in the path has no visual confirmation. _[quick-start.desktop.03–04, step 8 and 'Check it worked']_
- J5 (8): The fact that the hosted https till can only reach an https store (plain http only for localhost) is not a prerequisite before 'Open https://app.vendurepos.com'. It turns up only after failure, in '9. Troubleshoot' ('Use HTTPS for a remote store'), so a developer pointing it at an http staging server gets stuck at sign-in. The plugin's own 'New POS till key' role also lacks ReadOrder, so order history is refused until you edit the role by hand. _[quick-start/desktop/03-04 steps 8 and 9; quick-start 'Optional: a device key for each till']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A first-time visitor cannot tell who is behind VendurePOS: no maker, team or company is named on the home page or the docs overview. The only hint is 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' in the comparison table, which makes an unknown third-party domain the first sign of ownership. _[home/desktop/02 comparison table last row and footer]_ Fix: Add one footer or hero line naming who builds and maintains VendurePOS (with a link), and say in the table row that ph.wcpos.com is the maker's own self-hosted analytics server.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): My first question, what happens when the internet drops, is answered in clear text but never shown: At the till's 'If the internet drops' section has no picture of the till's header in the offline state, so I can't show my staff the 'N orders waiting to send' line they must watch for and must not reload over. _[at-the-till.desktop.03 / at-the-till.phone.07, section 'If the internet drops']_ Fix: Add one captioned screenshot of the live demo till's header showing '2 orders waiting to send' next to the 'Keep the till's tab open' warning, like the payment and closure screenshots already on that page.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The tax setup leaves developers with both inclusive and exclusive channels to determine how the two prescribed strategies fit one store-wide setting. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:873–875]_ Fix: State whether mixed pricing modes are supported and provide the supported configuration or an explicit limitation beside the strategy table.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): Home 'What it does today' gives one feature list two treatments: two bordered cards of unequal content (the 'Every sale lands once' card has ~90 px of dead space above its button), then eight unboxed text items. The section looks unfinished, as if the card treatment ran out partway. _[home.desktop.01, 'What it does today' section]_ Fix: Use one component for all ten features: either equal-height cards in a 2-column grid with buttons pinned to the card bottom, or no boxes at all, with the two test links as inline 'See the test →' links under their items.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): 'MIT licensed, open-source' is the headline, but leaving the hosted app.vendurepos.com means buying an RxDB Premium licence the site never calls paid or prices, and the site never says who operates the hosted till. A sceptic advising a client sees a till whose only free path depends on an unnamed operator. _[home/desktop/02 'Setting up the store' last bullet; footer 'MIT licensed'; connect-a-till/desktop/01]_ Fix: On the home page and Limitations, say in one line that RxDB Premium is a paid third-party licence (with a link to its pricing) and name who runs app.vendurepos.com and what happens to a store if it goes away.
