## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero lede is one 47-word sentence with a parenthetical definition stuffed in the middle ("...signs in to your own Vendure store (Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework). It keeps selling..."), and the badge above the H1 opens with jargon ("Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0") before the page has said what a plugin is for; it reads as four dense grey lines rather than a five-second answer. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01, hero]_
- J2 (8): Everything above the fold speaks to the developer (top nav 'Docs / Live demo / Quick start', the hero line 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'). The page for staff and owners, 'At the till', is only linked in the last paragraph above the footer ('For cashiers, At the till covers selling, taking payment and closing the day'). _[home/desktop/01 hero and nav; home/desktop/02 last paragraph; home/phone/05]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero carries three stacked text blocks of body copy: a 4-line centred paragraph with a parenthetical definition ('(Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework)'), then the buttons, then another sentence ('Your own store needs the plugin…'). At 390 px the centred paragraph runs to 7 lines, so the buttons only appear after a long read. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The home page never says who builds or maintains VendurePOS, or where to get support or report a bug; the footer offers only 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.', and the demo's analytics go to an unexplained third-party domain, ph.wcpos.com. A buyer weighing it against Shopify POS or Square cannot tell who stands behind it. _[home/desktop/02 footer and 'Live demo or your own store' table; text/home.txt]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo "sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com", a different domain that the home page never explains (who is wcpos?), so a privacy line meant to build trust makes a newcomer wonder whose product this really is. _[home.desktop.02 table row "Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline"; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): The home table says a Dashboard refund 'stays Pending until it is settled' but not that settling needs the Admin API's settleRefund mutation (only Limitations says so). An owner reading the home page would not see that every refund needs a developer. _[home/desktop/02 'Refund a sale at the till' row vs limitations/desktop/01 bullet 4]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): In the changelog, references such as '(ADR 0005)', '(ADR 0004)', '(#116)' and '(#117)' are plain unlinked text, so a reader cannot follow them to check the claim behind each entry. _[changelog.desktop.01, 0.3.0 and 0.2.0 bullets]_
- J5 (7): The hero, og card and JSON-LD ('isAccessibleForFree': true) present it as an open-source, free till. Only a later bullet says self-hosting needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token', and nowhere does it say that this is a paid proprietary licence or what it costs. The free route therefore depends on app.vendurepos.com, whose operator is never named. The latest plugin release is 2026-10-06 (0.2.0 and 0.3.0 share that date), six weeks before capture, while the demo already does refunds and customer notes the released plugin cannot. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' bullet 5; limitations/desktop/01; changelog/desktop/01; tech.json home JSON-LD]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Step 3 makes you hand-write two TypeScript scripts (src/generate-migration.ts and src/run-migrations.ts) and step 5 assumes "the scaffold's CORS_ORIGINS allowlist" exists without saying where it lives, so a developer who did not start from @vendure/create has to guess. _[quick-start.desktop.01-02, steps 3 and 5]_
- J2 (8): Quick start step 4 has the developer create cashier admins with five named permissions. The optional device-key section then says the plugin's own 'vendurepos-pos-till' role on 0.3.0 lacks ReadOrder, 'so add ReadOrder to that role yourself'. That is a known gotcha left to the user, it sits at the bottom of the page, and step 8 still assumes order history works. _[quick-start text 'Optional: a device key for each till'; quick-start/desktop/02 step 4]_
- J3 (8): The quick-start prerequisites omit Node ^20.19 || >=22.12, which Plugin setup requires, allowing readers to begin installation without checking the runtime requirement. _[text/quick-start.txt, introduction; text/plugin-setup.txt, Check your store]_
- J4 (8): The 6,300 px quick start has no picture of the sign-in screen or of the synced order in the Vendure Dashboard. Step 8 is a nine-item lettered list (a–i), and 'Check it worked' is five text bullets, so a reader never sees what success looks like. _[quick-start.desktop.03 (step 8 and 'Check it worked'); quick-start.phone.07-08]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A newcomer who has never heard of VendurePOS reads on the home page that the demo sends usage events to "ph.wcpos.com", a third-party-looking domain that is never explained, which undercuts the otherwise careful, honest tone at the moment they are deciding whether to try the demo. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04, "Live demo or your own store" table, last row]_ Fix: Name the owner in the same cell, e.g. "...to our self-hosted PostHog at ph.wcpos.com (run by WCPOS, the team behind VendurePOS), with no cookie", or link "anonymous usage events" straight to the Connect a till section that already explains it.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): When the store refuses a register update (open, close, cash movement or closure) with any code other than the two named ones, Troubleshooting says the till will never resend it and that nothing clears it. It gives the owner no way out and does not say whether that day's closure and cash records ever reach Vendure. For someone who has to close the day, that is a dead end that breaks trust. _[troubleshooting/desktop/01 'Any other code:' bullet (also limitations/desktop/01 'When the store refuses a till update' bullet)]_ Fix: Under 'Any other code', add the next step in plain words: what is kept on the till and what is missing in Vendure, that the printed Z report is still the day's record, and where to report it (a GitHub issue link) with the copied log line.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The changelog's claim that the generated device-key role does everything a till does conflicts with the setup instructions requiring ReadOrder to be added manually. _[shots/changelog.desktop.01.jpg, 0.2.0 final bullet; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, Changelog]_ Fix: Annotate the changelog claim with the current ReadOrder requirement and link to the device-key setup instructions. Include that qualification in llms-full.txt.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The docs landing page's 'Next steps' block has three cards plus a full-width, right-aligned pager card in the same style, which reads as a stray fourth card. The step the reader needs most, Quick start, sits only in that pager card, while Plugin setup gets a primary card, and Quick start also appears twice in the sidebar. _[docs.desktop.01 / docs.phone.02]_ Fix: Make Quick start the first 'Next steps' card, with Live demo, Plugin setup and GitHub in a 2x2 grid, and give the prev/next pager a distinct borderless style. Drop the duplicate 'Quick start' from the sidebar's top link group.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells 'an open-source till' (hero, og card, JSON-LD isAccessibleForFree) but buries the fact that running it on your own host requires a paid, proprietary RxDB Premium licence. The cost is never stated, and the only free alternative is the hosted app.vendurepos.com, run by an operator the site never names, with no support or maintainer contact. A sceptic advising a client reads that as hidden lock-in on a pre-release project whose plugin has not shipped since 2026-10-06. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start'; limitations/desktop/01; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where your data goes']_ Fix: In 'Before you start' and Limitations, state plainly that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence, with a link to its pricing. Add one line naming who runs app.vendurepos.com and maintains the project, with a support or issues link.
