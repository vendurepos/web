## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but past the feature list the home page turns into a spec sheet for someone who has not decided yet: the 'Before you start' block lists 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and 'Vendure 3.6 or a later 3.x release on Postgres (tested on 3.7.3)', and the comparison table says a refund 'stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls'. A newcomer has to wade through admin and config detail on the marketing page. _[home desktop slice 02 / phone slices 04-05 ('Before you start', 'Refund a sale at the till' row)]_
- J2 (8): The hero speaks to developers only: "VendurePOS is an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework", and its two buttons are "Live demo" and "Quick start" (a server install guide). The hero has no entry for a shop owner or cashier. "At the till" appears only in the desktop nav and the closing paragraph. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Hero subcopy under the headline is a four-line centred paragraph; centred multi-line body text is slow to scan and weakens an otherwise tight hero (clear product line, Live demo / Quick start CTAs, till screenshot). _[home.desktop.01 hero]_
- J5 (9): The hero's proof image, captioned 'A sale in progress on the live demo till.', is scaled down so far on desktop that its prices, tax lines and 'Checkout €20.09' render at roughly 9 px and cannot be read without zooming. _[home.desktop.01 (hero screenshot)]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The live demo row says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'. A visitor who has never heard of VendurePOS now sees data going to a different, unnamed brand's domain (wcpos.com) with no word on who that is, so an honest disclosure reads like a hidden third party. _[home desktop slice 02 and phone slice 04, 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' row]_
- J2 (9): The home comparison table shows "Refund a sale at the till" as Yes in the demo column, but on the owner's own store the refund "stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". A quick reader can take the Yes away and miss the caveat. _[home.desktop.02 (Live demo or your own store table)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The demo privacy line "All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com" names a different brand's domain (wcpos) with no word on who that is, which reads as an unexplained third party on an otherwise candid site (Limitations page, pre-release pill, linked 200-send test). _[home (demo section), home.phone slices]_
- J5 (7): Nowhere does the site say who builds or maintains VendurePOS, or where to report a bug or get support. The only clues are a footer 'Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' and an unexplained third-party domain, 'ph.wcpos.com', in the demo row of the comparison table. Separately, 'Barcode scanners … work out of the box' oversells a feature that needs a ProductVariant custom field and a database migration (Connect a till). _[home.desktop.02 (table row 'Sales reach Vendure…' and footer); home.desktop.01 'Barcode scanners']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): Step 7 'Prepare the channel and stock' is a checklist with no how-to or Dashboard path: 'Check its currency, pricesIncludeTax, default tax zone and enabled tax rates (including a zero rate where appropriate)' leaves the reader to guess what 'usable default stock location' and 'where appropriate' mean, while every other step gives exact code or commands. _[quick-start desktop slice 03 / phone slice 07, step 7]_
- J2 (8): Step 8, "Sign in and make a sale", sends you to app.vendurepos.com but has no screenshot of the sign-in screen, so after nine developer steps (migrations, CORS, tokenMethod, tax strategy) the owner cannot see what the staff login looks like. The optional device key section was also clipped in the capture. _[quick-start.desktop.03/04 (step 8), tech.json clipped=true]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 prescribes different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but 'A store mixing pricing modes must account for the store-wide strategy' leaves developers without a configuration recommendation or verification procedure for that case. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:876–905]_
- J4 (9): Step 3 asks the reader to hand-write two TypeScript files (generate-migration.ts and run-migrations.ts) before the first sale; the path is ordered and has a "Check it worked" step, but this is the one point where a copy-paste path turns into authoring. _[quick-start.desktop.02 / quick-start.phone.03]_
- J5 (8): Step 8c asks for 'an existing single text ProductVariant custom field, such as barcode' (how to add one). Adding that field means another database migration, so a developer who followed step 3 has to go back and generate and run a second migration. Step 3 never mentions declaring the barcode field before migrating. _[quick-start.desktop.03 bottom / quick-start.phone.08 step c]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The home page tells a first-time visitor that the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' without saying who wcpos is or how it relates to VendurePOS. Right before the 'try the demo' decision, an unexplained third-party domain is exactly what makes a stranger hesitate. _[home desktop slice 02 / phone slice 04, comparison table, last row]_ Fix: Name the owner in the same sentence, e.g. 'to our own analytics server (ph.wcpos.com, run by WCPOS, the team behind VendurePOS)', and keep the link to 'Where your data goes'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): The home page gives a shop owner or cashier no route of their own. The hero offers only "Live demo" and "Quick start", a developer install guide with migrations, CORS and tax strategies. The cashier guide "At the till" is missing from the hero and hidden from the phone header, so staff who need to sell tomorrow have to hunt for it. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_ Fix: Add a third hero link, "For cashiers: At the till", and keep "At the till" visible in the phone header next to Docs and Live demo.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both inclusive and exclusive pricing must guess how to resolve the conflicting recommendations for the single store-wide tax strategy. _[Quick start, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:876–905]_ Fix: Add a tested recommendation for mixed-pricing channels, or explicitly state the unsupported combination. Include how to verify receipt and Vendure tax totals for each pricing mode before accepting live sales.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): The cashier-sheet figure on At the till carries a grey vertical strip down its left edge and stray lines (a crop of the sheet overlay), and on desktop the same page's three till figures sit at three different widths (full column, ~380 px centred card, ~510 px centred card). _[at-the-till.phone.07; at-the-till.desktop.02-04]_ Fix: Re-crop the cashier-sheet screenshot to the sheet's own bounds with no overlay edge, and give every till figure one rule: same max width (full content column or one fixed figure width), same frame, border and radius, and the same top/bottom spacing.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A buyer cannot tell who is behind VendurePOS, how it is supported, or whether it is maintained. The site names no maintainer, gives no issue or support route beyond a bare GitHub icon, and leaves 'ph.wcpos.com' (a different brand) unexplained on the home page. The plugin changelog shows nothing since 2026-10-06. Together these are exactly what would make me advise a client to wait. _[home.desktop.02 (demo row and footer); changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one line to 'Before you start' or the footer that names who maintains VendurePOS and says what wcpos.com is, and link the GitHub issue tracker as the support channel. Add a short till-app entry to the changelog so recent activity is visible.
