## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the home page then turns into a server checklist a newcomer can't parse: 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and 'settles it with two Admin API calls' sit on the marketing page under 'Before you start' and in the comparison table. _[home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' table, 'Before you start' > 'Setting up the store'); home.phone.04-05]_
- J2 (9): The hero sub-copy frames the product for developers ("open-source headless commerce framework") before it tells a shop owner what staff do at the counter; the till screenshot and "Point of sale for Vendure" carry it otherwise. _[home.desktop.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero lede under 'Point of sale for Vendure' is a 5-line centered paragraph ending on the one-line widow 'connection drops.'. On phone it grows to 6 lines, so the CTAs end up below a block of grey text before the eye reaches the till screenshot. _[home.desktop.01, home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): Nothing near the top tells a buyer who it is not for or how it sits against alternatives: the hero says what it is and who it suits, but a shop weighing it against Shopify POS, Square or a hosted till only learns that it is desktop-Chrome only and has no card-terminal link, cash drawer or coupons further down, in 'Before you start' and on Limitations. _[home.desktop.01 hero / home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The demo row says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'. That is a third domain under another brand that the site never explains, and it appears right before the visitor clicks Live demo. _[home.desktop.02, 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' row; home.phone.04]_
- J2 (9): The 'Live demo or your own store' table says the demo "sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com" without saying who or what wcpos is, so an honest disclosure reads as an unexplained third party. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J3 (9): The acceptance-run and 200-send claims link to test source on main without identifying a passing run or tested revision. _[text/home.txt, What it does today; tech.json, home.desktop.links]_
- J4 (9): The Refund row of the 'Live demo or your own store' table runs to 4 lines in the third column ('two Admin API calls...') against a bare 'Yes' in the second. The row gets visually heavy, though the content is honest. _[home.desktop.02]_
- J5 (8): The site never names who builds or maintains VendurePOS. The only clues are the footer's 'Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' and the demo analytics host 'ph.wcpos.com' (the domain of another POS product), which is not explained. On top of that, the changelog shows the plugin's last release as 0.3.0 on 2026-10-06, 'the same day as 0.2.0, a few hours later', six weeks ago. A sceptic cannot tell whether a team stands behind it. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure…' and footer; changelog.desktop.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): Just before the visitor tries the demo, the comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. That domain belongs to an unexplained other brand, so a newcomer who has never heard of VendurePOS is left asking who receives their data. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home.phone.04]_ Fix: Name who runs ph.wcpos.com and why, in a few plain words (e.g. 'usage analytics run by the VendurePOS maintainers'), or move the analytics detail to the linked data page and keep a plain 'nothing leaves your browser except anonymous usage stats' in the table.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till mixes plugin-0.3.0 limits and live-demo-only steps into the cashier instructions ("On plugin 0.3.0 the till cannot refund", "Refund at the till (live demo only)", "On plugin 0.3.0, Order details has no customer note. On the live demo…"), so staff reading it on a phone can't tell which steps work on their own store. _[at-the-till.desktop.03 / at-the-till.phone.05]_ Fix: Open the page with one 'On your own store (plugin 0.3.0)' box listing what the till can't do yet (refunds, customer note), write the cashier steps for the real store only, and move the demo-only refund steps to a separate collapsed 'On the live demo' section.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both inclusive and exclusive pricing must guess how to satisfy the conflicting store-wide tax-strategy recommendations. _[Quick start, step 6 and Why these pairings; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:893-895]_ Fix: Document the supported strategy and receipt-versus-order verification steps for mixed pricing modes. If that configuration is unverified, state that boundary explicitly instead of leaving 'must account' as the instruction.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The 'At the till' figures are framed inconsistently: a full-width payment screen with ~420 px of dead cart space, a ~420 px cashier sheet in a double-bordered padded box, and a ~546 px closure box with ~50 px of empty top padding. Three widths and three framing styles on one page. _[at-the-till.desktop.02-04]_ Fix: Crop each screenshot tight to its content (cut the empty cart column; drop the outer padded frame), render all three at one content width with the same 1 px border and radius as the home till image, and give each the same caption style.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): No maintainer, company or team is named anywhere. The only trace is the unexplained third-party analytics host 'ph.wcpos.com', and the changelog shows no plugin release since 2026-10-06. A competitor-aware reviewer cannot tell a client who supports this till or whether it is still active, which is the first question before recommending an open-source POS for a live shop. _[home.desktop.02 footer and live-demo table; connect-a-till 'Where your data goes'; changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one line to the home footer and the Overview naming who builds and maintains VendurePOS, how it relates to WCPOS, TallyUI and MedusaPOS (which explains ph.wcpos.com), and where to get support. Link it to the GitHub org.
