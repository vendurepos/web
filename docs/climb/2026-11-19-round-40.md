## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The top of the home page is clear, but its second half reads like a developer spec sheet: 'Before you start' lists 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and plugin-version refund caveats. A newcomer who only half knows Vendure hits this jargon before any plain 'who this is for' line. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start'; home/phone/04-05]_
- J2 (8): The hero speaks to developers ('Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework') and the page my staff need, 'At the till', is not a hero button: it shows only in the top nav and a paragraph at the bottom. _[home.desktop.01 hero (CTAs are only 'Live demo' and 'Quick start')]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero helper line under the buttons ("Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first: the quick start sets it up…") is set on a wider measure (~830 px) than the lead paragraph above it (~640 px), so a one-line strip of small grey text overhangs the centred column and breaks the hero's shape. _[home.desktop.01, hero below the Live demo / Quick start buttons]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is ('Point of sale for Vendure', 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0') but nowhere says who builds or maintains it or who it is not for yet, so a buyer weighing it against Shopify POS or Square gets no 'not for you if…' framing up front. _[home desktop.01 hero / home phone.01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The demo row of the comparison table says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'. The home page never says what wcpos.com is or why it is involved, so to a first-timer it looks like an unrelated third party tracking them. The explanation that it is a self-hosted PostHog server is only on Connect a till. _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): Two version numbers with no explanation on the home page: the hero badge says 'plugin 0.3.0' while the hosted till is 'till app version 0.1.0', explained only in the Changelog. The demo's analytics go to 'ph.wcpos.com', a domain from another brand, also unexplained. _[home.desktop.01 badge / changelog.desktop.01 intro / home.desktop.02 comparison table]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): The comparison table's demo cell names a foreign domain on a VendurePOS page: "All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie". It is honest, but the wcpos.com host is unexplained and looks like a leftover from another product. The Refund cell also wraps to three lines ("No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard, where the refund stays Pending until it is settled"), which makes that row the tallest. _[home.desktop, "Live demo or your own store" table]_
- J5 (7): The footer says 'MIT licensed', yet self-building the till needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' with no price or terms, and nothing says who runs app.vendurepos.com, whether it stays free, or why usage goes to 'ph.wcpos.com'. _[home desktop.02 'Before you start' + footer; connect-a-till]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (8): Step 4 says to sign in with an administrator whose role has TallyPosSell, ReadCatalog, ReadSettings, ReadCustomer, CreateCustomer and ReadOrder. These are spread across a prose paragraph, and the quick start gives no steps for creating that role; it sends you to another page ('Give each cashier their own account'). There is also a leftover collapsed note, 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app', about an old domain a newcomer never saw. _[quick-start/desktop/02 step 4 and step 5 collapsible]_
- J2 (8): Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' has no picture of the sign-in screen. With the optional device key, the till role on 0.3.0 'has no ReadOrder', so order history is refused until someone adds it by hand. A non-developer would not know how to do that. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8 / quick-start text 'Create and use a device key']_
- J3 (8): The tax instructions prescribe different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but only say a store mixing pricing modes 'must account for the store-wide strategy', leaving its configuration unresolved. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:823-849, Quick start step 6]_
- J4 (7): Quick start is 9 numbered steps of text and code with no screenshot of the till's sign-in screen or of the first synced order in the Vendure Dashboard. The reader never sees what success looks like. "Check it worked" is an unnumbered H3 wedged between steps 8 and 9, so the path's rhythm breaks right at the payoff. _[quick-start.desktop.01-05, steps 7-9]_
- J5 (8): The path works only after a manual fix for a known shipped bug ('On plugin 0.3.0 that role has no ReadOrder: add it yourself'), and step 5 has an unexplained collapsed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app', which names a different domain from the one the reader uses. _[quick-start desktop.02 steps 4–5]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The home page tells a first-time visitor that the demo sends usage events to 'ph.wcpos.com' without saying who or what wcpos.com is. A newcomer just learning what VendurePOS is reads that as an unknown third-party tracker. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04, comparison table, 'Live demo' cell of 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_ Fix: Name it in place, e.g. 'anonymous usage events to our own self-hosted PostHog analytics (ph.wcpos.com), no cookie'. Or link the words straight to the Connect a till 'Where requests go' section.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till, the page staff read at the counter, spends a long section on 'Refund at the till (live demo)', a flow that does not work on plugin 0.3.0. A shop owner skimming on a phone can easily think staff can refund at the till. _[at-the-till.desktop.02-03, section 'Refund at the till (live demo)']_ Fix: Collapse that section into one short boxed note labelled 'Live demo only, not on plugin 0.3.0' (or move it to the demo page). The At the till refund section should then only describe what works today: refund in the Vendure Dashboard, record cash with Pay out.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to satisfy the two different recommendations for one store-wide tax strategy. _[text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:823-849, Quick start step 6]_ Fix: State the supported configuration and expected rounding behavior for mixed-pricing channels, or explicitly identify this configuration as unsupported.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a 390 px phone, the first image under the hero is a 1.2-screen till capture on the Products tab: two rows of product cards, the chip row cut off at "Gif", and the cart hidden behind the "Cart (3) · €20.09" tab. The caption promises "A sale in progress on the live demo till." and the picture shows none. _[home.phone.01-02, hero image and caption]_ Fix: Serve a phone-specific capture of the Cart view with the three lines, totals and Pay button, cropped to at most ~0.8 screen height (or use the desktop till image with object-position on the cart). Keep the caption only if the image shows the sale.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The till is sold as 'MIT licensed', but self-hosting needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' with no cost or terms. The site also never says who operates app.vendurepos.com (or the ph.wcpos.com analytics), whether the hosted till stays free, or for how long. A client cannot judge lock-in or total cost against Shopify POS or Square. _[home desktop.02 'Before you start' and footer; connect-a-till self-build steps]_ Fix: Add a short 'Who runs it and what it costs' block on home: name the maintainer behind vendurepos.com and ph.wcpos.com, state that the hosted till is free (and on what terms), and say what an RxDB Premium licence costs, noting it is needed only to build the till yourself.
