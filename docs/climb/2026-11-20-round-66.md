## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but just below it the home page turns into developer jargon a first-timer can't parse: the comparison table's refund cell says "The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls", and 'Before you start' lists "Node ^20.19 || >=22.12" and "RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)". _[home/desktop/02 (table 'Refund a sale at the till' row, 'Setting up the store' list); home/phone/04-05]_
- J2 (8): The hero's two buttons are 'Live demo' and 'Quick start' (a developer config walkthrough), and the hero copy leads with 'headless commerce framework'. The staff guide only shows up in the closing paragraph ('For cashiers, At the till covers selling, taking payment and closing the day'). On phone, the top bar shows only 'Docs' and 'Live demo', so 'At the till' is hidden. _[home.phone.01 (header, hero) and home.phone.05 (closing paragraph)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[home.desktop.01.jpg; home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero itself is clean: a 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' pill, the H1 'Point of sale for Vendure', two buttons and a large till screenshot. Below it, the 'Live demo or your own store' comparison table loses its Yes/Yes rhythm in two rows. The 'Refund a sale at the till' cell runs to 4 lines ('No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls; see Plugin setup'), and the last row also holds a long cell ('No: the demo store lives in your browser. All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'). The comparison stops reading at a glance. _[home.desktop.02 (comparison table)]_
- J5 (8): The hero screenshot shows the cart's 'Add charge' button, but 'What it does today' never mentions custom items, fees or shipping charges. Those are the headline of the latest plugin release (0.3.0), so a buyer comparing feature lists misses them. _[home/desktop/01 hero image vs 'What it does today' grid; changelog 0.3.0 'In short']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)

The only clue is an unexplained third-party domain in the demo row ("All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com"), and the footer's "Built with TallyUI. - J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the rest of the home page talks to someone who already runs a Vendure server. The table and 'Before you start' use 'two Admin API calls', 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12' and 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', and the page never says who makes VendurePOS. A first-time visitor half-sure what Vendure is loses the thread, and the demo stays the only clear next step. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04-05]_ Fix: On the home page, cut the refund cell to "No, not yet: refund in the Vendure Dashboard (see Limitations)". Move the Node range and the RxDB self-hosting note to the Overview or Connect a till page, and add one footer line saying who builds and maintains VendurePOS.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): A shop owner checking on a phone whether staff can sell tomorrow gets two choices: a demo, or a developer setup guide. The page written for them ('At the till': selling, payment, closing the day, what happens when the internet drops) is missing from the phone header and the hero, and only appears in the last paragraph of the page. _[home.phone.01 hero and header; home.phone.05 closing paragraph]_ Fix: Under the hero buttons, add one line such as 'Running the counter? At the till covers selling, the internet dropping and closing the day', linked to /docs/at-the-till, and keep 'At the till' visible in the phone header.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Developers with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels must guess how to reconcile the two recommended tax strategies with a store-wide setting. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:874]_ Fix: Document the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and how to verify its totals. If that combination is unsupported, state the limitation explicitly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): On the home page, 'What it does today' renders 2 of its 10 features as bordered cards with buttons, and those two cards have unequal heights (empty gap above 'Read the 200-send test'). The other 8 features are borderless text pairs, so the site's main feature section looks unfinished. _[home.desktop.01]_ Fix: Give all 10 features one treatment. Either use borderless pairs throughout and move the two proof links to inline text links, or make every feature an equal-height card with its action pinned to the card bottom. Separately, remove the outer grey frame from the At the till figures and give all figures one width.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): A sceptic advising a client cannot find out who is behind VendurePOS, how to get support or report bugs, or when the gaps it admits (refunds at the till, the ReadOrder role bug present since 0.2.0, coupons, 1.0) will close. The demo meanwhile shows features the released plugin lacks, and the only ownership clue is an unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com' analytics host. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' and footer; changelog/desktop/01 (last plugin release 2026-10-06); connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go']_ Fix: Add a short 'Project status' block on home and the Overview: who maintains it (and its link to WCPOS/TallyUI), where to get help (GitHub issues link), and the next plugin release with what it brings (refund at the till, ReadOrder in the till role), with a target date.
