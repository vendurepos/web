## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero explains Vendure only as 'the open-source headless commerce framework' and never says who the product is for: nothing tells a visitor that the shop must already run on Vendure and needs a developer to install a server plugin. The line 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first' only hints at it. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01, hero paragraph and line under the buttons]_
- J2 (8): The hero is clear ("Point of sale for Vendure", "keeps selling when the connection drops"), but it never says what the hosted till costs; the only money signal is "MIT licensed". The first screen also asks for a "keyboard-wedge" scanner, which a shop owner has to look up. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Hero till screenshot is scaled down so far that its UI text is ~9px at 1440 wide; the product reads as a thumbnail rather than a showcase _[home.desktop.01]_
- J5 (7): Hero says 'Point of sale for Vendure' and 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' but never says who it is for or not for, and there is no comparison with Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed or the WooCommerce/Odoo tills; a client cannot tell in five seconds why this over a hosted POS _[home.desktop.01 hero; no comparison or 'not for you if' section anywhere on home]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who searched 'Vendure point of sale' cannot tell from the hero whether this is for them. It assumes they know Vendure ('headless commerce framework'). It never says plainly that it is for shops already running on Vendure, that a developer installs a server plugin, and that cashiers then use a browser till. The jargon-heavy 'Before you start' list (Node ranges, Postgres, RxDB Premium token) is the only place the home page answers that question. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero; home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_ Fix: Add one plain sentence under the h1 such as 'For shops already selling on Vendure: your developer adds a plugin to the store, then staff sell from any browser.' Swap 'headless commerce framework' for something like 'the open-source online-shop platform'.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The home offline card promises "25 sales, 20 of them offline" "With the till's tab left open" but does not say the risk a shop owner most needs to know: a reload while offline cannot load the till, and clearing the browser loses unsent sales. That is buried in At the till and Limitations. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone offline card]_ Fix: Add one plain sentence to the home offline card: "Don't reload the page or clear the browser while sales are waiting to send — unsent sales would be lost." Link it to At the till#if-the-internet-drops.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels cannot determine the supported store-wide tax strategy from the setup instructions. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Document the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and how to verify its rounding, or explicitly state that this combination is unsupported.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): At-the-till screenshots are framed inconsistently: payment screen at ~836px full width, cashier sheet ~418px and closure ~545px, the last two double-framed (outer bordered box + inner card, ~50px dead padding), so the page looks assembled from three different systems _[at-the-till.desktop.02-04]_ Fix: Render all three till screenshots in one figure component: a single bordered frame, one consistent max width (or a consistent device-width per screen type), no outer box, caption directly beneath
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site leads with an 'open-source till' and 'MIT licensed', yet self-hosting needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', a commercial licence the site never calls paid or prices; in practice a client depends on app.vendurepos.com, and no page says who operates it, on what terms, or what happens if it goes away. A sceptic advising a client reads this as lock-in dressed as open source. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; limitations 'Setting up the store'; connect-a-till 'Where requests go']_ Fix: Add one plain line on home and in Limitations: 'Building the till yourself needs an RxDB Premium licence, a paid third-party licence (link, price). Or use the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com, run by <operator>, free during pre-release; if it shuts, <what happens to your data and how to self-host>.'
