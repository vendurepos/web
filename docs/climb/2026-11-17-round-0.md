# vendurepos.com climb: round 0 (baseline), 2026-11-17

Live site judged: https://vendurepos.com at `a1984d1` (main), captured 2026-11-17 13:40 UTC.

## Method

- **Judges**: five personas, each a separate session that sees only this round's capture (screenshots,
  page text, endpoint text, `tech.json` with Lighthouse) and the fixed rubric, never the source, other
  judges or earlier rounds. J1, J2, J4, J5: Claude Opus 5.5 (high effort) on the pool. J3: Codex GPT-6
  Astra, read-only.
  - J1 first-time visitor · J2 working shop owner · J3 developer evaluating it · J4 designer ·
    J5 competitor-aware sceptic.
- **Criteria (1-10)**: C1 first impression and clarity · C2 credibility and honesty · C3 path to a working
  till · C4 docs quality · C5 visual design (desktop) · C6 phone layout · C7 machine and technical surface.
  Anchors: 10 could not reasonably be better, 8 good with one or two small defects, 6 works with several
  visible defects, 4 a defect that makes the persona leave or distrust it, 2 broken.
- **Round mean** = mean over judges of each judge's mean over C1-C7.
- **Loop**: the three lowest-scoring in-scope defects become one small spec each; Codex implements; the
  worker reviews the full diff and reruns the acceptance commands; merge on green; deploy; re-judge. A
  round whose changes lower the mean has the responsible change reverted.
- The site is English only, so the nl/de squeeze check does not apply; phone width (390 px) does.

## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 7.2)
- J1 (7): The home page is all text: there is no picture of the till anywhere, and it never says what Vendure is (the hero says 'signs in to your own Vendure store' and the word Vendure has no link or gloss). So a visitor from search can't see the product or place it without leaving for the demo. The four equal buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub') add to that, and 'Try with your store' goes straight to app.vendurepos.com with no hint that the plugin has to be installed first. _[home.desktop.01 hero and button row; home.phone.01; tech.json home.desktop links ('Try with your store' -> https://app.vendurepos.com)]_
- J2 (7): The home page never shows the till itself: the hero is a headline, a paragraph and four equal-weight buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub), then ten text cards, so a shop owner cannot see in five seconds what the staff will be looking at; the second card also leads with developer words ('idempotency ledger', 'The same sale sent 200 times'). _[home.desktop.01 hero and 'What it does today' grid; home.phone.01]_
- J3 (8): The hero offers 'Try with your store' before explaining the required server plugin and Postgres prerequisites, which appear below the feature grid and comparison table. _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.desktop.02.jpg]_
- J4 (7): The home page never shows the product: no screenshot of the till anywhere, so the hero is a headline, a paragraph and four equal-weight buttons, followed by ten identical grey text cards. On phone the four buttons wrap 2+2 with unequal widths ('Live demo' | 'Quick start' over 'Try with your store' | 'GitHub'). _[home.desktop.01 (hero, 'What it does today' grid); home.phone.01 (button rows)]_
- J5 (7): A point-of-sale home page with not one image of the till: the hero is text plus four equal-weight buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub), and the ten feature cards are text only, so you cannot judge what the product looks like next to Shopify POS or Square without clicking away. 'Try with your store' also doesn't say it opens a hosted app at app.vendurepos.com. _[home.desktop.01 (hero and 'What it does today' grid); home.phone.01]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 7.4)
- J1 (8): The headline proof, 'Our acceptance run makes 25 sales, 20 of them offline, and checks that Vendure holds exactly 25 orders', and 'The same sale sent 200 times makes one order' link to no test, CI run or source. They read as assertions you have to take on trust. Otherwise the limits are stated plainly (the Limitations page, and the demo-vs-store table saying 'No: ... nothing leaves the page'). _[home.desktop.01, cards 'Keeps selling offline' and 'Every sale lands once']_
- J2 (7): The biggest gaps for a shop ('Refunds: not yet.', 'Coupon codes: not yet', 'The till does not talk to the terminal') appear only on the Limitations page; the home page's 'What it does today' and 'Before you start' never mention refunds, so an owner reading the home page alone would assume returns work. Troubleshooting also says 'Run plugin 0.1.0 or later' while every install line pins 0.3.0 and device keys need 0.2.0. _[home.desktop.01-02 vs limitations.desktop.01; troubleshooting.desktop.01 'Act on a header notice']_
- J3 (8): The specific claims about 25 sales and 200 resends have no direct link to the acceptance test or its results. _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg, 'Keeps selling offline' and 'Every sale lands once']_
- J4 (8): Internal project vocabulary leaks into user docs, which makes the site look unfinished: the step heading '6. Choose tax rounding for your pricing mode (ADR-048)' points to an ADR the reader cannot open, the changelog is full of '(ADR 0005)', '(#116)', and the plugin is called 'TallyPosPlugin' on a site branded VendurePOS. _[quick-start.desktop.02 (step 6 heading); changelog.desktop.01; quick-start.desktop.01 (step 2 heading)]_
- J5 (6): Quick start step 8 and Connect a till tell you to type 'your administrator email and password' into the hosted app at https://app.vendurepos.com (with https://vendurepos.vercel.app 'serves the same build'), but no page says who runs that host, what it stores or sends, or how to self-host the till instead. On top of that, the hero proof ('Our acceptance run makes 25 sales, 20 of them offline', 'The same sale sent 200 times makes one order') links to no test or CI run. The changelog shows three releases between 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-06 and nothing in the six weeks since, and no page names who maintains the project. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8 and step 9; connect-a-till.phone.01; home.desktop.01 cards 1-2; changelog.desktop.01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor never sees the product. The home page has no screenshot of the till and no one-line explanation of what Vendure is, so the only way to find out what VendurePOS looks like is to leave for the demo. The four equal-weight buttons, one of which ('Try with your store') drops you on a sign-in screen that needs the plugin already installed, leave the next step unclear. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01, hero]_ Fix: Put one screenshot of the till (cart and tender screen) under the hero buttons, and add a short gloss such as 'Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework' linked to vendure.io. Then demote 'Try with your store' to a text link labelled 'needs the plugin', next to Quick start.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): Nowhere on the site, neither the home page nor 'At the till', is there a picture of the till: the cart, the pay screen, the offline/sync notice or the Z report. A shop owner deciding whether two staff can sell with this tomorrow has to open the demo to find out, and has nothing to print or show the staff. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero; at-the-till.desktop.01]_ Fix: Put one screenshot of the till mid-sale (cart and Pay buttons) under the home hero, and add small screenshots to 'At the till' for Open register, Take payment and the Z report.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The quick start reaches a real sale without requiring the email-handler replacement or review of transaction side effects described in Plugin setup. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/plugin-setup.txt, 'Set up email' and 'Check your own code']_ Fix: Before the first-sale step, add an explicit prerequisite linking to both Plugin setup sections. State that stores using the email plugin must replace the confirmation handler and that custom hooks must be reviewed for external side effects before testing sales.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): In the quick start on phone, the copy button sits on top of the code in every code block wider than the screen, the code is cut off with no scroll hint, and the step-6 tax table clips its right column. The page a developer copies from is the hardest one to read on a phone. _[quick-start.phone.02 / .03 / .05]_ Fix: Reserve right padding in code blocks equal to the copy button's width, or move the button into a small header bar, and add a right-edge fade on overflow. On narrow screens, make the 2-column tax table stack as label/value rows.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site asks a Vendure developer to type a store administrator email and password into a hosted third-party app (app.vendurepos.com, also served from vendurepos.vercel.app) but never says who operates it, what it keeps or sends, or how to run the till yourself. I would advise a client not to try it with a production store until that is answered. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8; connect-a-till.phone.01 'Open your till'; home.desktop.01 'Try with your store']_ Fix: Add a short 'Where your data goes' section to the Overview, linked from Quick start step 8. It should say the hosted till is static, who serves it, that credentials and sales go only from the browser to your own Vendure, and give the one command to self-host the till from the repo. In the same section, recommend device keys over admin passwords.
