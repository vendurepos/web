## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.8)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear, but the first feature card a skimmer reads, 'Keeps selling offline', spends most of its words on failure cases ('a reload needs the connection, and clearing that browser's data for the till loses them') before the proof, so the headline benefit gets buried under a caveat the page repeats again under 'Before you start'. _[home/desktop/01 'What it does today' first card; home/phone/02]_
- J2 (9): Hero lede explains Vendure as 'the open-source headless commerce framework', jargon a shop owner does not use and skips past _[home.desktop.01 hero lede]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (9): Feature section mixes treatments: only the first two features ('Keeps selling offline', 'Every sale lands once') are bordered cards, and their unequal text leaves a large empty gap above the 'Read the 200-send test' button, while the other eight features are borderless text blocks _[home.desktop.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero and the ten 'What it does today' cards never say who the till is not for. Its hard limits (desktop Chrome only, no cash drawer, no card-terminal link, Postgres only) only appear two screens down under 'Before you start', so a buyer comparing it with Square or Shopify POS reads the strengths first and the deal-breakers late. _[home.desktop.01 (hero + 'What it does today') vs home.desktop.02 ('Before you start')]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.8)
- J1 (9): The demo row says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' without saying what wcpos.com is, so a visitor who has never heard of VendurePOS sees an unexplained second brand's domain receiving data. The explanation is only on Connect a till. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): Refund row admits 'The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls' but never says who does that or how hard it is for a shop whose store was built by a freelancer _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table, Refund row]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): none found: the comparison table and the '200-send test' proof link read as honest and specific; the og-image crops the product cards' 'In Stock' pills mid-shape at the bottom edge, which is a small polish slip on the shared card _[og-image.png]_
- J5 (7): The footer says 'MIT licensed', but the only way off the hosted app is gated: building the till yourself 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', and the site never says this is a paid commercial licence or what it costs. ## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A first-time visitor skimming for under a minute meets 'What it does today' with its first card spent on data-loss caveats, followed by eight unbroken grey paragraphs. What the till does well is hard to pick out at a glance, even though the hero is clear. _[home/desktop/01 'Keeps selling offline' card and the feature list below it; home/phone/02-03]_ Fix: Cut the 'Keeps selling offline' card to the claim plus the 25-sales proof, with a 'see Limitations' link for the reload and cleared-data caveats (already stated under 'Before you start'). Give the eight short features a scannable treatment, such as one-line summaries or icons.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): The site never tells a shop owner what to run the till on at the counter: 'Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome; tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet' is the only device guidance, so 'can staff sell tomorrow' ends in a hardware guess _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add a short 'Recommended counter setup' box to At the till and to Before you start: a laptop or desktop with Chrome, a USB keyboard-wedge barcode scanner, any printer the browser can print to, the card terminal standalone, and the drawer opened by hand, plus a five-line 'before your first sale' checklist (sign in, opening float, test scan, test sale, print X report)
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing must guess which store-wide tax strategy to use. _[Quick start, step 6, 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:851–880]_ Fix: Add an explicit recommendation or supported-configuration boundary for mixed pricing modes, explaining the rounding consequences. Include a receipt-versus-Vendure tax-total check for each mode.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): On at-the-till the cashier-sheet and closure images are double-framed (outer bordered box with ~50 px empty padding around an inner card) and shown at about half column width, inconsistent with the full-width single-border payment screenshot on the same page _[at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.desktop.04]_ Fix: Drop the outer padded frame and render these crops with the same single border, radius and width rule as the payment screenshot (full column width, or a consistent fixed max-width for narrow UI crops)
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells itself as MIT-licensed open source but never says who runs app.vendurepos.com, the hosted till that receives your Vendure admin login. The only alternative, building it yourself, needs an 'RxDB Premium licence token' whose price and commercial nature are never stated. A sceptic advising a client reads that as a free front door with a proprietary lock on the back one. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store' + footer 'MIT licensed'; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Where your data goes'; text/limitations.txt]_ Fix: In 'Before you start' and Limitations, say in one line that RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence (with a link to its pricing). Add one line naming who operates app.vendurepos.com and the ph.wcpos.com analytics server.
