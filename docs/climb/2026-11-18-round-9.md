- J2 (9): The hero gives four buttons the same weight ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'), and on a phone they wrap into two uneven rows. 'Try with your store' sits beside the others even though the line under it says your store needs the plugin first, so a non-developer can press it and hit a dead end. _[home.phone.01 / home.desktop.01 hero buttons]_

- J4 (8): The hero gives four buttons and three of them ('Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub') are the same outlined style, so 'Try with your store' looks like an equal next step even though the caption under it has to warn that it needs the plugin first. At 390 px the four buttons wrap into an uneven, centred 2+2 block. _[home.desktop.01 hero button row; home.phone.01]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 7.8)
- J1 (7): Step 3 offers three migration routes in one paragraph (an npx tsx generate script plus a run script, 'npx vendure migrate' for @vendure/create projects, and the 'plugin's bundled migrations' from the README / Plugin setup) without saying which one to pick, so the reader has to guess at the riskiest step. _[quick-start.desktop.01 step 3 / quick-start.phone.02-03]_
- J2 (8): The quick start ends with the till signed in as 'your administrator email and password' and never says to make a per-cashier account. A developer who follows the quick start leaves staff on an admin login, or on a role that can't see the order list. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8a; connect-a-till 'Give each cashier their own account']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who arrives from 'Vendure point of sale' and only half knows Vendure gets no one-line explanation of it and no link to it on the home page, then faces four equal hero buttons where 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' look like the same thing. In the first minute the next step is unclear unless you are already a Vendure developer. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero]_ Fix: Add a half-sentence gloss that links Vendure ('Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework') to the lede. Cut the hero to one primary 'Live demo' and one secondary 'Set up your store' (the quick start), and move 'Try with your store' and GitHub to text links.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): The site's headline promise is 'keeps selling when the connection drops', but it never states the risk a shop owner needs to know. Offline sales exist only in that one browser until they send, so clearing site data, a private window or a broken tablet before reconnecting loses them. There is also no plain rule for checking that '0 orders waiting' before the day is closed or the device is swapped. _[home.desktop.01 'Keeps selling offline'; troubleshooting 'Sell while offline'; limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: Add one bullet to Limitations and one line to a 'Selling offline' section on At the till: unsent sales live only in this browser until they send, so don't clear site data or swap devices while the header shows orders waiting, and check that it shows none before you close the register.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Following the quick start's device-key path on the pinned plugin 0.3.0 leaves out the ReadOrder permission needed by the till's order list. _[text/quick-start.txt, Optional: a device key for each till; shots/connect-a-till.desktop.02.jpg]_ Fix: Add the instruction to grant ReadOrder to the generated till role on plugin 0.3.0. Link directly to the local device-key instructions for the complete role and pnpm setup.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On a 390 px screen, Quick start code blocks wrap mid-string ('./vendure-' / 'config'; generateMigration's options spread over three lines), so the page a developer copies from looks broken and is hard to check line by line. _[quick-start.phone.02 and quick-start.phone.03 (steps 2-3 TypeScript blocks)]_ Fix: Render code blocks with white-space: pre and overflow-x: auto (horizontal scroll) instead of soft-wrapping. Give inline <code> white-space: nowrap or break only at '/' and '.'.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells VendurePOS as MIT-licensed open source on the home page and in the docs overview. The catch only appears in Limitations and Connect a till: self-hosting the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence, so without one a store depends on the project's hosted app.vendurepos.com. A sceptic advising a client reads this as the open-source claim being oversold. _[home/desktop/02 footer 'MIT licensed' and docs/desktop/01 'Source on GitHub … MIT licensed' vs limitations/desktop/01 'RxDB Premium licence token']_ Fix: Next to each 'MIT licensed' mention, and in the home 'Before you start' list, add one plain line: 'The hosted till is free to use; building and hosting the till yourself needs a paid RxDB Premium licence.' Link it to Connect a till.
