## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero sentence leans on jargon a half-informed visitor has to decode: 'open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework' ("headless" is unexplained, and 'till' is the only word for what the product is), and the first card under 'What it does today' explains idempotency ('Each sale carries its own id, and the Vendure plugin saves that id with the order in one step') rather than a shop owner's benefit. _[home/desktop/01 (hero paragraph and 'Every sale lands once' card); same on home/phone/01-02]_
- J2 (8): The hero sentence explains the product as an 'open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework', which is developer language that tells a shop owner nothing about selling at the counter. On a phone the header shows only 'Docs' and 'Live demo', so 'Quick start' is hidden behind the chevron. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01 header]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home desktop and phone screenshots]_
- J4 (8): The hero stacks three text blocks around the CTAs: a four-line centred lede, then the buttons, then a second two-line grey caveat ("Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first..."). The buttons sit sandwiched in copy instead of closing the block, and at 390 px that caveat pushes the till screenshot below the fold. _[home.desktop.01 (hero), home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): Hero 'Point of sale for Vendure' with 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' is clear, but home never says who should NOT use it (e.g. stores needing a cash drawer, coupons or non-Chrome tills) and never positions against the obvious alternatives a client would ask about (Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed, or rolling their own on the Admin API); no maintainer or company is named beyond 'Built with TallyUI'. _[home.desktop.01–03 / text/home.txt (hero, feature cards, footer 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.')]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The home comparison table says the demo 'sends ... anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com', a different brand's domain that nowhere on the home page is said to belong to VendurePOS's makers. A first-time visitor sees their data going to an unknown third party; even the linked Connect a till section only says it is 'a self-hosted PostHog analytics server', not who WCPOS is. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): The site is honest throughout. One small mixed signal: 'Before you start' says 'tablets and phones, an iPad included, are not tested yet', while At the till describes 'On a narrow screen' tap flows, which reads as if the untested mode is supported. _[home 'Before you start'; at-the-till text]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): In the 'Live demo or your own store' table the demo cell says it sends "anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com". The wcpos.com domain is never explained on a VendurePOS page. The Refund row's 'Your Vendure store' cell packs a four-line caveat into one cell, so it reads as small print rather than a plain 'No'. _[home.desktop.02 (comparison table), home.phone.04]_
- J5 (7): Last plugin release is 0.3.0 dated 2026-10-06 (0.2.0 the same day), six weeks stale, while a known bug ('in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder, so the till's order history is refused until you add it to the role') is documented as a manual workaround in changelog, quick-start and connect-a-till instead of shipped as a fix; the live demo also shows till refunds and customer notes that 'plugin 0.3.0' stores do not get, so the demo oversells the product. _[changelog.desktop.01 'Not in the release notes…'; quick-start 'Optional: a device key'; at-the-till (demo-only refund/customer note); home demo-vs-store table]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Step 5 of the quick start has a collapsible headed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app'. That domain is never introduced anywhere else, so it reads as a leftover from an old deployment and makes a newcomer wonder whether they are on the right URL. Step 9 also asks the reader to parse 'plugin 0.3.0 advertises order.create 1–5, which includes v4'. _[quick-start/desktop/03 (top, under step 5); quick-start/phone/05 (bottom)]_
- J2 (8): The warning 'Before a sale on a live store' (swap the email plugin, 'Check your own code') first appears at step 8 of 9, not in the prerequisites. Also, the device key role on 0.3.0 lacks ReadOrder and has to be fixed by hand, otherwise 'the till sells but its order history is refused'. _[quick-start.desktop.03 step 8; quick-start step 4 and changelog note]_
- J3 (8): The tax setup recommends different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but leaves mixed-mode stores with only 'must account for the store-wide strategy' and no supported configuration. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_
- J4 (8): Step 8 'Sign in and make a sale' opens with a warning callout that sends the reader to two other pages ("Set up email" and "Check your own code") before they make the first sale. The one linear path breaks at its last step. _[quick-start.desktop.03 / quick-start.phone.07]_
- J5 (8): The plugin's own 'New POS till key' path creates role vendurepos-pos-till without ReadOrder on 0.3.0, so following the docs' optional device-key route yields a till whose order history is refused until the admin hand-edits the role; there is also no simple 'is the plugin installed' check (e.g. hit /tally/v1 info) before the sign-in step, and a collapsed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' leftover adds noise. _[quick-start 'Optional: a device key' and step 4 permissions; quick-start collapsed vercel.app section]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The home page tells a first-time visitor the demo sends usage events to 'ph.wcpos.com' without saying who WCPOS is or how it relates to VendurePOS. On a site whose whole pitch is trust (sales land once, data stays in your browser), an unexplained foreign domain receiving data is what makes a newcomer hesitate before clicking 'Live demo'. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_ Fix: Name the owner in the same cell, e.g. 'anonymous usage events to our own analytics server (ph.wcpos.com, run by WCPOS, the team behind VendurePOS), with no cookie', and add the same clause to Connect a till › Where requests go.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): When orders are refused, a cashier opening Troubleshooting on a phone gets one long block that mixes cashier and store-owner instructions. 'Leave the app open and do not reload it' is buried in a paragraph about error codes and copying the refused line, and the sync-log step is collapsed. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 / troubleshooting.phone.01, 'N till updates need attention']_ Fix: Open the entry with a three-line 'At the counter' callout: keep selling, do not reload or sign out, call the store owner. Move the error codes, the sync-log line and the Admin API steps into a separate 'For the store owner' subsection.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with different pricesIncludeTax settings must guess how to satisfy the conflicting tax-strategy recommendations. _[Quick start, step 6 and 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:840–869]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed inclusive/exclusive channels, or explicitly state that this setup is unsupported. Include the expected verification result for a sale in each pricing mode.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The At the till page shows its three product screenshots at three unrelated widths (full column, ~418 px centred, ~545 px centred), each with its own left-aligned caption. The docs' only visual moments look assembled rather than designed, and the page's left edge stops reading as one column. _[at-the-till.desktop.02, at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.desktop.04]_ Fix: Render every docs screenshot in one figure style: either the full content-column width, or one shared max-width (e.g. 560 px) with the image centred and the caption aligned to the text column. Drop the extra inner frame around the cashier-sheet image.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Plugin's last release is 0.3.0 from 2026-10-06 and its known bug (vendurepos-pos-till role lacks ReadOrder, so the till's order history is refused) is documented as a hand-edit workaround on the changelog, quick-start and connect-a-till pages instead of being fixed — to a sceptic advising a client this reads as stalled maintenance on a pre-release product. _[changelog.desktop.01 'Not in the release notes: in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 this role has no ReadOrder…'; quick-start 'Optional: a device key'; connect-a-till]_ Fix: Ship plugin 0.3.1 adding ReadOrder to the till role and drop the three workaround notes, or state on the changelog when the next plugin release lands and why it has not yet.
