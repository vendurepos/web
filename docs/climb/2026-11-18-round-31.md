## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The hero and the two buttons are clear, but the home page's 'Before you start' list hands a newcomer build-it-yourself jargon ('you need your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)') that means nothing to someone who has not tried the demo yet. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start', 5th bullet; home/phone/05]_
- J2 (7): The home page speaks only to the developer: the nav is 'Docs / Live demo / Quick start' and the hero CTAs are 'Live demo' and 'Quick start'; the only pointer for staff ('For cashiers, At the till covers selling, taking payment and closing the day') is the last paragraph before the footer, below the 'Before you start' list full of Postgres, RXDB_PREMIUM and tokenMethod talk. _[home/desktop/02 (bottom paragraph), home/phone/05; nav in home/desktop/01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero product screenshot is cut through a fourth row of product tiles: a strip of coloured tile tops sits on the bottom edge under Filter Coffee to Leather Keyring, and the cart's 'Guest · 3 items' bar has a stray blue progress line, so the first image looks cropped at random instead of framed. _[home/desktop/01 (screenshot bottom edge, y≈1140); same crop in home.desktop-dark.jpg]_
- J5 (8): The first screen says what it is and gives two clear buttons, but nothing above the fold says who should not use it. The deal-breakers a buyer comparing it with Square or Shopify POS needs (no integrated card terminal, no cash drawer, Postgres only, web till only) first appear in 'Before you start', about two screens down. _[home.desktop.01 hero vs home.desktop.02 'Before you start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' but never says who or what wcpos is, so a visitor who just learned the name VendurePOS sees an unrelated domain collecting data and wonders whose product this really is. _[home/desktop/02 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row; home/phone/04]_
- J2 (9): The live-demo column says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie': a different brand's domain (wcpos) appears with no word on who that is, which makes a non-developer wonder whose product this really is. _[home/desktop/02, 'Live demo or your own store' table, last row]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (8): The home comparison table says the demo sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' and nothing on the home page explains why a VendurePOS demo reports to a wcpos.com host. Connect a till explains it, but the home page reader never gets there. _[home/desktop/02, 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline' row]_
- J5 (7): The site asks you to run your tills on the hosted app at app.vendurepos.com, but gives that app no version, release date or changelog anywhere. The Changelog covers only '@vendurepos/plugin', last released 2026-10-06, six weeks before capture. A buyer can't tell what the hosted till is running, when it changes ('expect changes before 1.0'), or whether the project is still active. The live demo already refunds at the till and takes a customer note, which no released plugin supports, with no version or date for either. _[changelog.desktop.01; home.desktop.02 table row 'Refund a sale at the till'; text/at-the-till.txt 'Order details']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The quick start opens by giving two addresses for the same till ('The hosted POS is live at https://app.vendurepos.com. https://vendurepos.vercel.app serves the same build.'), and step 3 then offers three ways to migrate, so a first-time installer has to stop and work out which one to follow. _[quick-start/desktop/01 intro paragraph and 'Other ways to run the migration' box on quick-start/desktop/02]_
- J2 (8): The role a cashier needs is one long sentence in step 4 with ReadOrder bolted on afterwards ('Also add ReadOrder, or the till sells but its order history is refused'), and the device-key route then says the auto-created vendurepos-pos-till role lacks ReadOrder on 0.3.0, so the developer has to add it by hand: two places to get the same permission wrong, with no single permission checklist. _[quick-start/desktop/02 step 4; quick-start text 'Optional: a device key for each till']_
- J3 (8): The tax setup prescribes different store-wide strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but tells mixed-mode stores only to 'account for the store-wide strategy', leaving the configuration decision unresolved. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_
- J4 (8): 'Check it worked' is the step that proves a working till, but it is five text bullets ('the payment is tally-pos, settled; the fulfilment is delivered…') with no image of the resulting order in the Vendure Dashboard. Everything before it is setup code, so there is no picture of what success looks like. _[quick-start/desktop/04, 'Check it worked']_
- J5 (8): The path is complete and ordered, and 'Check it worked' is good. But the role the plugin's own 'New POS till key' creates is missing ReadOrder in both 0.2.0 and 0.3.0, so every reader has to apply the same manual workaround ('Also add ReadOrder, or the till sells but its order history is refused'), repeated in quick-start step 4, Connect a till (twice) and the Changelog. Nor is there a step that confirms the plugin is live (for example GET /tally/v1/info) before step 8's sign-in. _[quick-start.desktop.02 step 4; text/connect-a-till.txt 'Create and use a device key']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): The home page names 'ph.wcpos.com' as the place the demo sends usage events without ever introducing wcpos, so to a first-time visitor the product seems to report to an unexplained third party. _[home/desktop/02 comparison table, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; home/phone/04]_ Fix: Say who it is in the same cell, for example 'to our own self-hosted analytics server (ph.wcpos.com, run by the WCPOS team behind VendurePOS)', or drop the hostname from the home page and leave it on the linked page.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): A shop owner landing on the home page finds no way in for staff near the top: nav and hero point only at the developer Quick start and the demo, and the cashier guide (At the till, which covers opening, offline, and closing the day with a Z report) is linked only from the last paragraph of the page and the docs sidebar. _[home/desktop/01 nav and hero CTAs; home/desktop/02 last paragraph]_ Fix: Add 'At the till' (or 'For staff') to the top nav and a one-line link under the hero CTAs, e.g. 'Running the counter? See At the till: selling, offline and closing the day.'
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): Refund instructions end with recording settlement through settleRefund without explicitly requiring the separate external-terminal card refund. _[shots/plugin-setup.desktop.03.jpg, 'Settle a Dashboard refund'; text/at-the-till.txt, 'Refund a sale']_ Fix: Before the settlement mutation, instruct the administrator to return card funds through the external terminal or provider and retain its reference. Clarify that recording settlement in Vendure does not itself return those funds.
- **J4 Designer** (C1): The hero screenshot, the single biggest visual on the site, ends mid-way through a fourth row of product tiles. A strip of half tiles sits on its bottom border in light and dark mode, so the first impression of the product is a careless crop. _[home/desktop/01 and home.desktop-dark.jpg, bottom edge of the hero screenshot]_ Fix: Re-capture or crop the hero image to end cleanly after the third tile row (or put a bottom fade over the grid) so the frame closes on whole tiles and the cart's Checkout row.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site asks you to run your tills on the hosted app at app.vendurepos.com, but never says which app version is live, when it last changed, or who maintains it. The only changelog is for the plugin, last released 2026-10-06, and the demo already does things (refund at the till, customer note) that no released plugin supports. A sceptic can't tell whether the project is active or what a client's tills will be running next week. _[changelog.desktop.01; home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store'; home footer]_ Fix: Show the hosted app's version and last-deploy date (in the home badge next to 'plugin 0.3.0' and in the docs), and add an app section to the Changelog that lists the demo-only features with the plugin version they will need.
