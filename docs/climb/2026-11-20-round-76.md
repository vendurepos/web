## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The home page's comparison table and 'Before you start' block carry admin-level detail a newcomer can't parse: the 'Refund a sale at the till' row reads 'The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls', and the setup list names 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12'. The clear hero ends up followed by docs-grade jargon. _[home.desktop.02 ('Live demo or your own store' table, 'Before you start'); home.phone.04]_
- J2 (8): The hero speaks to whoever installs the plugin, not to the owner whose staff will use it. Both buttons are 'Live demo' and 'Quick start', and the line under them reads 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'. The cashier guide, 'At the till', shows up only in the top nav and at the foot of the page, so nothing above the fold answers 'can my staff sell with this tomorrow'. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 (hero)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home desktop, phone and dark screenshots]_
- J4 (9): On phone the nav collapses to 'VendurePOS', search and a chevron, so 'Quick start' and 'At the till' leave the header, and the hero paragraph ('VendurePOS is an open-source till for Vendure, the open-source headless commerce framework...') runs about six lines before the buttons appear. _[home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is and who it is for, but the default path is the hosted till at app.vendurepos.com, and nothing above the fold (or anywhere) says what that costs, who runs it or whether it stays free. A buyer used to Square or Shopify POS pricing pages is left guessing. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.txt 'Live demo / Quick start']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (9): The demo column says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com, with no cookie'. That is an unrelated brand's domain, unexplained on the page, and a newcomer wonders whose product this really is. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04, row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline']_
- J2 (8): The site is honest but leaves the owner with an open-ended dependency. The refund row says 'No, not on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls', and the 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0' badge gives no hint of when the till will refund on its own. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04 (Live demo or your own store table); limitations.desktop.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The 'Every sale lands once' card is stretched to match its taller neighbour, which leaves an empty band above its 'Read the 200-send test' button and makes it look like content is missing. _[home.desktop.01 ('What it does today' bordered cards)]_
- J5 (7): 'MIT licensed' sits in the footer and the hero, yet 'To build and host it yourself ... you need your own RxDB Premium licence token' never says that is a paid third-party licence. No maintainer, company or support channel is named anywhere, and the demo's analytics go to an unexplained 'ph.wcpos.com'. The real choice is a hosted app with no stated operator or terms, or a self-build that has a hidden licence cost. There is also no 'who should not use this / vs Shopify POS, Square' section. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store' last bullet + footer; limitations.txt 'Setting up the store'; connect-a-till.txt 'Where requests go']_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (9): Step 5 ends in a collapsed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' panel, which points to an old domain and reads like leftover migration notes. Separately, the 'Before a sale on a live store' callout sends the reader off to Plugin setup (Set up email, Check your own code) before step 1. _[quick-start.desktop.03 (top), quick-start.phone.06; quick-start.desktop.01 callout]_
- J2 (7): The path to a working till asks the owner to repair the plugin's own role. Connect a till says 'On plugin 0.3.0 that role has no ReadOrder: add it yourself, or the till sells but its order history is refused', and quick start step 6 changes the store-wide tax strategy, which also affects the web shop. Either is easy for a freelancer to miss, and the counter then breaks in a way staff cannot diagnose. _[connect-a-till.desktop.01 (Give each cashier their own account, item 1); quick-start.desktop (step 6, tax strategy table)]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 recommends different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels, but only says a store mixing modes 'must account for the store-wide strategy', leaving its configuration unresolved. _[Quick start, step 6 and 'Why these pairings'; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt]_
- J4 (8): The quick start (about 6,900 px) has no screenshots at all. Step 8, 'Sign in and make a sale', and 'Check it worked' are text only: there is no image of the POS sign-in form or of the synced order in the Vendure Dashboard, the two screens a first-time user most needs to recognise. _[quick-start.desktop.04 / text/quick-start.txt (no [image:] markers)]_
- J5 (8): The role the plugin's own 'New POS till key' button creates is missing ReadOrder in 0.2.0 and 0.3.0, so the till's order history is refused. The site documents this workaround in four places (quick-start step 4 and Optional, connect-a-till twice, changelog 'Not in the release notes') instead of shipping a fix, and the last plugin release was 2026-10-06. The collapsed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' also hints at a domain move that is never explained. _[quick-start.txt 'Optional: a device key for each till'; changelog.desktop.01 0.2.0 last bullet; quick-start.desktop.03 collapsed row]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the home page asks a newcomer to read admin internals ('settles it with two Admin API calls', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'ph.wcpos.com'). The product starts to look unfinished and complicated just as the visitor is deciding whether to try the demo. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04-05 ('Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start')]_ Fix: On the home page, shorten the refund cell to 'Not yet: refund in the Vendure Dashboard (see Limitations)' and move the Node range, RXDB_PREMIUM and the usage-events domain detail into the docs, keeping one plain line per point plus a link.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): On plugin 0.3.0 a real store cannot refund at the till. The home table says 'refund in the Vendure Dashboard. The refund stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls', so every counter refund turns into a call to the freelance developer. The home row never says who does this or where the steps are, even though Plugin setup has a 'Settle a Dashboard refund' section. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04 (Live demo or your own store, Refunds row); limitations.desktop.01; at-the-till refund section]_ Fix: In the home refund row and at the top of At the till's refund section, state the counter routine in one line: card terminal refund, Pay out on the till, manager files the Dashboard refund. Name who settles it and link straight to Plugin setup › Settle a Dashboard refund, so the owner knows today's routine and who to call.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The tax setup leaves developers with both inclusive and exclusive channels to determine how to satisfy two different recommendations using one store-wide strategy. _[Quick start, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Document the supported configuration for mixed pricing modes and a concrete tax-total verification example. If that configuration is unsupported, state the restriction explicitly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): At the till's screenshots use three inconsistent figure treatments: a full-column payment screen, then a centred ~420 px cashier sheet and a ~545 px closure, both double-framed with ~50 px of empty padding above the inner card. _[at-the-till.desktop.03, at-the-till.desktop.04]_ Fix: Give every docs screenshot one figure style: full content-column width (or one shared max width), a single border, and no inner card or top padding.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site sells 'open-source, MIT licensed', but in practice you either use app.vendurepos.com, with no stated operator, price, terms or longevity, or self-build with an 'RxDB Premium licence token' that it never says is a paid commercial licence. No person or company stands behind the project anywhere on the site, so I could not tell a client who they would be depending on. _[home.desktop.02 'Setting up the store' + footer; limitations.txt; connect-a-till.txt 'Where your data goes']_ Fix: Add two plain lines to 'Before you start' and Limitations: who runs app.vendurepos.com and on what terms (free, no SLA, etc.), and that RxDB Premium is a paid licence from a third party with a link to its price. Name the maintainer or company in the footer.
