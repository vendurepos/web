## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.6)
- J1 (8): The first thing above the headline is the badge "Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0", which names a plugin before the page has said there is one or what it plugs into; a newcomer has to read the third paragraph ("Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first") to decode it. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01, pill above the h1]_
- J2 (8): The home page speaks to whoever installs the plugin ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'); the only route for a shop owner or cashier is one sentence near the bottom, 'For cashiers, At the till covers…', and the Overview's next-steps cards (Live demo, Plugin setup, Source on GitHub, Quick start) leave out At the till. _[home.desktop/phone last slices; docs.desktop.01 / text/docs.txt next-steps cards]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero already has a 'Quick start' button, and the line right under it repeats the same destination as an inline 'quick start' link ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first: the quick start sets it up...'). That gives three calls to action in 90 px and weakens the two-button choice. _[home/desktop/01 (y~475) and home/phone/01]_
- J5 (9): The hero says what it is and gives two clear next clicks, but nothing above the fold says who it suits and who it does not: no line such as 'for developer-run Vendure stores; not a Square/Shopify POS replacement with integrated card payments and hardware'. A buyer comparing tills has to scroll to 'Before you start' to work that out. _[home/desktop/01 hero; home/phone/01]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C2): A newcomer is told the till is "open-source" and "MIT licensed", then meets "your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)" in the home page's requirements list. Nothing says what RxDB is, that it is a paid licence, or that it only matters if you self-host the till. It reads like a hidden cost behind the open-source claim. _[home/desktop/02, 'Before you start', fifth bullet]_ Fix: Rewrite the bullet in plain words, e.g. "Using app.vendurepos.com costs nothing extra; building the till yourself needs a paid RxDB Premium licence (the offline database it uses)", and leave the RXDB_PREMIUM variable name to Connect a till.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): When sales stop reaching the store (the 'N till updates need attention' line), the page addressed to the store owner sends them into a DEBUG sync-log hunt for 'Register command result … status=rejected errorCode=…' within 'the last 500 events', with no plain instruction a shop owner can follow between customers. _[troubleshooting.desktop.01 (and its phone slices)]_ Fix: Start the section with three counter-level lines: keep selling, don't reload or clear this browser, call whoever runs your Vendure store. Move the Sync log, errorCode and needs_admin steps into a collapsed 'For your developer' block.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer maintaining channels with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing cannot determine which store-wide order tax strategy to configure from the quick start. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6 and 'Why these pairings'; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg]_ Fix: State the supported configuration for mixed-pricing channels and how to verify totals in each mode. If that configuration is unsupported, say so explicitly.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The home feature section is ten same-size, text-only grey cards. The page's strongest evidence (offline sales reconciled 25/25, the 200-send idempotency test) gets the same weight as minor features. The cards also misalign: in row 1 the test links sit about 20 px apart. The result is a flat, template-looking middle section on an otherwise polished page. _[home/desktop/01 (y~1300-1800), home/desktop/02, home/phone/02-03]_ Fix: Make the two proof-backed claims a wider lead row with the number large ('25 of 25 orders', '200 sends → 1 order') and the test link pinned to the card bottom. Put the other eight in a denser 3- or 4-column grid of short items.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): No maintainer, company, support channel or terms are named anywhere. The default till runs from app.vendurepos.com, operated by an unnamed party ('ph.wcpos.com' analytics hints at one and is never explained), and the self-host escape hatch needs an 'RxDB Premium licence token' without saying it is paid. A sceptic cannot tell a client who stands behind it or what it costs to stop depending on the hosted app. _[home/desktop/02 'Before you start' and demo table row; footer; connect-a-till/desktop/01 'Where requests go']_ Fix: Add one short 'Who runs this' line to the home footer or Overview: who maintains VendurePOS, who runs app.vendurepos.com and ph.wcpos.com, and that the hosted app is free with no account. Then add 'RxDB Premium is a paid commercial licence' wherever RXDB_PREMIUM is mentioned.
