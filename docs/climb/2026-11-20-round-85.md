## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the home page's "Live demo or your own store" table and "Before you start" lists talk to someone already running a Vendure server: Admin API calls to settle refunds, semver Node ranges, an RXDB_PREMIUM licence token, and an unexplained ph.wcpos.com. A first-time visitor deciding whether to try the demo hits a wall of insider detail. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.04-05]_ Fix: On the home page, shorten the Refund cell to "No on plugin 0.3.0: refund in the Vendure Dashboard (see Limitations)". Move the Node range and the RXDB_PREMIUM note to the Quick start and Connect a till pages, and leave a single "Needs Vendure 3.6+ on Postgres" line on the home page.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C3): The home comparison table tells a shop owner that, on their own store, a refund "stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls". To a non-developer that means staff can't refund a customer at the counter, and the plain-words workaround is buried on another page. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone (Live demo or your own store table, Refund row); limitations.phone.01]_ Fix: In that home cell (and the Limitations bullet), first say in plain words what staff do at the counter: refund the order in the Vendure Dashboard, use Pay out for cash handed back, and refund the card on the terminal. Link to At the till › Refund a sale, and leave the Admin API settle step as a note for the developer.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): The tax configuration instructions leave developers with mixed inclusive/exclusive channels to choose between two conflicting recommendations for one store-wide setting. _[Quick start, step 6; shots/quick-start.desktop.03.jpg; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, 'Why these pairings']_ Fix: Add an explicit mixed-pricing case stating the supported configuration or its limitation. Include a concrete check of POS and storefront tax totals for both channel modes.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The home 'What it does today' section mixes two bordered cards with six unbordered items. 'Every sale lands once' has a dead gap above its button, so the section's lead cards look unfinished and the grid has no single rhythm. _[home.desktop.01 ('What it does today')]_ Fix: Give all eight items one treatment: either all cards in an even grid with buttons pinned to the card bottom, or all plain items with the two test links as inline text links. Remove the empty space above 'Read the 200-send test'.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site never says who maintains VendurePOS or who it's not for, never compares itself with established POS options, and calls itself 'open-source … MIT licensed' while self-hosting needs a paid 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' with no cost stated. _[home 'Before you start' and footer; home.desktop.02]_ Fix: Add one line under 'Before you start' saying who builds and supports it and who should not use it yet (e.g. multi-lane retail needing a cash drawer or card terminal), and state the RxDB Premium terms or price next to RXDB_PREMIUM.
