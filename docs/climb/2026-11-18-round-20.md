## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The top nav puts "Try with your store" next to "Live demo" as an equal choice, but it links straight to https://app.vendurepos.com, which only works after a server plugin is installed; the warning appears only in the small grey line under the hero buttons, so a newcomer who clicks the nav link lands on a sign-in form asking for a store URL and admin password. _[home.desktop.01 header nav + hint line under the CTAs; same link in the docs sidebar (quick-start.desktop.01)]_
- J2 (8): The home never says what device or browser the till runs on. 'Before you start' says only 'A web till for now', and the fact that matters for buying hardware ('Its browser tests run in desktop Chrome; other browsers are not tested yet') appears only on Limitations. An owner with a tablet at the counter cannot tell from the home page whether it will work. _[home.desktop.02 / home.phone.03 ('Before you start'); limitations.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero body copy is a centred block of long lines with a parenthetical definition of Vendure inside it; on phone it is seven centred lines at lead size ('...signs in to your own Vendure store (Vendure is an open-source headless commerce framework). It keeps selling...'), which is slow to read before the two buttons, and a second centred hint line ('Your own store needs the plugin... try the till with your store') sits under them. _[home/phone/01 (also home/desktop/01)]_
- J5 (8): The hero says what it is ('Point of sale for Vendure', 'Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0'), but nothing positions it against the options a client would weigh (Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed, a hosted till) or says who should not use it beyond the Limitations page. The only comparison on home is the footer line 'Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.' _[home.desktop.01 hero / home footer (text/home.txt)]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): "Try with your store" is a top-level nav link (header and docs sidebar) that goes straight to the app's sign-in screen. A first-time visitor who doesn't yet have the plugin on a Vendure server hits a dead end asking for a store URL and admin password, and the only warning is a small caption under the hero buttons. _[home.desktop.01 header nav; quick-start.desktop.01 sidebar]_ Fix: Point the nav "Try with your store" link to /docs/quick-start (or a short anchor that says "needs @vendurepos/plugin on your server first" with the app link below), or relabel it "Open the app (plugin required)".
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): At the till's 'If the internet drops' section tells staff they can keep selling offline but never warns them that unsent sales live only in that browser. The warning appears only on Limitations: 'If this site's data is cleared, or the device is lost, while the header still shows N orders waiting to send, those sales are lost.' A cashier who clears browsing data or uses a private window during an outage loses the takings. _[at-the-till.desktop.03 / at-the-till.phone.06-07 ('If the internet drops'); the sentence is on limitations]_ Fix: Add to 'If the internet drops': 'Until the header stops showing N orders waiting to send, those sales exist only on this device. Do not clear browsing data, use a private window or swap devices until they have sent.' Then link to Limitations.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The changelog's unqualified claim that the generated device-key role 'does everything a till does' conflicts with the current setup warning that it lacks ReadOrder and cannot read order history. _[shots/changelog.desktop.01.jpg, 0.2.0 entry; text/connect-a-till.txt]_ Fix: Add a current-version note beside the historical permission claim stating that plugin 0.3.0 requires manually adding ReadOrder for order history, and link to the role setup instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): On phone the quick start is about 10,000 px of mostly prose: the store-wide tax nuances in step 6 and the 'How POS sales take stock.' and server-start paragraphs in step 7 are 8–13 line blocks between the actionable steps, so the commands and checks a developer needs are buried in long scrolls of chip-heavy text. _[quick-start/phone/05–07]_ Fix: Move the explanation paragraphs in steps 6 and 7 ('For inclusive pricing...', 'How POS sales take stock.', 'On server start...') into collapsed callouts, like the existing 'Other ways to run the migration' box, and leave only the action and its snippet in the step.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site never says who builds or maintains VendurePOS or how a store gets support. The newest release is plugin 0.3.0 from 2026-10-06, and a known 0.3.0 bug (the created till role lacks ReadOrder) is documented as a manual step rather than fixed. A competitor-aware client weighing this against Shopify POS or Square reads it as a possibly stalled solo side project and walks away. _[home footer; changelog.desktop.01; quick-start 'Optional: a device key']_ Fix: Add a short 'Who maintains this' line to home's 'Before you start' that names the maintainer, the support channel and the release cadence. Ship 0.3.1 with ReadOrder in the created role, or give the fix a date in the changelog.
