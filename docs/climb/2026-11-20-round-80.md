## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.4)
- J1 (8): The hero is clear and the two buttons are obvious, but below the fold the marketing page turns into admin detail a newcomer can't parse: the comparison table's refund cell says the refund 'stays Pending until an administrator settles it with two Admin API calls', and 'Before you start' lists 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12' and 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)'. _[home/desktop/02 (Live demo or your own store table, Before you start); home/phone/04-05]_
- J2 (8): The hero's two buttons are 'Live demo' and 'Quick start' (a developer install guide); the cashier page 'At the till' is only a small top-nav link on desktop and is missing from the phone header, which shows just 'Docs' and 'Live demo'. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01]_
- J3 (10): none found _[text/home.txt; home desktop and phone screenshots]_
- J4 (9): The line that tells a Vendure developer what they need ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first: the quick start sets it up…') is set in small grey text below the CTAs. It carries less weight than the hero lede does. _[home/desktop/01]_
- J5 (7): Home never says who should NOT use this or how it compares with Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed or Odoo POS; no maintainer or company named. The demo row says it sends 'anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com' and the wcpos domain is never explained. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table; home footer 'MIT licensed. Built with TallyUI. For Medusa, see MedusaPOS.']_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.6)
- J1 (9): The demo row says 'All it sends is anonymous usage events to ph.wcpos.com'. The home page never says who wcpos is, so to a newcomer an unrelated brand's domain receiving data looks odd. The explanation (self-hosted PostHog) is only on Connect a till. _[home/desktop/02, last table row; text/home.txt line 75]_
- J2 (9): The till screenshots mix clock formats in one image ('open since 22:07' next to 'Last synced 10:07 PM'), which makes them look stitched together. _[at-the-till.phone.07 (also at-the-till.desktop.02/03)]_
- J3 (9): The 25-sale and 200-send claims link to test source without a dated passing result tied to the released version. _[text/home.txt, 'Keeps selling offline' and 'Every sale lands once'; tech.json home.desktop.links]_
- J4 (9): In the comparison table, the 'Refund a sale at the till' row reads 'Yes' for the live demo but runs to a four-line 'No, not on plugin 0.3.0…' for your own store. The demo therefore shows a feature real stores lack. The table says so plainly, but the heavy right cell is easy to skim past. _[home/desktop/02]_
- J5 (7): The till is sold as 'MIT licensed', but self-hosting it needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' and no price or explanation is given. The plugin also went quiet after three releases in six days (0.1.0 2026-10-01, 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 both 2026-10-06), with nothing since. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; limitations.desktop.01; changelog.desktop.01]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): Step 5 ends with a collapsed 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app' box. A new user has never heard of that domain, so it reads as a leftover from an older setup in the middle of the main path. _[quick-start/desktop/03 (just above step 6); quick-start/phone/06]_
- J2 (8): On plugin 0.3.0, the role created by 'New POS till key' lacks ReadOrder, so a till set up via the device-key path sells but has its order history refused until someone adds the permission by hand. The site states this honestly, but it is a trap left in the path. _[quick-start 'Optional: a device key' / connect-a-till]_
- J3 (8): Step 6 recommends different store-wide tax strategies for inclusive and exclusive channels but leaves mixed-pricing stores with only 'must account for the store-wide strategy'. _[text/quick-start.txt, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:900–903]_
- J4 (8): Step 4 reads 'For a key per till instead, see Optional: a device key for each till below.' The section name is bold text, not an anchor link, while the page's other cross-references are links. _[quick-start/desktop/02]_
- J5 (8): The 9-step quick start has no screenshots. Step 8a says enter 'your administrator email and password' but never mentions the Email and password / Device key choice on the sign-in screen. A leftover collapsible reads 'If your store already allows vendurepos.vercel.app'. _[quick-start.desktop.03 / quick-start.phone.06; quick-start step 8a]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): After a clear hero, the home page's lower half (comparison table and 'Before you start') is written for a Vendure administrator, not a visitor: 'two Admin API calls', 'Node ^20.19 || >=22.12', 'RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)', 'ph.wcpos.com'. Someone deciding in a minute whether to read on hits jargon instead of a plain 'what you need'. _[home/desktop/02 and home/phone/04-05]_ Fix: On the home page, keep each table cell and 'Before you start' bullet to plain words (e.g. 'Refunds: in the Vendure Dashboard for now', 'A Vendure 3.6+ store on Postgres') and link to the Overview/Limitations pages for the Node range, RXDB_PREMIUM and the settleRefund detail.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C1): A shop owner reading on a phone gets no direct way to the cashier guide: the phone header shows only 'Docs' and 'Live demo', and the hero offers only 'Live demo' and the developer 'Quick start'. 'Can my staff sell with this tomorrow?' is answered on 'At the till', which they have to dig for through the docs menu. _[home.phone.01 (also home.desktop.01 hero)]_ Fix: Add 'At the till' to the phone header, and add a third hero link such as 'Running the counter? At the till' next to Live demo and Quick start.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): A developer with both tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive channels is told to choose conflicting store-wide tax strategies without a supported configuration or verification procedure. _[Quick start, step 6; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt:900–903]_ Fix: Document the supported choice and checks for mixed-pricing channels, or explicitly state that this configuration is not covered by the tested setup.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): The docs screenshots use two different figure treatments. The payment screen fills the column, but the cashier sheet sits as a small card centred in an oversized outer frame, with its caption aligned to the frame rather than the text column. _[at-the-till/desktop/03]_ Fix: Give every screenshot the same full-column figure frame, or drop the outer frame for small ones and let the image sit left-aligned at its natural width. Align every caption to the text column.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The headline is an 'MIT licensed' open-source till, but self-hosting it needs 'your own RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM)' with no price, licence terms or explanation. A sceptic comparing it with Shopify POS or Square reads this as a hidden paid dependency. _[home.desktop.02 'Before you start'; limitations.desktop.01]_ Fix: On home and in Limitations, say in one sentence what RxDB Premium is, what it costs and who sells it, and that the hosted app.vendurepos.com till is the free path that needs no token. Alternatively, qualify 'MIT licensed' wherever self-hosting is mentioned.
