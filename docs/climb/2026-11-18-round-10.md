## Worst defect per judge and criterion

- J1 (7): The hero never says what Vendure is: 'signs in to your own Vendure store' only links the word 'Vendure', so a visitor who half-knows it gets no 'open-source TypeScript commerce platform' line. Below that sit four near-equal buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub), and 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' overlap. _[home/desktop/01 hero; home/phone/01]_
- J2 (8): The hero puts four equal-looking buttons side by side ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). 'Try with your store' opens app.vendurepos.com, which only works once a developer has installed the plugin. Only the small grey line underneath says so, so a shop owner who taps it first gets a sign-in screen they cannot use. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap to two rows)]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero gives four CTAs (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub), and the last three are the same outlined style with equal weight. That splits the next click three ways, and the helper line under them is needed only to warn that 'Try with your store' fails without the plugin. _[home/desktop/01 hero button row]_
- J5 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' overlap, and nothing says who it is not for: there is no line comparing it with the Shopify, Square or Lightspeed kind of hosted till, which is the first question a buyer asks. _[home.desktop.01 hero; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap 2+2 at uneven widths)]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor from a 'Vendure point of sale' search who only half knows Vendure is never told what it is. The hero sentence assumes it, and the next decision is among four similar buttons, two of which ('Quick start', 'Try with your store') lead to the same plugin-first path. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 hero]_ Fix: Add a short clause to the hero sentence, e.g. 'your own Vendure (the open-source TypeScript commerce platform) store'. Then merge 'Try with your store' into 'Quick start' so the hero offers one primary (Live demo) and one secondary (Quick start) action plus GitHub.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C4): The site's main promise to a shop owner is 'It keeps selling when the connection drops'. The docs never say what happens if the till page is reloaded or the computer restarts while the internet is down, or how long a till can stay offline. The only offline section is one sentence in Troubleshooting, and the 'Can't reach the store's settings yet ... takes no sale' line suggests a cold start offline may not sell at all. _[troubleshooting.desktop.02 'Sell while offline'; home.desktop.01 'Keeps selling offline' card]_ Fix: Expand 'Sell while offline' (and link it from the home card) with three plain answers: can a till that is reloaded or reopened offline still sell, how long it can stay offline, and what the staff see when the connection comes back.
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C3): Following the quick start's device-key path leaves out the ReadOrder permission that Connect a till says must be added manually on plugin 0.3.0 for the till's order list. _[shots/quick-start.desktop.04.jpg, Optional: a device key for each till; shots/connect-a-till.desktop.02.jpg]_ Fix: Add the manual ReadOrder step immediately after creating the device key, explicitly naming plugin 0.3.0 and earlier. Link to the on-site Connect a till instructions.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): At 390 px the home hero falls apart into two ragged rows of four equal-weight buttons, sitting above a 1440-wide till screenshot scaled to ~330 px where every product name and price is unreadable. The quick start's code blocks then wrap mid-string ('./vendure-' / 'config'). The phone experience of the two key pages looks unfinished next to the desktop. _[home/phone/01 hero; quick-start/phone/02-03]_ Fix: On phone, stack the hero CTAs full width (primary Live demo, secondary Quick start) and demote 'Try with your store' and GitHub to text links. Swap the hero image for a narrow-viewport till screenshot. Set code blocks to scroll horizontally (white-space: pre, overflow-x: auto) instead of soft-wrapping.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The site's honesty hinges on an undated 'plugin release after 0.3.0' that the demo already shows: refunds, customer notes and the ReadOrder role fix are all deferred to it, yet the last release was 0.3.0 on 2026-10-06. That reads as a stalled project whose demo oversells what a real store gets. _[home.desktop.02 'Live demo or your own store' table and 'Before you start'; at-the-till.txt; limitations.txt; changelog.desktop.01]_ Fix: Name the next release (e.g. 0.4.0) with a target date or a linked milestone everywhere 'the plugin release after 0.3.0' appears, and add an 'Unreleased' section at the top of the changelog listing what it will contain.
