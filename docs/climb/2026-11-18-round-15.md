## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.2)
- J1 (7): Nothing on the home page says what Vendure is: the hero assumes it ('signs in to your own Vendure store') and only links the word to vendure.io, so a visitor who half-knows Vendure has to leave the site to learn whether this is for them; the hero then offers four near-equal buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub), and 'Try with your store' leads straight to the app's sign-in, which fails without the plugin. _[home/desktop/01 hero; home/phone/01; text/home.txt (no definition of Vendure on home or docs overview)]_
- J2 (8): The hero sentence is about Vendure plumbing ('signs in to your own Vendure store', 'lands in Vendure once, as a normal order at the till's price'); nothing in the first screen says it is meant for a counter with a scanner, card terminal and cash drawer, so an owner has to read down to the feature cards to know it fits a physical shop. _[home desktop slice 01 / phone slice 01, hero paragraph]_
- J3 (10): none found _[shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero carries four near-equal buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub) and repeats 'Try with your store' in the top nav, so 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' compete as the second step; on phone the row wraps 2+2 with unequal widths and ragged centred edges. _[home.desktop.01 hero button row; home.phone.01]_
- J5 (8): Home never says who it is for or not for, and never places itself against Shopify POS, Square or Lightspeed. 'Quick start' and 'Try with your store' sit side by side as near-duplicate CTAs with no hint which to pick. _[home.desktop.01 hero CTAs; home.txt]_

### C2 Credibility (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The demo row of the comparison table names 'ph.wcpos.com' as where usage events go without saying what WCPOS is, so a newcomer sees an unrelated brand's domain on a VendurePOS page (the explanation is only on Connect a till); the docs screenshot of the payment screen also shows a test product called 'Tally Fixture Mug', which looks unfinished. _[home/desktop/02 table row 'Sales reach Vendure, once each, even offline'; at-the-till/desktop/02 screenshot (cart line 'Tally Fixture Mug')]_
- J2 (7): The site never says whether a cash drawer opens on a cash sale or which receipt printers work: the only printing words are 'print the receipt from the browser' and 'opens the browser's print dialog', and Limitations, which is honest about the card terminal ('The till does not talk to the terminal'), says nothing about drawers or thermal printers. _[limitations desktop slice 01; home 'Cash, card and receipts' card; at-the-till 'Receipt and next sale']_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; text/limitations.txt; text/connect-a-till.txt]_
- J4 (9): The refunds caveat is stated twice within one screen on home: in the comparison table row 'Refund a sale at the till … Not yet: it needs the plugin release after 0.3.0' and again in the 'Before you start' bullet 'Refunds at the till need the plugin release after 0.3.0, and the live demo has them', which reads as padding rather than an extra fact. _[home.desktop.02 table + Before you start]_
- J5 (7): Home says 'MIT licensed' and 'open-source till' but never mentions that building the till yourself 'needs your own RxDB Premium licence token in RXDB_PREMIUM'. That appears only on Limitations and Connect a till. Refunds and Customer note wait on 'a plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', with no date, and the last release was 2026-10-06 (three releases in five days, then six weeks of silence). No maintainer or organisation is named on home. _[home.desktop.02 footer / home.txt vs limitations.txt, connect-a-till.txt, at-the-till.txt, changelog.txt]_

### C3 Path to a till (mean 8.0)
- J1 (8): The quick start has no screenshots at all: step 8 lists nine sign-in and sale actions (a–i) and 'Check it worked' lists five Dashboard checks, but there is no picture of the till's sign-in screen or of a correctly synced order, so a first-timer can't tell what success looks like. _[quick-start/desktop/03 step 8 and 'Check it worked'; text/quick-start.txt has no [image:] marker]_
- J2 (8): Quick start step 8 signs the till in with 'your administrator email and password'. The safer setup (one cashier account per person with the vendurepos-pos-till role, and ReadOrder added by hand until the next plugin release) is only on Connect a till, so a developer following the quick start step by step leaves staff on an admin login. _[quick-start desktop slice 03, step 8a; connect-a-till 'Give each cashier their own account']_
- J3 (8): The quick start's device-key instructions omit the manual ReadOrder permission required on the pinned 0.3.0 release and send readers to an external README instead of the local instructions that explain it. _[text/quick-start.txt, 'Optional: a device key for each till'; text/connect-a-till.txt, 'Create and use a device key']_
- J4 (8): Step '8. Sign in and make a sale' is a nine-item lettered list (a–i) that mixes sign-in, scanner-field setup, opening the register, the sale, verification and an offline test, and item h duplicates the 'Check it worked' list right after it, so the actual first-sale path is buried. _[quick-start.desktop.03, quick-start.phone.07-08]_
- J5 (8): Step 8 has you type the Vendure admin email and password into the hosted app.vendurepos.com. The reassurance about where that data goes is only on Connect a till, and there is no sign-in screenshot. On a real store, refunds at the till are 'Not yet', so the path stops short of a full till. _[quick-start.txt step 8; home.txt demo-vs-store table]_

Partway down, 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way' turns into raw GraphQL (tallyNeedsAdminCommands, tallyResolveNeedsAdmin) with SuperAdmin access, mixing developer repair steps into staff help without a 'for your developer' label. _[troubleshooting desktop slice 02 / text 'Resolve a sale the store kept part-way']_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who arrives from a 'Vendure point of sale' search and only half-knows Vendure is never told what Vendure is, and has to pick from four similar-looking hero buttons; one of them, 'Try with your store', is a dead end unless the plugin is already on their server. _[home/desktop/01 and home/phone/01 hero]_ Fix: Add one clause to the hero line, e.g. 'Vendure, the open-source TypeScript commerce platform', and cut the hero to two buttons (Live demo, Quick start), moving 'Try with your store' and GitHub into the nav.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): A shop owner deciding whether staff can sell tomorrow cannot find out from the site whether the cash drawer opens or whether a normal receipt printer works. Printing is only 'through the browser' and Limitations is silent on both, while it is clear about the card terminal. _[limitations desktop slice 01 / phone slice 01; home 'Cash, card and receipts' card]_ Fix: Add one Limitations line, and one sentence in At the till under 'Receipt and next sale', saying what printing and the cash drawer actually do today (for example: 'Receipts print through the browser's print dialog to any printer the computer can use; the till does not open a cash drawer by itself').
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): A developer facing a rejected register update learns that subsequent updates are blocked but gets no documented next step after collecting the sync log. _[text/troubleshooting.txt, 'Till updates line'; shots/troubleshooting.desktop.01.jpg]_ Fix: Add the supported recovery procedure or link to an explicit escalation route, stating what diagnostic information to provide. If recovery requires maintainer intervention, say so.
- **J4 Designer** (C5): On desktop the At the till payment screenshot is a full-window app capture scaled down to the docs column. The keypad, quick-amount chips and cart totals the paragraph describes render at about 7-8 px and can't be read, and about half the frame is empty white cart space. It is the docs' main 'what the till looks like' image and it looks careless next to the crisp hero. _[at-the-till.desktop.02 (payment image); at-the-till.desktop.03 (cashier sheet image)]_ Fix: Recapture both images at a ~1000 px wide viewport, or crop to the payment pane plus totals and to the cashier sheet alone, so they render near 1:1 in the 836 px column, and add a one-line caption like the home hero has.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): Home sells an 'MIT licensed', 'open-source till' but hides that self-building the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence token (RXDB_PREMIUM). That is stated only on Limitations and Connect a till. Refunds on a real store wait on an undated 'plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', six weeks after the last release (2026-10-06). A client cannot tell what is free or when the gaps close. _[home.desktop.02 footer / home.txt vs limitations.txt, connect-a-till.txt, changelog.txt]_ Fix: Add one line on home under 'Before you start', e.g. 'Self-building the till needs an RxDB Premium licence; the hosted app.vendurepos.com does not.' Give the next plugin release a target date or link its milestone wherever 'after 0.3.0' appears.
