## Worst defect per judge and criterion

### C1 First impression (mean 8.2)
- J1 (8): The hero has four near-equal buttons (Live demo, Quick start, Try with your store, GitHub). 'Try with your store' opens the app's sign-in, which cannot work until the plugin is installed, and the line underneath ('Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first') has to warn you off it. A newcomer gets three competing next steps instead of one. _[home.desktop.01 hero buttons; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap into two uneven rows)]_
- J2 (8): The hero 'Point of sale for Vendure' is clear and honest ('Pre-release · plugin 0.3.0', 'Your own store needs the plugin on your Vendure server first'). But nothing tells a shop owner what they do on day one versus what their freelancer does. 'Try with your store' sits as an equal-weight button beside 'Quick start', with no hint that it fails until the plugin is installed. _[home.desktop.01 / home.phone.01 hero CTAs]_
- J3 (9): none found _[text/home.txt; shots/home.desktop.01.jpg; shots/home.phone.01.jpg]_
- J4 (8): The hero has four buttons of nearly equal weight ('Live demo' filled, then 'Quick start', 'Try with your store' and 'GitHub' all in the same outline style), and the header repeats 'Live demo' and 'Try with your store'. 'Try with your store' sits beside 'Quick start' even though the caption under them says your store needs the plugin first, so the second step is a three-way tie. On phone the row breaks into a ragged 2+2 with mismatched widths. _[home.desktop.01 hero button row; home.phone.01 (buttons wrap 2+2)]_
- J5 (8): The hero gives four equal-weight buttons ('Live demo', 'Quick start', 'Try with your store', 'GitHub'). 'Try with your store' sits beside 'Quick start' but cannot work until the quick start is done, and nowhere says who it is NOT for (e.g. a shop that wants an integrated card terminal or a native app should look elsewhere). _[home/desktop/01 hero buttons]_

## Overall worst defect per judge

- **J1 First-time visitor** (C1): A visitor who has never heard of VendurePOS meets four equally weighted hero buttons. One of them, 'Try with your store', leads to a sign-in that cannot work without first installing a server plugin, so the clear first step (the demo) competes with a dead end and a caveat line. _[home.desktop.01 and home.phone.01 hero button row]_ Fix: Keep two hero buttons, Live demo (primary) and Quick start, and drop 'Try with your store' from the hero (it is already in the top nav). The caveat line under the buttons can then go.
- **J2 Working shop owner** (C2): Refund at the till is promised for 'the plugin release after 0.3.0, which is not out yet', with no version number, date or tracking link, repeated on several pages. A shop owner deciding whether staff can sell tomorrow cannot plan around a refund gap of unknown length. _[home.desktop.02 table row 'Refund a sale at the till'; at-the-till 'Refund a sale' and 'Order details'; limitations; connect-a-till]_ Fix: Name the release (for example '0.4.0') with an expected month or a link to its milestone. Say it once on Limitations with that date, and link to it from the home table and At the till. Add one line on what to do meanwhile (refund in the Vendure dashboard and the card terminal).
- **J3 Developer evaluating it** (C4): The partial-sale recovery instructions leave the developer to decide whether a needs_admin sale should be marked applied or rejected without explaining the required evidence or consequences. _[shots/troubleshooting.desktop.02.jpg, Resolve a sale the store kept part-way; text/endpoint_llms_full_txt.txt, corresponding section]_ Fix: Add a runnable listing query and a short decision procedure identifying the order, payment and stock evidence to inspect before each resolution. State what each resolution changes and how to verify recovery before retrying.
- **J4 Designer** (C6): In the quick start's step 6 on a 390 px phone, the tax-strategy table becomes unlabeled stacked blocks, with its header row shown as a fake first row. That is the step where a developer picks a store-wide setting, and they cannot see which class goes with which pricing mode without decoding it. _[quick-start.phone.05]_ Fix: Give this table the same narrow-screen treatment as the home comparison table: hide the header row under the breakpoint and put a bold label ('orderTaxCalculationStrategy:') before each value. Or, since it is only two short columns, keep it as a horizontally scrollable 2-column table.
- **J5 Competitor-aware sceptic** (C2): The home page and Overview headline the project as 'MIT licensed' open source. They hide, until the Limitations page, that self-building the till needs a paid proprietary RxDB Premium licence. A client who wants to fork or self-host is dependent on the hosted app.vendurepos.com in practice, which is the classic open-source oversell I would warn a client about. _[home/desktop/02 footer; docs/desktop/01 'Source on GitHub' card; limitations/desktop/01]_ Fix: Next to 'MIT licensed' on the home footer and the Overview 'Source on GitHub' card, add one clause: 'self-building the till needs a paid RxDB Premium licence; the hosted app at app.vendurepos.com does not', linked to Limitations.
